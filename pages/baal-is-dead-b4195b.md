## 3. Key strands of evidence presented

**The Dan / mariner “exodus”**

- The *Danavas*, “children of Danu,” came into Egypt from west of India under “Beli” (an eastern form of Baal).
- Irish records split this people into the **Milesians** and the **Tuatha Dé Danann**; Homer’s *Iliad* has a mariner ally called the **Dan** at Troy.
- He argues the biblical Exodus is a fictional overlay on a **maritime exodus** from Egypt, and that the tribe of Dan was inserted for prestige and later removed from the Israelite roster (citing the song in **Judges** — “why does Dan remain in ships?”). Thebes in Boeotia is named after Egyptian Thebes via Cadmus.

**El Shaddai from desert demons**

- “Shaddai” derives from *Shadu* / *shedim* — Assyrian desert demons, sometimes fiery — hence the burning bush and “El Shaddai” as “god-demon.”

**Herodotus on Dionysus = Osiris**

- Dionysus was Osiris and “almost the only deity worshipped at Maro” (Mer = ancient word for pyramid), resembling the Indian **Mount Meru**.
- He rejects the North Pole/Mount Meru theory as 400-year-old map invention, holding that Giza was the ancient “middle of the earth,” with a pre-cataclysm global grid now 30° out of alignment.
- The Nile was named for him: *Nus* / *Nahusha* → Dionysus, “the god Nus”; also called Sirius, and “Nilus” was the name of the voyaging **Heracles Nius**.
- The club/poplar of Hercules, the staff with wings and serpent (caduceus) — the same symbol he later ties to Moses’ rod and to Baal.

**Ancient stature and lunar chronology**

- He argues Genesis lifespans are **lunar reckonings**, reducing the patriarchs to normal lifespans, and that nowhere in Mosaic law is the sun used for time — the Jews are a lunar culture.
- Giants were **earth-born**, produced by environmental change (vapour canopy/ice-age cycle), not by angels mating with women; he challenges anyone to find that motif outside a Judeo-Christian context.

**The Olympiad**

- The games became a four-year calendar of control, with the **Pythian** cycle two years off it; he calls the ceremonies “predictive programming” and says he has decoded the 2012 opening/closing ceremonies.

**Rama = Nimrod**

- The *Ramayana*: 24,000 stanzas, Sanskrit, Tamil version widely recognised; Rama is dark-skinned, wielder of **Shiva’s bow**, “lord of the animals,” enemy of giants, tasked with eradicating the **Asura** race (equated with the Titans).
- Two celestial events in his life: the **Phoenix transit of 1963 BC** (when Rama was about 20) and the **Nemesis event of 1915 BC**; Rama slays the sky-demoness **Tataka**, paralleling Marduk slaying **Tiamat**.

**The war of Genesis 14**

- Chedorlaomer (Kudur-Lagamar) of Elam assembles four auxiliary armies (Babylon under **Amraphel** — “he made us fall” — plus Hittite Anatolia and others) and attacks the **Rephaim** (“unresurrected ones”/“undead,” root *rafa*, linked to **Rimmon**), the **Anakim**, **Emim** (“terrors”), **Zuzim** and **Zamzummim**, then the **Horim** and the **Serim/Seirim** of **Mount Seir** (Petra). He says cuneiform **Kudur-Lagamar tablets** also record this campaign.

**Bacchus = Bar-Kush = Sargon**

- Bacchus = *Bar Kush*, “son of Kush” = Nimrod.
- Sargon of Akkad: born on a river, rescued by an *entum* priestess, raised by a gardener, ruled **55 years**, personal name “king of Kush,” cupbearer to the king of Kish, marched to the west; Bacchus likewise: box on a river, rod turned to serpent, passed through a dry seabed, wrote laws on **two tables of stone**, depicted with **horns**, arrested sun and moon, and “light of the sun appeared during the night.”
- He says Sargon I and **Naram-Sin** are the same man (Naram-Sin merely meaning “Naram of the moon,” i.e. a title), and that Amraphel was later renamed **Ammurapi/Hammurabi** by the Amorites of Mari (Mitanni) — the lawgiver.

**Gilgamesh**

- Recommended translations: **N. K. Sandars**, **Samuel Noah Kramer**, and **Maureen Gallery Kovacs** [2][3]; he dismisses Zecharia Sitchin and rates “Alexander Delmare” lower.
- Sumerian king list: Gilgamesh ruled **126 years** in the first dynasty of **Uruk/Erech** — the same 126 years from Nimrod’s first year at Babel to his death in **1772 BC** (Book of Jasher).
- His son **Ur-Lugal** links to the **Lugals** — laws (*lugalum*) were passed against these exceptionally tall men.
- Gilgamesh and Heracles share: a best friend (Enkidu/Iolaus), a goddess (Ishtar/Deianira), semi-divine parentage, descent to the underworld, killing lions, defeating divine bulls, divine garments, a herb of immortality — and **no recorded death**.

**The aegis / goat-skin garment**

- The garment made by God for Adam in Eden, preserved by Enoch, Methuselah, Lamech and Noah, was **stolen off the ark by Kush** and given to Nimrod, making him “Lord of the Animals.” He equates it with the Greek **aegis** and the Golden Fleece.

**Menes = Nar = Anom**

- Citing **Professor L. A. Waddell** (“La Wadell” in the transcript): the Egyptian **Menes/Mina** is the Aryan **Anom**, i.e. the Sumerian king who conquered Egypt; the first five Egyptian dynasties were therefore Sumerian, and the original Egyptians called the founders the “Nearoo” (mariners considered gods).
- Egyptologists agree one of Menes’ titles was **Nar** — and the **Narmer Palette** text reads “king of Uru,” i.e. a Sumerian city.
- The **Book of Jasher**: only four manuscripts ever found (one in Iran); he claims a 43-page forgery was produced and condemned by the Royal Society. In Jasher, **Oirus (Osiris) son of Anom** rules Egypt, and the Sumerian **Rikayon** rises to become the first man called **Pharaoh**.

**Images shown**

- **Naram-Sin stele**: bearded, conical helmet, two horns, bow, standing on defeated enemies, associated with a divine mountain (the law).
- **Narmer Palette**: same posture, bull cult, beheaded enemies, no Egyptian hieroglyphs; he calls it a proto-Egyptian replica of the Naram-Sin stele.
- **Baal** statues: conical hat, beard, a single lock of hair, **two horns**, a rod and a spear.
- **Hercules Melqart** coins: standing on a subdued lion, staff, conical helmet; earliest mention in an early 9th-century BC Aramaic stele from “Bure” (Bureij) in northern Syria, dedicated by Bar-Hadad I of Aram; depicted bearded with conical *polos* and fenestrated axe, features of a storm god.

**Molech and child sacrifice**

- The Molech statue from Giovanni Pastrone’s **Cabiria (1914)**, National Museum of Cinema, Turin: two horns, an all-seeing eye inside a six-pointed star shape.
- He mentions a statue of Molech unveiled at a US state house by “Massachusetts Democrats,” framed as “artistic.”
- Child sacrifice to Molech: superheated bronze hands, with drums, horns and crowd chanting deliberately drowning the infant’s screams (illustrated in the old artwork he displays).

**The star of David = star of Remphan**

- *Umen* (via Harcourt, 1838) held that the star of the god **Remphan** in Stephen’s speech (**Acts 7:43**) was a star on the **forehead of Molech**.
- Occult sources call the hexagram the **talisman of Saturn**, showing a two-horned bull’s head beneath a crescent moon inside the star, surrounded by letters spelling **Rimmon**.
- He shows the hexagram in the **Great Seal of the United States**, on US money, equating it with **Mammon**; a “42” diagram with the **tetramorph** (bull, lion, eagle, man) identifying “the god of this world”; a ~2,000-year-old synagogue; and a 4,000-year-old Sumerian seal bearing the same star.
- Remphan/Rephan = “the royal Pan” — the goat-legged, two-horned satyr/devil; hence “panic,” “pandemic,” Peter Pan, and the Baphomet connection.

**Moses with horns; the brazen serpent**

- Mediaeval and Renaissance church art depicts **Moses with two horns** holding the tablets of the law; he notes an illustration where the horns appear to be covered by “Saturn box” cubes.
- Contradiction claimed: **Deuteronomy 4:23–25** forbids graven images, yet **Numbers 21:5–9** has Yahweh command Moses to make a bronze serpent on a pole.
- **2 Kings 18:4**: Hezekiah broke the brazen serpent **Nehushtan** because Israel burned incense to it — he argues this means a **700-year religion**, not an accident, and that there was no known Mosaic law.
- **Amos 5:26** and **Acts 7:43** call the (only) tabernacle in the Old Testament “the tabernacle of Molech” and the star “of your god Remphan.”
- **Hosea** (given as 2:16): Israel long called Yahweh by the name **Baal**.

**A scriptural “tell” about the god of this world**

- **2 Samuel 24:1**: God’s anger burned and **God incited David** to take the census He had forbidden.
- **1 Chronicles 21:1**: the same story says **Satan** incited David. Two versions of one story, which he presents as identifying the god of this world.

**The black stone and the cube**

- Harcourt on the Arabian reverence for a **black stone** as a miniature emblem of what columns, steeples and pyramids represent.
- The six-pointed star is said to generate a **perfect cube** — the “cube of Saturn” — linked to the black stone, to 666, and to **NASA** imagery of a hexagonal storm at Saturn’s pole, which he calls CGI/artificial.

**The 1687 BC reset**

- The **Ogygian cataclysm of May 1687 BC** (he compares it to the “Avellino event”): worldwide volcanism, red dust/rain, a red celestial dragon, tsunamis, collapse of every megalithic civilisation; ~200 years to recover, after which ancient men became gods. He derides “Younger Dryas” researchers as “morons.”

**Gods who died by a pig**

- Attis (“Addis”) — gored by a boar beneath a pine tree; **Tammuz** — killed by a boar while hunting (women weeping for Tammuz, quoted from the prophets); **Adonis** — killed by a boar in Lebanon near the temple of Astarte; **Osiris** — killed by a deity disguised as a wild boar / black pig; **Krishna** — hunting accident; **Zeus** — killed by a pig (per Robert Graves); **Dionysus** — killed by a boar or torn apart by Titans; **Hyacinthus-type figure (“Uriton”)** — killed by a javelin aimed at a boar.
- His point: **gods do not die by pigs** — they were men.

**The death of Baal**

- Ugaritic/Canaanite epic: *“They came upon Baal fallen on the ground. The prince, Lord of the earth, is perished. The god Baal is dead.”* Baal was killed while hunting in the **Huleh marsh**, southern Lebanon range — the same place and manner as Attis, Adonis and Dionysus.
- **Book of Jasher**: **Esau** ambushes and beheads Nimrod, taking the valuable garment (the aegis) that Kush had bequeathed him; Esau then sells his birthright to Jacob. Arabic tradition agrees Nimrod’s mutilated body was found in the Lebanon/Mount Hermon area.
- **Queen Semiramis** then claimed he was resurrected as her newborn son, founding mother-and-son worship and the claim of supernatural conception.

**Edom, Esau and Rome**

- Babylonians and Assyrians called the **Edomu/Serim** demonic vampires, enemies of humanity, cave-dwellers (troglodytes) of **Seir = Petra**; Esau is described as hairy and takes their caverns.
- British and Welsh folklore of **boars as shapeshifters** and druids calling themselves boars is offered as a parallel to the “boar” that kills Baal.
- Prophecy renames the Jews **Edom**; the curse follows intermarriage during the **Hasmonean** (Edomite) dynasty, with **Herod** ruling for Rome — a “trifecta” of Serim/Edomite blood in Rome (he cites his own video on “repetitive timelines of Rome” and **Zepho**).
- Speculation offered: the pork prohibition and the Gospel “Legion” demons begging to enter **pigs** fit a fear of shapeshifting vampire-kin.
