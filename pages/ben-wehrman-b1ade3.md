## 1. Opening: names, communications, shared ground

- Wehrman insists on the American pronunciation of his surname ("Wehrman", not "Wehrmann"); neither man knows what it means, though Wehrman assumes a German root. Delingpole offers the etymology of *werewolf* as "man-wolf", tracing "wer" to Latin *vir* (man) — "you could be man-man… a great superhero name… He's just a dude."
- Both admit to being poor correspondents. Wehrman: social media has produced "over a dozen" messaging channels and "it's overwhelming"; he assumes non-replies are never malice. Delingpole: "I always assume that somebody's not being an asshole when they don't reply."
- Wehrman signals his position immediately: he once wrote a Substack titled *Why I No Longer Talk to Moonmolds*, i.e. he no longer argues with moon-landing believers.
- Delingpole sets out his own tolerance doctrine: "conspiracy theory 101… everything we've been told is a lie," so he does not judge people for missing particular rabbit holes (chemtrails, adrenochrome/child sacrifice, etc.).
- Both mock the scale of NASA's budget — Wehrman: "NASA takes 70 million of taxes a day" — and joke about founding their own space agency with that money.

## 2. How Wehrman got in: health first

- No single "brick wall" moment; the process was "very gradual". He describes himself as having been "as normie as normies could be" for most of his life.
- **Entry point (early 2020s): seed oils.** He stopped eating them and stopped burning in the sun; as a child he burned easily and was "caked" in sun cream with hat and sunglasses. He now sunbathes for hours without burning.
- Delingpole agrees, adding that sunglasses block the body's melanin-producing processes; he and his son don't use sun cream and his son's burning has decreased.
- Practical caveats from Wehrman: street food in places like Thailand is often cooked in vegetable oil; he cheats occasionally (beer and wings); seed oils "can stay in your body kind of for years"; after a cheat he avoids **sun gazing** for about two days because the eyes are "really sensitive".
- This becomes the template: "As soon as I learned that one thing is a lie, I want to learn more lies."

## 3. Tattoos (the claim Wehrman says he pushes hardest)

- "No one's talking about that… it's just a different version of vaccine": injecting heavy metals that lodge in organs; "half of people have tattoos" so it is socially unpoliced.
- Mechanism as he describes it: macrophages attack the foreign substance permanently; the tattoo fades because macrophages "eat the ink and die"; ink spreads into the bloodstream and reaches lymph nodes and brain.
- He rejects the "organic ink" defence — the body treats any foreign substance as poison. "And it also looks stupid."
- Statistics he cites: **~60% of US women aged 18–30** have tattoos; the one demographic that has fallen is **Gen Z men**, now just under half, because tattoos stopped being edgy.
- Delingpole notes he decided against a tattoo (his brother "Dick made the wrong decision") and compares it to ticking items off magazine lists. The pair joke about the comparison he draws with other life choices.
- Brief, allusive exchange about theories concerning the origins of tattooing; Wehrman: "check their early history… I can't believe they haven't even deleted that from Wikipedia yet."

## 5. Elites, ethnicity and "follow the pattern"

This is the most contentious stretch of the episode and is summarised here only as what was said.

- Wehrman: "It's not all Jews, but it's always a Jew" — a line he presents as the best formulation he has heard; he says any big institution "aimed at poisoning people and reducing fertility" leads back the same way (big pharma executive lists, Moderna/Pfizer), as do pornography, banking, media, music, Hollywood and education.
- Delingpole's anecdote: in a pub after fox hunting, he "red-pilled" a Jewish fellow rider about the "13 Satanic bloodline families" (naming the Collins family — "Jackie… as is Joan" — DuPont and Van Duyne). He recounts the man flinching at the name Jacob Rothschild and argues that at "the Rothschild level, they really don't care whether you're a goi or a Jew… It's not all the world's Jews who are doing the bad shit."
- Wehrman's caveats: he knows and likes individual Jewish people; a family friend who married outside the faith is "not even following the Jewish rules", which he reads as a good sign; he attributes reflexive offence partly to "programming". He adds that different groups have "wired in biological patterns".
- Both agree it is "morally wrong to not say things are true for fear of… somebody who's been programmed to be offended".
- He also claims **education** was effectively authored by "Ghislaine Maxwell's dad's textbook company" — i.e. McGraw Hill — supplying "the fake history".
- Delingpole: "I don't think it's going to be pretty for any of us. I think we're approaching end times probably." Wehrman: "Oh, for sure."

## 6. The "old world": mud floods, Tartaria, star forts, free energy

- Wehrman names **"My Lunch Break"** (YouTube/X/Rumble) as "the best in the game" on this material — "I can't believe he hasn't been banned yet" — because he does building-by-building deep dives and rings up building managers asking for blueprints. Delingpole suggests booking him; Wehrman says he has tried and the man is a solo operator.
- **The core claim:** magnificent masonry buildings worldwide (every US state capitol, cathedrals, rotundas) are sunk one or more storeys into the ground, share a style, and allegedly date to the 1800s — a period Wehrman associates (from schooling and Westerns) with "cowboys and Indians", wooden saloons and no power tools.
- Evidence offered:
  - **Seattle Underground** — a full storey beneath the city, "20, 30, 40" feet down; similar underground levels in Portland, Paris, etc. "Just look up underground with your city."
  - Buildings with windows cut off by soil.
  - **US Capitol excavation** in the early 2000s (for a gift shop extension): "They're literally digging out the building and it just keeps going down… like 70 feet", then boxing it up.
  - **Cathedral of St John the Divine, New York** — he thinks single columns weigh "like 90 tons" and were moved "400 miles on horseback" when there were no roads (he flags that he is bad with numbers).
  - **Idaho State Capitol** — postcards showing the finished, aged building predate its official construction date; construction photographs "are totally photoshopped" with "the sky… always whited out" — allegedly to hide airships.
  - **No surviving blueprints or documentation** for any of these buildings.
- **Star forts:** star-shaped structures worldwide, some "the size of towns", geometrically precise, following "sacred geometry and the Fibonacci sequences". Official narrative: military defence. Wehrman doubts arrows justify the design and links them to an **energy grid**.
- **Free energy via mercury:** European spires with a bulb beneath; mercury inside; "when you apply electricity to mercury, it causes it to spin around" and generate charge; spires draw static from the sky. "These buildings were electrical generators." Much mercury has since been removed.
- **Mud floods:** a soil liquefaction event, roughly **late 1700s to the 1800s**, which sank buildings "straight down" (hence no tilting) — framed as a deliberate way of suppressing their resonant frequency, "like putting a cloth on a tuning fork".
- He is explicit that this is contested even inside the community: "the new regime… did a really good job wiping it all out… So we have to get very creative."

## 8. Giants, DNA, and "why are there races?"

- Wehrman's current personal view (he stresses it has changed repeatedly): humans were once taller, longer-lived, "crystalline-based", with telekinesis, psychic abilities and levitation; giant statues on the buildings are literal; "I think the Giants were real."
- **DNA claim:** "10 of our 12 DNA strands are deactivated" — dismissed by science as "junk DNA", which he reads as deliberate manipulation ("nerf us"), possibly by non-human forces.
- From this he derives his favourite provocation: **why are there different human races at all?** If God is perfect and loving, why create populations with different neurological designs that "clash"? He says Christians answer "diversity" or "iron sharpens iron" and finds neither satisfactory. He criticises race-focused posting that stops at videos of crime statistics: "if that's as far as we're getting in the rabbit hole then we're not getting anywhere."
- He offers **loosh harvesting** as one candidate explanation: "draconians and higher dimensional entities" who built Earth "as a prison farm" and engineered racial division to maximise yield.

## 9. Entities, prayer and deliverance

- Delingpole introduces a friend who claims to see such entities: low-level ones with "suction things", feeding on negative energy, congregating at pub closing time, bookmakers and weddings; **they will not follow people into church**; the worst cases have attachments "as big as a house". The friend calls it "the world's crappest superpower".
- Reported findings: **the Lord's Prayer** "acts like napalm"; other prayers merely make them part — "like walking through the Red Sea".
- Wehrman plays devil's advocate: is the effect the truth of the words, or the believer's conviction? Delingpole: "you don't even have to have a great level of belief backing it. The words are so powerful."
- Delingpole cites **Jerry Marzinsky**, a psychotherapist he interviewed about demonic entities allegedly feeding on paranoid schizophrenic patients in prisons and secure hospitals: the voices are external, not symptoms; **Psalm 23** proved most effective at driving them off; Muslim patients' own scriptural material worked comparably (the *jinn*). Delingpole calls it one of his most popular episodes.
- Delingpole's method: "always testing Christianity against the conspiracy theories and testing the conspiracy theories against Christianity."

## 10. Satan's Little Season, eschatology, and the afterlife

- Wehrman raises **"Satan's little season"**: the millennial reign of Christ has already occurred — the great buildings were built during it, "covered with angels" — and we are now in the brief release of Satan. "It sure does" feel like it, Delingpole agrees, though he says he has drifted in and out of the theory.
- The end-point as described: **New Jerusalem** descends, judgement follows; the resurrected live on this earth, which is "cleaned up" (concrete, wind turbines and solar panels included, per Delingpole's joke).
- Delingpole's cumulative conclusion: "the longer I'm in this game, the more certain I become that the Christian narrative is the correct one", and "space is fake and gay and… the earth is not a spinning globe".
- He offers a moral argument against Gnostic/New Age framings: we are drawn to goodness, truth and beauty, which is either a sadist's trick or a reflection of our maker; and the elites who favour those philosophies (secret societies, child sacrifice) are, for him, a "tell".
- Wehrman's counter-worry: the "we are tiny ants" framing might itself be a psalm designed "to detach us from how powerful we really are".

## 11. Flat Earth

- Framing: Wehrman says flat earth "should be something that we should have a foundation that we can agree on", and that the "it was invented by the CIA to discredit conspiracy theories" line is heard "every single day". Delingpole refuses to judge anyone by their position on it.
- **The numbers he recites:** ~1,000 mph axial spin; 66,600 mph around the sun ("a funny number"); ~500,000 mph through the galaxy; ~2 million mph galaxy through the universe; universe expanding. Yet **Polaris has never moved**, stars show no change in position or brightness across 6,000 years of recorded astronomy, and the moon always presents the same face — which, he says, "is a Flat Earth map".
- **The moon** as timestamp/mirror: landmasses matching a flat-earth map, including a large landmass off the coast of California absent from official maps; candidate lost lands — Lemuria, Atlantis. Source cited: the documentary series ***The Lost History of Flat Earth*** by **Ewaranon**.
- **The sun** as an interdimensional portal projecting higher-dimensional light into a dark third dimension; he links this to near-death accounts of "walking into the light", and to sun gazing at sunrise/sunset ("rainbow vibrating colours").
- **Solar flash:** some expect the sun to shift the Earth to a higher frequency/dimension in ~30 seconds, restoring 5D Earth; the fall to 3D is variously blamed on the destruction of Mount Meru / the tree of life at the North Pole.
- **The ice wall and Antarctica:** the Antarctic Treaty is presented as concealment; Wehrman claims nobody may come within **200 miles** of the continent, that an Australian boat was intercepted by an aircraft carrier and turned back, and that permitted visits are ~$50,000 escorted penguin tours. Beyond the wall: hidden lands, "gates", and alleged guardians incl. Leviathan. Delingpole mentions books by a ship's captain who supposedly passed the wall, and the idea that portals open only to the right songs — Wehrman is openly unsure: "I'm not really confident on that."
- **Flight paths:** he concedes a friend produced a globe-consistent flight-path set; he says he came to the question late, and raises "flux pinning" near the firmament reducing friction.
- **His decisive evidence, in his own order:** measuring the moon's temperature and finding it cold; **long sight lines** — first test was **Mount Rainier visible from Vancouver** ("over 100 miles", he says he is bad with numbers), which on the stated curvature "should be completely hidden by miles".
- **Indoctrination argument:** a child raised without schooling would find a spinning-ball model absurd; globes appear from the crib mobile onward, on classroom desks, in the Universal logo; "dinosaurs and rocket ships" on boys' pyjamas. Delingpole adds the globe in Holbein's portrait of Henry VIII (16th century) — "this has been going on for hundreds of years."
- **Ancient unanimity claim:** all ancient cultures held a flat-earth model; the "ancients were stupid" framing is the taught default. Corollary: dragons, unicorns, mermaids, giants and Cyclopes in art worldwide are memory, not myth — and the fantasy genre is the psyop that quarantines them as fiction. Delingpole: folklore encodes truth ("vampires is really about adrenochrome").
- **Von Braun's gravestone:** Delingpole says it cites **Psalm 19** ("the firmament") — "He knew." He characterises the early space programme as "Luciferian… cultists rather than rocket scientists" and the "final frontier" narrative as a meaning-erasing cosmology.

## 12. Simulation, NPCs and reincarnation

- Wehrman: "nobody fucking knows what actually happens" after death; he keeps multiple models open.
- **Prison planet / Gnostic model:** Earth as a loosh farm; the light at death as a trap that returns you to reincarnation; the correct move is to turn away. Gnostics, he says, regard the welcoming light and deceased relatives as bait.
- **Simulation model:** 3D is not real; most people are **NPCs** without souls; a small number of true souls are "trapped" or have volunteered. His tell for an NPC: "NPCs do not ask questions."
- **His current lean:** souls are powerful outside the game and co-created a hard 3D "Earth level" for soul upgrade; the mind-wipe at birth is part of the sign-up; at death the soul is drawn into the sun ("a spawning system… a checkpoint"). "Only the most powerful souls can come here."
- Delingpole's reconciling image: "You don't go to the gym to have fun and be in love and light. You go there to fucking sweat and grind."
- Delingpole cites **Seth Kikliter**, a former astral projector turned Christian, who holds that NDEs, machine elves and DMT "greys" are demonic deceptions. Delingpole: "who's tricking who here is the question."
- Closing tone: both describe themselves as relaxed about the disagreement ("some people get so angry… 'You're going to hell for talking about this'"); Wehrman: "I love the haters… they keep the algorithm juiced up."
