### 18. How Astrology Works — Mechanism vs Geometry

- Critics' "killer blow": no plausible mechanism has been identified, therefore astrology doesn't work
- Dan's counter: astrology is fundamentally a **science of consciousness** — it describes the "warp and weft" of conscious experience over time
- Science hasn't explained consciousness either; "trying to explain how astrology works without an understanding of consciousness is like trying to explain the tides without knowing what water is"
- The demand for a "mechanism" is materialist language assuming the universe is a great machine
- Dan points to **natural geometry**: the Golden Ratio, Fibonacci sequence, and the **Titius–Bode Law** (a formula predicting planetary spacing that works until Neptune, and predicts a planet where the asteroid belt is)
- Astrology suggests there is natural geometry not just to matter in space but also to **events in time** — the same rhythms embedded in nature via planets are also embedded in conscious experience
- A **Platonist view**: the material cosmos is a projection from higher dimensions; the "mechanism" likely exists in realms inaccessible to materialist inquiry
- Dan's conclusion: we will never figure it out so long as we assume the fundamental substrate is matter rather than consciousness
