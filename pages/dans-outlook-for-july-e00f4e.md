- Dan expects **fast-acting, shocking, potentially violent events**.

### Venus — A Rocky Month for Love

- Venus starts July in **Leo** (not her favorite sign — too self-oriented).
- On **July 6**, she enters **Virgo**, the sign of her fall, staying until August 6 — a period of dissatisfaction, nitpicking, and relational strain.
- **July 11**: Venus conjoins the **South Node** (exact) — a signature of shedding and release; relationships/friendships may fall away.
- **Venus square Uranus** (mid-month) injects demands for authenticity and freedom; solid relationships benefit from doing something genuinely different together.
- **Venus square Mars** (exact July 29, within orb from ~July 21) — desire, instability, and scandalous energy.
- **Retrograde Mercury** (ruler of Venus in Virgo) compounds communication problems all month; Mercury doesn't station direct until **July 24**. Expect tech glitches, delays, and having to redo things.

#### What It Signifies — Digital Technocracy

- Dan reads this as the **emergence of digital technocracy**: AI, digital control grids, CBDCs, stablecoins, and new digitally-managed forms of society.
- Strong **Gemini** flavor (Uranus in Gemini; the current Pluto-Neptune cycle began in Gemini) — Gemini = division and doubling, hence the characteristic **bifurcations** of this era: US vs. China, left vs. right, nationalist vs. globalist, transhumanist technocracy vs. its opponents.

### Jupiter-Pluto Opposition (July 20) — The Financial Flashpoint

- Jupiter-Pluto = **great ambition and big money**. Historical examples:

- Dan expects July headlines about **extreme AI valuations, mega-deals, and tech-billionaire fortunes** — possibly a **market high watermark**.
- A client suggested the possibility of a **"bail-in"** (depositors' funds confiscated in exchange for shares) rather than public bailouts — Dan suspects sooner or later we'll see a financial "rescue" whose solution is the distribution of **digital tokens**.
