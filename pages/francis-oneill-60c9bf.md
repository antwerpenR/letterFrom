## Infiltration of the "Awake" Movement

**This is the interview's central and most striking theme.** Both men express deep dismay at how heavily infiltrated the alternative/truth movement is. Key points:

- **Francis** describes being "painfully aware" of infiltration, noting that even he — with experience from the 9/11 truth movement — has been surprised by how many people he interacted with as genuine turned out to be infiltrators. He says: *"they must have been laughing at me when I was saying these things on podcasts"*.
- **Deception operates through the unexpected.** Francis gives examples of unlikely COVID vaccine promoters:
  - A **page-three girl** (Leilani Downing)
  - A **black Instagram fitness/bodybuilding guru** who posted about getting his COVID shot — someone you'd expect to be in the "pure natural health" camp
  - The general point: *"Deception works by deceit. So if you can recognise it, then it's not going to work."*
- **An unnamed "ex-medic" podcaster** in awake circles who publicly says all vaccines are bad, yet published a lengthy Substack article endorsing vaccination as prophylaxis for meningitis — an article Francis believes was AI-generated. Francis and James see this as a tell: someone who publicly takes the "right" position but then subtly undermines it in writing.

Francis recounts witnessing **Abby Roberts' arrest** at the COVID Inquiry, identifying multiple anomalies:
