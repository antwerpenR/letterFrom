# Internet switching

![Internet switching](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/size/w2000/2026/08/fibrus.jpg)

Here's a comprehensive guide on how to switch to cheaper home internet (broadband) in Northern Ireland, with current best deals.

Since **12 September 2024**, Ofcom's **One Touch Switch (OTS)** process makes switching broadband almost entirely automatic. You no longer need to contact your old provider to cancel — your new provider handles everything [1].

### Step-by-Step Process

| Step | What to do | Who does it | Timescale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Check your current contract status — find out your end date and any early exit fees (check your bill or log into your provider account) | You | — |
| **2** | Compare deals using your postcode on Uswitch, MoneySuperMarket, or GoCompare | You | — |
| **3** | Sign up with your new provider. Give them your current account number and landline number (if you want to keep it) | You | Day 1 |
| **4** | Your new provider notifies your old provider via the industry switching hub | New provider | Automatic |
| **5** | Your old provider sends a **contract summary** listing the disconnection date, any early termination charges, and equipment return costs | Old provider | Within **1 working day** |
| **6** | Review the contract summary. If exit fees are too high, you can cancel within the **14-day cooling-off period** with no penalty | You | 14 days |
| **7** | Switch completes on the agreed date. Your old account closes automatically; service continues throughout | Both providers | ~10–14 days |

[1:1]

### Key Rules and Protections

- **You don't contact your old provider** — the new one triggers the cancellation automatically [2]
- **Same-network switches** (e.g., BT → Sky, both on Openreach) can complete in as little as **1 business day** [3]
- **Cross-network switches** (e.g., Virgin Media → Openreach, or Fibrus → BT) take up to **10 days** as a new line may need activation [3:1]
- **Automatic compensation**: If the switch goes wrong and you lose service, you're entitled to **£9.76 per day** of total loss plus **£29.15** for each missed engineer appointment — paid automatically to your bill within 30 days [1:2]
- **Early termination fees**: If you're still in contract, the fee is typically around **75% of your remaining monthly bills**. The One Touch Switch contract summary tells you the exact amount before you commit [1:3]
- **14-day cooling-off period**: You can cancel the new contract within 14 days for any reason, no penalty [1:4]

### Switching a Bundle (Broadband + Phone + TV)

If you have a bundle, the same One Touch Switch process applies — but be aware that switching broadband may end your TV and/or landline services with the old provider too. The contract summary you receive will spell out exactly what bundled services you'll lose [2:1].

## Broadband in Northern Ireland: What's Different

Northern Ireland has **the best full fibre coverage in the UK** — thanks to major investment from Openreach, Virgin Media, and local provider Fibrus [4]. This means most NI homes have access to genuine full fibre (FTTP) broadband, which is faster and often cheaper than in many parts of mainland UK.

### Networks Available in Northern Ireland

| Network | Type | Providers using it | Coverage in NI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Openreach** (BT infrastructure) | Full fibre (FTTP) + standard fibre (FTTC) | BT, Sky, EE, Plusnet, TalkTalk, Vodafone, NOW | Widely available across NI [4:1] |
| **Virgin Media** | Cable / own fibre network | Virgin Media | Parts of NI (mainly urban areas) [4:2] |
| **Fibrus** | Full fibre (FTTP) — NI's own altnet | Fibrus direct | Many towns and villages across all 6 counties; strong in rural NI [4:3] |

## Your Action Plan: Switching Broadband in NI

1. **Check your current contract** — log into your provider account or check your last bill for your contract end date and any exit fees
2. **Check availability** — enter your postcode on [Uswitch](https://www.uswitch.com/broadband/?ref=rogerprice.me) or [MoneySuperMarket](https://www.moneysupermarket.com/broadband/northern-ireland/?ref=rogerprice.me) to see what's available at your NI address
3. **Pick a deal** — for cheapest, try Rise Fibre or NOW; for best value full fibre, try Fibrus
4. **Sign up with the new provider** — give them your current account number and landline number (if you want to keep it)
5. **Your new provider handles everything** — they contact your old provider via One Touch Switch
6. **Review the contract summary** — you'll receive it within 1 working day, showing any exit fees. If too high, cancel within 14 days for free
7. **Switch completes** — typically 10–14 days later (faster if same network, e.g., BT → Sky both on Openreach)
8. **Return old equipment** — your old provider will tell you how to return your router (usually a prepaid bag)

> **Potential savings**: Uswitch reports the average customer could save around **£329/year** by switching broadband [13:1]. Combined with the mobile savings from earlier (£100–£300/year), you could save **£400–£600+ per year** total.
