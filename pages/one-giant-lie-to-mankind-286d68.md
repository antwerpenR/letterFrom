### 7. The Van Allen Radiation Belts

- Described as the **primary reason** humans could not have travelled to the moon
- Neil cites the head of the Russian space agency stating that passing beyond Earth's geomagnetic field through the Van Allen belts is fatal
- Hamer claims **James Van Allen himself** admitted in the 1960s that it was impossible for a human to pass through them, but "very quickly changed his mind"
- Gamma rays in the belts identified as the most lethal radiation component
- The belts were discovered in the late 1950s
- The lunar lander is described as having aluminium foil/thin metal walls — utterly inadequate for radiation shielding

### 8. The "Moon Rocks"

- **Petrified wood incident**: A "moon rock" given to the mayor of a Dutch town was kept in a glass case for years; when analysed, it turned out to be **petrified wood**
- **Antarctic origin theory**: Von Braun visited Antarctica in 1967, which Hamer describes as the biggest source of lunar meteorites on Earth (lunar fragments from meteorite impacts land across Earth, mostly in Antarctica). Von Braun reportedly collected vast quantities of samples and shipped them back via a Hercules C-130
- Moon rocks could also have been collected by **remote-controlled probes** — not proof of human presence
- A Soviet satellite (Luna programme) had already returned lunar samples robotically (implied, not stated explicitly)

### 9. Capsule Recovery / Rocket Trajectories

- Neil claims rockets do not go straight up but travel in an **arc**, with NASA explaining this via a "gravity well" slingshot
- The **Bermuda Triangle** is claimed to have been emphasised in the 1950s to create an area of minimal air/nautical traffic, providing a discreet landing zone for returning capsules
- Hamer adds that statistical analysis shows the Bermuda Triangle is **no different** from other ocean areas regarding disappearances — it was merely hyped as dangerous
- Neil claims the **Russians found a capsule** floating in the Bay of Biscay after it crossed the Atlantic, secretly returning it to American officials at a port in northern Russia (Murmansk)
- Capsules allegedly dropped from **Hercules C-130 aircraft** at high altitude, pushed out the rear cargo door — a trans-Pacific airline pilot and passengers reportedly witnessed this occurring

### 10. Lost Telemetry Data

- All telemetry data from the Apollo missions was reportedly **lost** - NASA claimed that they "*accidentally overwrote all the tapes*" — described as a key absence of proof

### 11. Photographic / Film Anomalies

- **No stars visible** in any Apollo photographs, despite the moon having no atmosphere. Hamer's explanation: they feared amateur astronomers would analyse the constellations and detect positional discrepancies
- A consulted photographic expert (30 years' experience) is cited as "adamant" that stars **should** appear regardless of camera type
- Shadows and backgrounds described as provably fake (referencing an Italian cinematographer/researcher)
- The moon lander is described as looking like "something I would make in my garage" — Hamer confirms having viewed it at close range at Cape Canaveral and being convinced it could never have gone to the moon
- The lunar rover ("dune buggy") allegedly folded into a suitcase-sized package — described as a "whopper" of a lie
- The ascent camera that **zoomed/panned perfectly** as the lander took off from the moon — questioning who or what operated it
- The lunar ascent vehicle had to rendezvous with the command module orbiting the moon at **4,000 mph**

### 14. Laser Reflectors on the Moon

- Apollo proponents claim astronauts left laser reflectors on the moon, and that bouncing lasers off them proves human visitation
- Neil counters: MIT discovered in **1962** that a laser could be bounced off the **bare lunar surface** with equally accurate results
- A Swiss laser specialist named **Andreas Märki** (consulted for the book) conducted his own experiments, measuring photons returned from the lunar surface versus from the alleged reflector locations — finding **no difference**
- The moon allegedly recedes from Earth by under a centimetre per year (the claimed measurement purpose)

### 15. Telescope Evidence

- Claims that telescopes can now see evidence of Apollo equipment on the moon are described as **false** — images show only "tiny little dots" impossible to identify

### 16. Life Support / Battery Power

- The PLSS (Personal Life Support System) allegedly recycled exhaled air with oxygen supplementation
- Some EVAs (moon walks) lasted **hours**, raising doubts about feasibility
- Battery power required for everything would have been "phenomenal" — there is no mains electricity on the moon

### 17. Gravity Skepticism

- Hamer expresses skepticism about gravity as commonly understood
- Claims **Isaac Newton** was "an alchemist, not a scientist"
- Advocates **relative density** as the simpler explanation (Occam's Razor): apples fall because they are denser than air; they float in water because they are less dense than water
- Newton's *Principia Mathematica* is dismissed because it "began with the word 'if'"
- Neil states gravity "has never actually been proved"

### 18. New Evidence in the Book

Neil highlights two pieces of **new evidence** presented in the book:

1. **Tracking falsification** — addresses the argument that the missions were "tracked all the way to the moon and back"; the book presents new evidence that this tracking could be and was falsified
2. **Earth position anomaly** — in the one photograph showing an astronaut with the earth in the background, the earth's relative position is described as "totally wrong" and "impossible"
