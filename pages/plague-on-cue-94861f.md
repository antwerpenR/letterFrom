## 1. The Bab al-Mandab and the Horn of Africa

Monica opens by arguing the real focus should be the **Bab al-Mandab** strait (between Yemen and the Horn of Africa — Somalia, Djibouti and Eritrea), not just Iran, Hormuz and oil prices.

- She has done news flashes on Somalia, and when Ethiopia's civil war/rebellion reappeared in the news she suspected a Middle East link.
- She believes the **Ethiopian government droned dormant rebels** to incite them to rise again in the north (on the border with Eritrea), which is drawing **Eritrea** in.
- Eritrea won independence from Ethiopia roughly 20–30 years ago and took **all of Ethiopia's Red Sea access** — which she thinks is the crux of the whole Horn of Africa situation.
- Ethiopia is "no friend of the US" but is cooperating with the US in this action.
- **Saudi Arabia** is hitting Yemen to take **Mocha** (a port city).
- Her central claim: **Bab al-Mandab is more important than the Strait of Hormuz**, because it gives access to the **Suez Canal**.
- The hot spots — Somalia, Ethiopia, Eritrea, Yemen — all ring that strait.
- She argues **Iran is a less challenging opponent** because it has a powerful central government, whereas the West finds it far harder to control the factional Horn of Africa (Somalia barely has a central government; Yemen is similar; Ethiopia has problems).
- She would not be surprised if the Iran war was started in the hope that it would spread and become caught up in subduing the Horn of Africa.

## 5. Historical Plague–Financial Reset Parallels

- **1300s:** The two largest European banks — the Florentine **Bardi** and **Peruzzi** — collapsed after too many loans to defaulting kings, triggering a Europe-wide banking crisis and collapsing merchant credit. Then the plague arrived, killing roughly two-thirds of the population, with property swept up; two centuries later came the Dark Ages. This occurred during a **grand solar minimum** (which they say we are entering). Mega-rich banking houses somehow survived 200 years of plague.
- **1600s:** In the **City of London**, the **1665 plague** killed over 50% of its 500,000 citizens; the **1666 Fire of London** killed another few hundred thousand; the population fell to ~50,000. The fire cleared the way for the rebuilding of the City, "rebuilt by a master freemason" using sacred geometry.

**Origins debate:** Some argue the plague was not spread by rats but by **poisoned wells** as a depopulation method. Monica says plagues are real, that some events may be organic and some manufactured, that impoverishment lowers immunity, and that she does not claim to know. She says she believes in **both germ and terrain theory** (terrain more important) but accepts infectious, contagious disease exists — while doubting that most declared "pandemics" are genuine.

**mRNA angle:** They cite a claimed timeline — **Russia 2019** producing a plague vaccine, **2023 Israel** working on an mRNA version — and speculate that a supply-chain-driven antibiotic shortage could be answered with an **mRNA pneumonic plague vaccine**. They also note the narrative that plague is being brought in by **trade/uncontained ships**, giving another reason to collapse the global economy.
