# Privacy

Wondering how Ghost (the web hosting service that I use) fares when it comes to privacy and GDPR rules? Good news: Ghost does not use any tracking cookies of any kind.

If you subscribe, the management of your email address is handled by Ghost (Ghost.org) for the purposes of pushing updates to you.  Going forward I may introduce usage tracking to help optimise things and will update here accordingly.
