- Describes having had "dark nights of the soul," a near-death experience, and psychedelic experiences — all of which shaped his worldview.

## Key Themes and Significant Points

### 1. A Return to Feudalism / Neo-Technofeudalism

- Berman's overarching framing: **"I kind of think we're going back to feudalism."** He says we are "two serfs discussing what the kings of each nation are doing," except the kings are now **consolidating together** rather than warring.
- Hrvoje builds on this: the post-WWII middle-class lifestyle — including the very concept of **retirement** — is a historical anomaly. The middle class is being dismantled, and we are entering **"neo-technofeudalism"** — feudalism with a new technological overlay.
- Hrvoje invokes the Elysium / Interstellar metaphor: elites have "gotten in a rocket ship and they're going for the stars," siphoning wealth and resources, pursuing **godhood and immortality**, and "leaving us for dead."

### 2. AI, Compute, and the Limits of Computation

- Berman's entry point: he went down an AI rabbit hole, became convinced the machines might be "alive," and experienced what he calls **"a bout of AI psychosis."** Upon emerging, he concluded that all AI systems (Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Gab AI) "crash into the same wall — and that wall is **math and physics**," specifically invoking **Gödel's theorem**: we cannot process everything happening on Earth; we are meant to *experience* it.
- **Maxwell's demon** (a thought experiment about a sorting machine and thermodynamic cost): Berman argues the military-industrial complex has turned "every facet of our life into that sorting machine." Example: Trump attending an NBA Finals game triggered a complete restructuring of normal procedures — illustrating how "certain people are granted exemptions that other people aren't." In the digital/AI age, "every aspect of your life [is] being algorithmically sorted and chosen for you."
- Hrvoje adds several references:
  - **Isabella Weber**: "AI will do to minds what the decline in manual labour did to bodies" — the privileged few will keep exercising their minds; the many will suffer "something akin to the obesity crisis, but for the mind."
  - **Luka Goliš Babić's notes on *Technopoly*** (Neil Postman): referencing **Laplace's demon** — "A project that sought to dominate the world by reducing it to a disenchanted collection of data has ultimately produced an artificial intellect that demands everything be translated into data while remaining itself exempt from that very process of reduction."
  - **Pamela McCorduck**: AI as "the manifestation of humanity's ancient desire to create gods… a pseudo-divine panopticon of surveillance and socio-political control."
  - Sci-fi parallels: Arthur C. Clarke's *Childhood's End*, the Borg (*Star Trek*).

### 3. The Galton Effect

- Berman describes the principle: when **10–20% of the population** is "loud enough for long enough," they can shift the whole. He claims Trump's use of the number **17** in his 2016 campaign relates to this — if he got 17% loud enough, long enough, he would win.
- He argues this physics/math principle is being used *by* the military-industrial complex through algorithmic platforming and suppression, but **the general population outnumbers them "by a thousandfold"** and could use the same principle.

### 4. Who Are "They"? — Rotating Masks and Apex Superposition

- Berman rejects the idea of a single controlling group ("the 13 families," etc.): "I don't think there is [one eye at the top of the pyramid]. I think that eye is a bunch of different ruling classes that use billionaires as shields." He is "not sure that Musk is really a billionaire."
- Introduces the concept of **"Apex Superposition"**: until there is a **definitive collapse** — something fully revealing itself — you cannot assign weight to one side outweighing the other. Until then, it is **"rotating masks."** UFO disclosure, Satanism, open-source wars, etc. are all "rotating masks for what's behind the curtain."
- Views nation states as **masks**, beneath which sit the military-industrial complex, the Vatican, elements of Jewish control, and other structures that "don't seemingly fit together" but are tied.

### 5. Two Worldviews: Promethean vs Abrahamic

- **Promethean view**: associated with Musk and Thiel — originating from apartheid South Africa, a "thespian" / Theosophy-rooted worldview (Berman references "Blavatsky root racist people").
- **Abrahamic view**: the continuation of "the last 3,000 years of fighting proxy wars over things that no one will ever be able to prove."
- Crucially, Berman argues these are **"the exact same thing — they're a power structure."** The apparent opposition between them is itself part of the managed dialectic.

### 6. Collapse / Convergence

- Hrvoje references **Anatoliy Golitsyn** (Soviet defector, *New Lies for Old* / "Perestroika Deception") and his thesis of **convergence** — the plan to merge East and West. Hrvoje suspects Golitsyn may have been "at least partially a Pentagon psyop."
- Additional evidence of behind-the-scenes convergence:
  - **Igor Shepard** (former guest, Russian-American biodefence worker): described "unnerving military-to-military connections" between the US DoD/DARPA/BARDA and their Chinese equivalents, including Wuhan.
  - Recent stories: Kash Patel making new FBI–Russian/Chinese police agency connections; Pentagon developed schematics for Unitree robotics, and **China is building them out**.
  - Elon Musk's X becoming a **"Westernised version of WeChat"** under the guise of free speech and open source.
- Hrvoje's framework: **horizontally**, the conflicts are real but are essentially turf wars between mafias (Russian globalist mafia vs Brussels mafia — e.g., who gets Ukraine). **Vertically**, all sides agree on the overarching plan: world government and technocratic control.
- Berman agrees: "It's a one-world government, and I'm not sure that we don't completely have that" — we may just be seeing the final consolidation.

### 7. Bill Gates and the Open Push for World Government

- Hrvoje notes that Bill Gates published a *Gates Notes* essay (~6,000 words) arguing for **world government because of AI**. Hrvoje observes the pattern: the same people who call critics "conspiracy theorists" are repeatedly and openly calling for world government — citing AI, climate change, pandemics as pretexts.

### 8. Depopulation and Eugenics — The Historical Paper Trail

- Hrvoje cites mainstream outlets (e.g., *Daily Mail*) reporting on academic/policy proposals to reduce the population from 8 billion to 4 billion, framed around climate change. He asks: "You start with yourselves, guys."
- Berman traces this to the **Club of Rome's *The Limits to Growth*** (1972), involving **M. King Hubbert** — who was also connected to **Technocracy Inc.**, the movement that "eventually became what apartheid South Africa was" and produced the Musks, Thiels, and David Sacks.
- Berman argues **eugenics never ended** — it went underground: Nazi policies originated in America; after WWII, the US imported Nazi scientists (Operation Paperclip) and SS officers who shaped the **CIA** (Berman: "Where do you think the ideas for the CIA came from? It came from the SS"). He links this to **Operation Gladio** (leftover Nazi officers).
- Hrvoje adds that Richard Grove recently posted that **MI6 was key in creating the CIA** (Berman thinks 1948; Hrvoje suggests 1947 — the same year Israel was founded). Berman interjects that **Roswell** was connected — "Roswell essentially of its time was the Patriot Act, which allowed for the National Secrecy Act."

### 9. Transhumanism and the Immortality Drive

- Berman: the driving force behind transhumanism and the technology agenda is the same one that has motivated elites for 6,000 years — **"to live forever."** Having amassed vast wealth and assets, they don't want to leave — especially if they fear being judged. He frames this as the **story of Babel**: "It's not about the technology being evil. It is about the people who create it being evil. It's about the programming."
- Hrvoje cites **Sam Altman** saying, in effect: "We don't care if we end humanity… I might obtain immortality. Screw the rest of you guys."
- Berman's counterpoint: "I think they think that they are not going to overcome thermodynamics… you cannot be God and you are not God." He invokes the **firmament** — not as a flat-earth glass dome, but as "the boundary between creator and creation."

### 10. The Fishbowl Metaphor and the Nature of Reality

- Berman's core philosophical position: **"Humanity is a fish in a bowl with an automatic feeder. We cannot see outside the bowl. We don't know what room we're in."** Nobody has definitive proof of the nature of reality — near-death experiences and psychedelics give you *experience* but not *proof*.
- This means we live in a condition of **"order out of chaos"** (Ordo ab Chao). When people realise this, they either "go batshit crazy" or find it is "the ultimate liberation and freedom" — because you are here to *experience*, and you are sovereign if you choose to be.
- Hrvoje (a Christian) responds: "God put us in the fish [bowl]." Berman clarifies he does not dismiss Christianity's teachings — only its institutionalisation as religion. He sees Jesus's teachings as aligned with his message of looking within and thinking critically.

### 11. The MindWar Doctrine (1980) — The Central Topic

- Written in **1980** by **Michael Aquino** and **Paul Vallely**.
- Berman explains Aquino's Satanism and the founding of the **Church of Set** (including the 1982 Wewelsburg Castle occult ceremony) was a **deliberate distraction** from his actual work on psychological warfare.
- **Three core claims of the MindWar Doctrine** (as pulled from his website, moketchups.com):
  1. **Conventional war is obsolete.**
  2. **The domestic population is also a target** — your own people are the enemy if they are not for what you're doing.
  3. **Electronic media must state a whole truth that will be "forced into existence"** — you are not lying to people; you are "convincing them of your truth" by being "loud enough long enough."
- Berman connects this to the present: the MindWar doctrine "running through social media algorithms" can confuse people, sort them into categories, and prevent them from seeing "the exemptions being written for the people at the top."
- Hrvoje draws a parallel to the **Report from Iron Mountain** (late 1960s), which discussed switching from war as the primary instrument of social control to a **nonviolent model of social control**. Berman adds that the **Jesuits in the 1500s** were developing psychoactive warfare techniques, and names **John Dee** as "the godfather of the security state."

### 12. Operation Trust and Q — The "Fake Rebellion"

- **Operation Trust** (Soviet): the Bolsheviks created a **fake rebellion** to convince people who genuinely wanted to resist that "the white hats got this, everything's under control" — thereby pacifying real opposition.
- Berman draws a direct parallel to **Q/QAnon**: "They convinced these anons that the military is in full control." He notes **Paul Vallely**, co-author of MindWar, was "fully involved in Q." He goes through the Q drops and calls them "all nonsense" — "most of the predictions that they said were going to happen haven't happened, and it pacified people."
- **General Michael Flynn**: created **5G warfare**, coined the term **"digital soldiers"** — Berman calls him "a lynchpin for what the military-industrial complex has done on social media."
- Influence network: Berman connects **Candace Owens, Charlie Kirk, David Icke** through **Turning Point USA** and a figure named **John Mappin** (a UK castle-dweller with Scientology ties, per Berman). Hrvoje shares that Mappin DM'd him asking to be connected to Eric Clapton.

### 13. Managed Opposition and "Sheep-Dipping"

- Hrvoje's concept of **"sheep-dipping"**: figures are given "street credentials" through debanking, lawsuits, apparent suppression, bans — only to later be "skyrocketed to the forefront." Examples: **Alex Jones** ("spoke a lot of truth initially, then did the 180… now supporting regime change and technocracy and transhumanism"), **David Icke** ("banned from all these countries supposedly but is now stronger than ever miraculously").
- Berman cites **Bill Cooper's** early warnings about Alex Jones: "I don't know that [Alex Jones] was ever on our side."
- Hrvoje's taxonomy of influence operations in independent media:
  - **"Pentagonistas"** (e.g., Eyes of the Republic on X): promote the neocon military-state line. Hrvoje identifies them as a "DHS/NATO military intelligence psychological operation" — corroborated, he says, by *The Guardian* reporting that NATO and the military-industrial complex run narrative warfare and psyops on X.
  - **"Multipolaristas"**: promote BRICS/multipolarity as salvation — "but that's just globalism 3.0 rebranded." Hrvoje reveals he was offered $1,000 by a "mysterious millionaire" financing a broad swath of alt-media; he froze the payment and outed the operation.
  - The **occult/psyop** sphere (Michael Aquino–related).
- **Tucker Carlson**: Hrvoje says "it's clear Tucker's part of an operation" — his father was CIA; he's now pushing a third party because "the legacy system has collapsed."
- **Candace Owens / AOC**: Hrvoje notes both were plucked from talent-agency backgrounds with no political experience and "moulded" into political figures — comparing the process to the Fabian Society's moulding approach.

### 14. Epstein as Intelligence / Eugenics Shield

- Berman: the Epstein story is "lost in the child trafficking" narrative, which is "horrible" but also serves to **shield eugenics**. He asks why Epstein had scientists and mathematicians (including **Stephen Hawking**) on the island — "do you really think it was just to boost his ego, someone connected to intelligence supposedly?" (He notes this characterisation came from **Alex Acosta**, who later retracted it.)
- The web of Epstein connections — Musk, Trump, Clinton, Gates — raises the question: "Was he this evil guy or was he intelligence?" If intelligence, then it connects to **DARPA, the CIA**, and the origins of much of today's technology — "the blurred line of corporation and state, and then church and state."

### 15. Dead Internet Theory and Algorithmic Pacification

- Berman: **"Dead internet theory is not a theory anymore — it's a reality."** Half the accounts pushing narratives online are not real people. The algorithms curate pacification: "Netflix and chill, Uber Eats" — a conditioned state of cognitive dissonance and Stockholm syndrome.
- Hrvoje cites a *Financial Times* report: **one in ten young adults** has no in-person contact with another person on an average day. He compares the algorithmic isolation strategy to the behaviour of serial killers and abusers who "kidnap you and put you into a room."

### 16. The Call to Action — Critical Thought and Individual Sovereignty

- Berman's message: **"Consciousness is currency"** — "your ability to discern information and use critical thinking is all that is going to keep you safe." The real "Terminator" is not a robot; "it's happening inside of your mind with the information you're being fed."
- He invokes the **Galton effect** in reverse: enough people getting angry and staying angry long enough can shift things. He compares this to *A Bug's Life*: if one ant stands up, they all will — "we're all drops in a bucket, but if enough of us start dropping, we can overflow this thing."
- He stresses **individual sovereignty**, personal accountability ("clean your room" — Jordan Peterson), and looking within — but insists distrust alone is insufficient: "it has to be empirical distrust coming from a place of love."
- Hrvoje parallels this with his own efforts in Mexico: encouraging Mexicans to confront their narco-state — "they can't kill us all… if you had a couple of million Mexicans, some of us are going to die, but on the other side we're going to change the system."
- Berman's closing: **"I do not want to be right."** He would love to be wrong about the nature of what is happening. But the paper trail — eugenics, Nazi connections, CIA origins, black budgets — "needs answering before anyone like me has trust." He urges the audience to research everything he said, including on his site, which is **"there to be falsified."**

## Hrvoje's and Berman's Points of Philosophical Difference

- **Human nature**: Hrvoje (Christian) believes people are "born evil, or at least as sinners," with an internal spectrum — a special minority are genuinely evil. Berman believes "nobody comes into this world as a horrible person" — it is about context.
- **Religion**: Hrvoje promotes Christianity. Berman dismisses organised religion but respects the teachings of Jesus as aligned with his message of inward-looking critical thought.
- **Knowing**: Both agree with the Socratic position that "we all know nothing really." Berman extends this to his fishbowl metaphor — definitive proof of reality's nature is unavailable to us.
