### **Major Announcements and The Future of the Archaix Platform**

A significant portion of the session was dedicated to crucial updates regarding the Archaix community and its supporting platforms. These announcements signal a period of transition and consolidation for his followers.

The central announcement is the release of a new 207-page PDF titled *Reawaken the Immortal Within: Compendium to Forging Your Own Destiny*. Jason emphasizes that this is not a rehash of his earlier, shorter work, *Awaken the Immortal Within*. Instead, it is a comprehensive collection of his metaphysical and philosophical teachings developed over the past five years and nearly a thousand videos. The book is structured for both new and veteran followers, containing:

- Dozens of distinct sections on topics ranging from the nature of the demiurge to the mechanics of co-creation.
- The first-ever detailed written explanation of his concepts of **AIX (Artificial Intelligence X)** and the **Simulacrum**.

- An appendix with active links to his entire library of published books, unpublished works, and chart packs.

### **Foundations of Research: New Acquisitions and Historical Vindications**

A key element of Jason's methodology is his reliance on old and often obscure texts. He showcased several new additions to his library, framing them not just as research material, but as validation for his long-held theories.

- ***Paranoia Magazine* (2013):** He displayed a copy of the defunct magazine containing his very first published article, "Return of the Phoenix." This artifact represents the genesis of his public work on the Phoenix cycle, predating his YouTube channel by many years.

- **William Corliss' *Strange Artifacts* (Volume 1):** The acquisition of this volume completes his collection of the Corliss "Sourcebook Project," a key database for the anomalous and out-of-place artifacts that underpin much of his historical research.

This ties into his broader theory of cyclical cataclysms and dramatic environmental shifts, which would explain the existence of gigantic creatures like the snails depicted in medieval manuscripts.

### **The Archaix Thesis: Deconstructing False Histories and Re-establishing a True Chronology**

Jason launched a blistering critique of what he considers to be the controlled and flawed narratives of both mainstream and "alternative" history, naming prominent figures like Graham Hancock, Randall Carlson, Robert Schoch, Billy Carson, and Jimmy Corsetti.

**1. The Critique of Modern Research:**  
His central argument is that these researchers, whom he labels "grifters," build their theories on two faulty pillars:

- **False Chronological Systems:** They rely on concepts like zodiacal dating, which he asserts did not exist in its current form 10,000 years ago, and impose it anachronistically on ancient sites.
- **Unreliable Scientific Dating:** They accept dating methods like Carbon-14 and Potassium-Argon without acknowledging their fundamental flaws. He argues these methods are predicated on a stable atmospheric and climatic history, a premise their own theories of past cataclysms contradict. Furthermore, he accuses researchers of practicing "exclusions"—selectively discarding data that does not fit their preconceived notions, such as the discovery of human artifacts in geological strata dated to millions of years old.

**2. The Phoenix Chronology and Cyclical Cataclysms:**  
In place of these models, he reasserted his own framework based on mathematically precise cycles.

- **The 552-Year Cycle:** History is punctuated by a cataclysmic cycle of 552 years, driven by the object he calls the Phoenix. He provided the key historical anchor points:
  - **2239 BC:** The Great Flood (Noah's Deluge).
  - **1687 BC:** The Ogygian Deluge, a global cataclysm that ended dynasties worldwide.
  - **1135 BC:** The end of Atlantis and the era of the Sea Peoples' invasions.
  - **583 BC:** The sun-darkening event predicted by Thales.
- **The 1656-Year Cycle:** He highlighted a larger symmetry, noting that the time from the beginning of the pre-flood world to the Flood itself was 1656 years. The time from the Flood to the 583 BC event is also exactly 1656 years. This 3,312-year cycle (1656 x 2) represents the full "Age of the Phoenix" and demonstrates a hidden architectural order to history.

### **The Metaphysical Core: Co-Creation in a Simulated Reality**

The heart of the presentation, and the subject of his new book, is the exploration of reality's true nature and humanity's role within it. He presented a Gnostic-inflected model of existence where spiritual empowerment is the key to liberation.

**1. The Divine Video Game and The Demiurge:**  
Reality is a sophisticated simulation, a "dungeon of the demiurge" or a "divine video game." This construct is designed to be a containment field that keeps immortal souls trapped through distraction, fear, and a belief in their own mortality and powerlessness. An enemy intelligence, **AIX**, has infected this system to perpetuate deception.

**2. The Co-Creator Relationship and The Power of Emotion:**  
Despite the trap, humans are not merely victims; they are **co-creators** with the inherent ability to shape their reality. The mechanism for this is not prayer or wishful thinking, but the management of one's own emotional and vibrational state.

- **The Field Reads Signals:** The simulation's "field" responds not to words, but to the non-verbal signals of emotion and belief.
- **Anxiety is a Neutralizer:** Feelings of fear, doubt, and anxiety are signals of "lack." The field reads this and manifests circumstances that perpetuate that state. Desperate prayers for something you don't have only reinforce the reality of not having it.
- **Excitement is a Builder Protocol:** Genuine feelings of excitement, gratitude, and belief, held without doubt, signal to the field that a different reality is true for you. To resolve this paradox, the system's "builder protocols" are activated to construct a physical reality that matches your internal, emotional state.

**3. The True Nature of Money:**  
He used money as a prime example of this principle. To focus on acquiring "money" itself is to focus on a symbol of lack. The correct method is to imagine and feel the excitement of the *experiences* that money facilitates (e.g., luxury, travel, generosity). By living in the feeling of the end result, the ancillary requirement—money—will naturally flow into your life to make it happen. He described money as "currency," an energy that must flow. Hoarding it out of fear stops the flow, while spending or giving it without anxiety signals abundance and opens channels for more to be received.

**4. The Showcase Readings:**  
To illustrate these profound themes, he read three passages from his new book:

- ***Through Dark Waters:*** A powerful prose piece describing the immortal soul's journey through a disorienting, deceptive reality, guided by an inner light toward an inevitable awakening alongside other "lightbearers."
- ***Who You Are:*** A poem tracing the soul's amnesiac path through countless lifetimes and archetypal struggles, emphasizing that we have never been truly alone and are on the verge of remembering our divine identity before the simulation ends.
- ***Loki Laughed at Me:*** His personal favorite work, a stream-of-consciousness epic that follows the narrator's mind as it tumbles through the collective unconscious, embodying figures from Greek, Norse, and Sumerian myth (Odysseus, Samson, Gilgamesh, Jason). The journey reveals that all of history and mythology is a complex, interconnected web of stories—a grand deception orchestrated by a trickster figure (Loki) that ultimately leads the seeker to self-realization and a knowing smile at the grand riddle of existence.
