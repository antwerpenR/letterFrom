- Adams worked in the corporate world in the 1990s–2000s, including a **Microsoft "Life Squared" installation in London (~2005)** — a mini smart-city mock-up (a school converted into a London square) that Bill Gates came to view. She later worked for Google and overheard conversations involving **Eric Schmidt and Jared Cohen** (a former Condoleezza Rice aide), including claims of weekly breakfasts with George Bush. - Around 2008–2010 she discovered **Agenda 21**, read Rosa Koire's *Behind the Green Mask* and James Delingpole's *Watermelons*, then left the corporate world after the London Olympics and relocated to the West Country.
- **COVID was her real awakening**: she saw it as a planned acceleration of the UN/WEF 2030 agenda rather than a genuine health crisis.

## Key Claims and Arguments

**1. Agenda 21 → Agenda 2030 as a technocratic road map**

- Agenda 21 was instigated at the 1992 Rio Earth Summit; 178 delegates were wined and dined (including aboard the Royal Yacht Britannia with Prince Charles) into signing what she says was actually **global governance**, not environmentalism. Attendees/promoters included George Bush, the Rockefellers, Edmund de Rothschild and Al Gore.
- Because the eight Millennium Development Goals stalled, the agenda was accelerated into the **17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**, launched in 2015 with Pope Francis endorsing them in Rome — "church and state coming together". She argues the goals, boiled down, amount to a **technocratic dictatorship**.
- In June 2019, UN Secretary-General **António Guterres and Klaus Schwab** signed a UN–WEF Strategic Partnership Framework to accelerate digital cooperation; four months later came **Event 201** at Johns Hopkins, attended by the Gates Foundation. She considers COVID a pretext for the digitalisation agenda: biometrics in supermarkets, fibre rollout, cash removal, and "the biggest transfer of wealth" from ordinary people to the elite.

**2. Digital identity and the AI agenda**

- Host played a clip of a Nigerian official (credited via Tim Hliff of *Sociable*) describing the "new world order" in which **digital identity determines access to services, the economy and government** — referencing Nigeria's NIMC Act 2026.
- Both discussed the **GovTech board in Berlin and Ukraine**, and Ukraine's rebuilding as a "smart country" with Diia-style apps, arguing that a fully digital system enables "unpersoning" of dissidents and removes accountability ("how can you hold a bot to account?").
- Bill Gates, they note, has written that **global government is needed to manage the AI threat** — world government offered as the solution to every manufactured crisis (climate, health, AI).

**3. The UK: farming, 15-minute cities and regionalisation**

- **Farming**: inheritance tax is forcing farmers off the land; children must go to university to learn to run robotic/vertical farms; farmland is being replaced by wind and solar farms and housing for migrants. She predicts food shortages and frames it as "a deliberate attempt to starve us", with synthetic "Soylent Green"-style proteins waiting in parallel.
- **15-minute cities**: Oxford is now a fully-fledged 15-minute city where residents can only leave their zone roughly 100 days a year; the ring road is congested and polluting — the opposite of the stated environmental aim. Pushback continues in Canterbury and Ireland.
- **IPCC reversal**: she cites the AR6/AR7 reports as admitting the most extreme emissions scenario is no longer considered plausible — "they've gone back on net zero". Even Tony Blair said at COP (Belen, Brazil, 2025) that net zero "isn't working", pivoting to AI.
- **Local government reorganisation**: via the Devolution White Paper and the Levelling-up/communities legislation, counties are being merged into regions governed by powerful mayors tied to the **Global Covenant of Mayors for Climate and Energy** (e.g. Andy Burnham, Sadiq Khan), with parliament to be dissolved — "no democracy left once it's done".
- **Mass migration** framed as "replacement migration", contrasting today's arrivals with earlier working immigrant generations like the Windrush generation. She believes the UK and Ireland are being deliberately targeted to "break the nation".
- Host cited Richard Wellings (X commentary): anti-car policies slowing buses, free-speech restrictions and a coming "Ministry of Truth" to persecute domestic dissidents, and Brexit sabotage aimed at pushing the UK back under Brussels control (citing Tony Blair's call for rejoining the EU).

**4. Mexico (host's observations)**

- Mexico is reportedly among the first countries where **petrol can no longer be bought with cash** — payment must be by card or bank-app QR code, forcing smartphone use.
- Guadalajara, a "Rockefeller-financed resilient city" (per documents he holds), is rolling out 15-minute-city plans, EV incentives and toll barriers on the outskirts that effectively charge poor residents to leave their homes — some lift the barrier manually each day.

**5. Transhumanism and the "posthuman" agenda**

- She cites **Peter Thiel and Yuval Harari** on the posthuman, non-biological world, and philosopher **Nick Land** (dark enlightenment) on "technoplastic beings shaped by cybernetics, AI and genetics". Downloading consciousness is, she argues, impossible — "you can't download your soul".
- Elon Musk's **Neuralink**, Sweden's RFID hand implants, ingestibles and wearables are stepping stones to the same endpoint.

**6. Spiritual dimension**

- She quotes **Revelation 17:13** ("These have one mind, and shall give their power and strength unto the beast") and calls the agenda an anti-creation, "egregore"-like thought-form.
- She sees a **Luciferian/esoteric strand** running through the UN via **Alice Bailey's Lucis Trust** (originally Lucifer Publishing Company), its World Goodwill arm, and the "Group of World Servers" — anti-human and anti-creation in nature. The host, who attended a Lucis Trust meeting in 2009, agrees it underpins a universalist one-world religion in which Lucifer/Prometheus is recast as the good guy.
- Technocracy's 1930s roots: an energy-based economy replacing prices — "communism for the people, capitalism for themselves", i.e. communitarianism, neo-feudalism, stakeholder capitalism and a two-tier system.

**7. Resistance**

- **Ireland**: massive street protests ignored by mainstream media; SDG uptake in Ireland is claimed to be **84% versus 14–17% for most of Europe**, so the Irish are "hammered". She stresses protest must remain peaceful — violence would trigger martial law and digital ID.
- Both endorse **non-violent civil disobedience**, invoking Martin Luther King (the host noting Gene Sharp's methods were later weaponised via the NED into colour revolutions — and can be used both ways).
- Host flagged "Patriot Platform" in the UK (men in black and masks, website harvesting names/addresses/phones) as a possible fed-style honeypot, mirroring the US "Patriot Front"; UK Column did good work exposing it.
- Hope is placed in **Gen Z (16–23-year-olds)** rejecting the technocratic and "woke" project.
- She half-jokes that a **Carrington-class solar storm** (like 1861) might be the only thing to short-circuit the entire grid-dependent control system.

**8. Alt-media infighting and influence operations**

- Host defended himself against podcaster **Dan Cohen's** "fraudulent podcaster" accusation over lab-leak coverage, saying he always treated COVID as a ruse for global technocracy; he claims 50%+ of independent media is compromised — a spectrum from well-meaning people to paid intelligence assets.
- A mysterious "financier" offered money to many in alt media; some (e.g. Derek Bros, James Corbett, Whitney Webb, the host) refused, others accepted. Adams left **UK Column**, alleging censorship, propaganda she couldn't accept, and an incident where presenters (Mike Robinson, Vanessa Beeley, Charles Malet) challenged her on migration in an edited *UK Column News* episode; she says she held her ground with the live chat's support and later received a retraction.
