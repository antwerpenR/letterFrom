## 2. Significant points raised (by theme)

### 2.1 Old books as sources / Bible translations

- Jason highlights Harcourt's citation of the **1478 Bible of Bonifacio Ferrer** (Valencian/Catalan) as an example of how **words and meanings changed from translation to translation**. This is historically accurate — Ferrer's Valencian Bible was printed at Valencia in 1478 [4][5].
- He uses this to rebut claims that "everything before 1776 is fake" or that "the Bible was invented in the 1500s." He notes his own **1662 Bible** references earlier translations.
- Harcourt also cites a **Greek emperor "Isaac" (1185)**, styled "Cersac" by "Via Hardun," and (per Jason) **Edward Gibbon**. A chat member ("Joel") identified the cited "Angelus" as a **Greek monk**.

### 2.2 The three branches of the "Caucasian race" (Harcourt's classification)

Jason stresses this is Harcourt's 19th-century classification, "not saying it's real":

1. **Scythian and Tartarian** — "of no consequence in literature"; oral tradition, no documents.
2. **Aramaean** — Assyrians, Chaldeans, Arabs, Phoenicians, Abyssinians, Jews, and probably the original Egyptians.
3. **Slavonian / Indian (Sanskrit)**, called **Pelasgic** by the ancient Greeks, from which Greek, Latin and southern-European idioms derive.

- Jason adds that modern Greek is heavily influenced by Indo-Iranian via the **Dorian invasions** after the "sea peoples" disappeared (1135 BC).

### 2.3 The Giza / Great Pyramid thesis

- The pyramids "carried the reader of hieroglyphics back to the origin of the existing world"; Jason claims pyramids **antedate hieroglyphics**, and that Giza hieroglyphics were "forged in 1837" (he names **Howard Vyse** — transcript "Humphrey's Brewer / Colonel Howard Vice").
- The pyramid was "said to be the work of **Philitis**" (transcript "Feliton"). Jason links this to his book *Lost Scriptures of Giza*: Philitis was post-flood, knew the "secret chambers of Thoth," and was invited to Pharaoh's court.
- **Northern Egypt was "the raised land"** (not Mitzraim/Waset/Keit) because it had recently emerged from water.
- He cites evidence for the pyramid having been **underwater**: "wave crust"/water damage on the Great Pyramid; two-thirds submerged; water damage on the Sphinx; fossils and sea shells found at Giza by **Frederick Norden** (transcript "Frederick Nordon Lewis") in the 1700s; high saline/moisture readings inside the pyramid.
- He attributes the submersion to a **~300 ft dip of the North African plate**, not to rainfall/uniformitarian explanations. He cites the **Lisbon earthquake of 1755** (transcript "71755") as an example of rapid catastrophic change (60,000 killed; the quay broke off and sank).
- **Diodorus Siculus and Strabo** called the Sahara the **Triton Sea**. He dates the plate's rise to **1899 BC** (340 years after the Noachic flood of **2239 BC**), creating the **Nine Bows** (nine rivers) of the Giza delta and the land of Goshen.
- The Great Pyramid = the **"ēkur"** (divine mountain with "the plans with numbers"); later Sumerian ziggurats were named after it. **Giza means "border"** — a border between realms/planes, effectively a "stargate."
- **Mena/Menes** (Egyptian) = **Anom** (Sumerian) — the same name written in opposite directions; also called the **Scorpion King / Narmer**. He places Menes/first dynasty at about **1879 BC**.
- **Abraham/Abram** connection: a **110-year-old man from the Philistine city of Gerar** (Greek *Philistion*, "native of Philistia") visited Pharaoh and knew the secrets of Thoth — the same story Jason says appears in Egyptian texts and in Genesis. He equates **Abram with Brahma**, and **Sarai with Saraswati**. All in *Lost Scriptures of Giza*.

### 2.5 The vapour canopy and the post-flood world

- The pre-flood world had a **vapour canopy/mesosphere** ("an ocean in the sky"), filtering sunlight, letting in some UV. Native Americans called it the **"time of the dark purple light."**
- Result: **giant creatures and people**, higher oxygen and pressure, rapid healing; killing someone "took work" (his reading of Cain and Abel).
- Collapse = sky "flash-froze" like glass/crystal (the **firmament**); iceberg-sized pieces fell, then melted; the **burning sun and distant moon** appeared; large creatures died from **lack of oxygen**, not cold; only smaller creatures born afterwards thrived.
- Volcanoes eject **superheated gases/water** into the atmosphere (creating canopies), not primarily lava.

### 2.6 Ovid, Deucalion and the ark/pyramid

- He quotes **Ovid** on the flood (Jupiter/Zeus destroying the race by flood; clouds pouring down; fountains of the great deep broken up by earthquake).
- **Deucalion** (transcript "Ducalon") = the Greek Noah ("new wine sailor"); his wife **Pyrrha** ("red") = the wine link; offspring = a **"stony progeny."** He links this to petrifaction at the flood (citing physicist **Paul LaViolette**,
- The **ark/original Argo**: the Argo of **Jason and the Argonauts** was named after the original **ark**; the ark's builder = Noah. The **caduceus** (staff with serpents) predates Moses by seven centuries.
- The **first "ark" was the Great Pyramid** — built to preserve knowledge through a cataclysm; survivors later reinterpreted "ark" as a **boat** and had it land on a **divine mountain** (Mount Meru), the "centre of the earth" = Giza (0° latitude/longitude of the pre-cataclysm world).

### 2.7 Babel / Shem / Sham

- Harcourt identifies the **Tower of Babel** with a great pyramid of earth and brick. Jason ties **shem** ("name" *and* "architectural structure") to boundary pillars with witnesses — the Great Pyramid as a **shim/boundary marker** to "somewhere else."
- A **Samaritan Targum** on Babel (Latin: *faciamus... mediocrem locum simulacrum*) is translated by Jason as "let us make a place of worship and an image in the midst of it."

### 2.8 Al-Ma'mun and the alpha pyramid

- In **820 AD** the Caliph **Al-Ma'mun** tunnelled into the Great Pyramid, dislodged a ceiling block and found **three granite plugs**, then the **Grand Gallery, Queen's Chamber, King's Chamber, antechamber, and an empty sarcophagus** — with **only salt crystals** inside.
- Because **no other pyramid worldwide** replicates these upper features, Jason argues the Great Pyramid is the **alpha pyramid** and all others are copies.

### 2.9 Sippar / City of the Sun / Eden

- **Berossus** and **Abydenus** (via **Josephus**, *Contra Apionem* and *Antiquities*, and **Eusebius**) mention **Sippar** ("Sephar"/Hebrew *Sifir* = "place of books"), where the flood hero buried pre-flood records. Jason links this to the **City of the Sun (Heliopolis)** near **Saqqara**, the **benben stone**, and the phoenix.
- He argues **Sippar → Hesperus → the garden of the Hesperides = Eden** ("walled garden/fortification"), the Giza complex with its retaining wall and Nile-fed orchards.

### 2.10 Ham, Noah's nakedness, and the "stony progeny"

- **Hercules = Ham** (per the *Recognitions* of Clement); Ham, knowing the flood was coming, engraved forbidden sciences on metal/stone and recovered them after the flood.
- On **Noah's nakedness**, Jason argues (using "scripture to interpret scripture" and Levitical law) that Ham slept with his **mother**, not a homosexual act — hence Canaan's curse. He cites **Strong's Concordance**: only two references to "father's nakedness."
- Noah's wife = **Naamah** (transcript "Neya/Neyma"), granddaughter of Enoch; he describes Noah and Naamah as "titans" whose immediate offspring were giants.

### 2.11 The serpent, the bull, and the moon

- Harcourt: "The serpent was an ancient symbol of the flood… but after it came into contact with Jewish history, it assumed another meaning." Jason ties **nahash** ("serpent") to "interpreter."
- He links **Typhon** to the **Ouroboros** (circle/cycle = the **Typhonian calendar**), and says **Typhon was originally a canine** — hence the Sphinx was originally **Anubis**, later recarved into a lion (head of younger, discoloured rock).
- **Cerberus** (three-headed dog guarding the underworld) = progeny of **Typhon and Echidna**; he connects it to Anubis and to the Aztec "great dog" at the head of the **nine rivers** (the Nine Bows).
- **Kneph** (transcript "Nef") = temple at Elephantine; he links the name to the **Phoenix**.
- The **bull** was a **lunar** symbol (horns = crescent), sacrificed to the moon; **Minoan** bull-leaping reliefs at **Knossos, Crete**. He argues the bull became solarised only after the patriarchal takeovers following the cataclysm, when goddesses were "kidnapped" as consorts of male gods — his proposed origin of **marriage** (he says he has **no pre-cataclysm references** to marriage as an exclusive contract).

### 2.12 Medusa, Andromeda and human sacrifice

- He reinterprets **Perseus and Medusa**: the **Kraken** = a tsunami; **Joppa (Jaffa)** was destroyed; **Argos** was threatened; a **virgin was sacrificed** to the sea. Jellyfish toxin paralysed the victim (to appear willing); a giant squid was placed on her face; if she survived, her disfigured face ("turns men to stone") became **Medusa**, and the squid's tentacles became the serpent-hair.

### 2.13 Rome, Numa, and the 170-year count

- **King Numa** forbade images of the deity for **170 years** of Rome. Jason notes Rome was founded **753 BC**; 170 years later = **583 BC**, the year the **sun darkened** (predicted by **Thales of Miletus**) and the **Pythian oracle** declared a new age — a "phoenix year" on the 138-year countdown.

### 2.14 May and November / the Typhonian calendar

- The **Scots** avoided marriage in **May** ("only bad women marry in that month").
- The **Celtic year began 1 May**; **beltane** fires (two fires, men and beasts passed between them; purification) and **1 November** fires — Jason links May↔November as a six-month pair.
- **Phoenix = mid-May; Nemesis X = 1–2 November** (his model: the Terran and Nemesis planes intersect twice yearly, at the "ecliptics").
- Irish **Samhain** (transcript "Shna O Shamna"/"midoo") = month of mourning = the Egyptian **mourning for Osiris**.
- The **Milesians'** banner was a **serpent around a rod** (same as the "appendage of Hercules").
- He ties the **Tuatha Dé Danann** and **Fomorians/Fir Bolg** at the **Battle of Moytura** to weaponising a sun-darkening, as **Jacob** did against the Canaanites (1687 BC), and **Atreus** (an astronomer) did against his warlord brother in **1273 BC**.

### 2.15 The "egg" and parthenogenesis

- The **Typhon/orphic egg** symbol (winged egg, pyramid, eye; serpent encircling) = the Typhonian calendar.
- The Egyptian egg-god **Ta** = Phoenician **Vulcan** = Greek **Typhon/Phoenix**.
- Jason predicts (his own view) that **eggs of amphibians, reptiles, fish and birds** will hatch "something else" under a **parthenogenetic** field of Nemesis X — hence dinosaurs on the surface while human remains are found deep in coal seams. He frames this as a natural cycle recurring in **2039–2046**.

### 2.16 Moses, the zodiac and Job

- Harcourt: the writings of Moses show **no astronomical observation**; **no star is named** in the Pentateuch; years/months are **lunar**, with **no intercalation rules**.
- Jason argues there was **no solar zodiac** in the ancient world (Egypt had the **36 decans**); the solar zodiac was built from the 7th century BC over ~600 years.
- **Mazzaroth** (Job) is, he says, a later Jewish interpolation, not "zodiac" — he cites a Jewish source to that effect.
- **Kesil** (transcript "Chessel") = Orion; **Eridanus** = "river of Orion," an emblem of the deluge; **Dendera zodiac** scorpion accompanied by the **hippopotamus** = emblem of Typhon.
- **Wilkinson** dates the zodiac to ~1,800 years before his time, i.e. no zodiac in the most ancient temples (Thebes).

### 2.17 The 1838 author's core argument Jason commends

- Harcourt argues **Deucalion's flood was confused with Noah's**, and that the ancients attributed the flood to **abundant rains**, not lake-bursting. Jason repeatedly accuses Harcourt of the **uniformitarian error** — assuming water *rose* rather than the land *sank*.
