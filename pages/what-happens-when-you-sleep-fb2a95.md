### Core Thesis

Jason presents his metaphysical theory about manifestation centred on the **sleep-wake cycle** as the mechanism by which humans ***co-create reality***. The central claim is that **reality is constructed during sleep (specifically REM sleep)**, not while awake.

### Key Concepts

**1. Money as Energy**

- Money is described as energy, not a physical commodity
- Anxiety about money creates a repellent force: "anxiety will always push away from you the very thing that you're thinking about"
- Focus on money itself pushes it away; the solution is to focus on what the money enables

**2. The "Photo-Negative" Reality Principle**

- "We live in a photo-negative of a real reality"
- The construct operates on **opposite-day logic**: "it's like opposite day every day"
- To address a negative, you must **entertain the exact opposite** of what appears true
- "You literally have to put yourself in the position of entertaining the exact opposite of whatever negative it is that you're trying to address"

**3. The Sleep-Wake Cycle as Co-Creation Mechanism**

| Awake State | Sleep State |
| --- | --- |
| "Falsified version of reality" — a "world of senses" | Where reality is actually built |
| Cannot manifest; only experiences what was previously manifested | The construct processes "probability moulds" and "builds your reality tunnel in REM sleep" |
| "Every single moment is pregnant with possibility" but people act as "slave to the present" | "All the change happens when you check out" |

- **Critical claim**: "You can't build a future reality until you disengage"
- "If you didn't go to sleep at night, you would never be able to create anything"
- The disengagement period is "necessary in order to forge fantasy into facts"

**4. The Broadcasting Mechanism**

- Humans are "broadcasters and receivers"
- Anxiety broadcasts a frequency that confirms lack: "the anxiety tells the construct you don't have a million dollars"
- "You have exactly what you broadcast"

**5. Frequency Alignment**

- "Everything you already want exists in the field"
- "You're not getting it because you're not on the frequency of what it is you desire"
- The construct "puts space between" things not on the same frequency
- "Everything you want has a frequency"

### Surprising/Provocative Points

1. **Sleep is not rest but active creation**: The theory re-frames sleep not as recuperation but as the **only** period when manifestation occurs — the awake state is merely experiencing what was previously "downloaded"
2. **Action is unnecessary**: The claim that "you don't have to do anything" to obtain desired outcomes — only frequency alignment matters
3. **The construct prevents you from being "made a liar"**: If you envision debt-free activities, "the construct is going to correct the problem because you're not going to be made a liar"
4. **The "photo-negative" principle**: Reality operates inversely to appearances — ***acknowledging a problem reinforces it***; ***denying it (through opposite-thought) dissolves it***
5. **The snowball effect**: Eventually, "you don't even recognize when the very thing you wanted from the beginning is received. It's just one of the many things that have improved your life"
