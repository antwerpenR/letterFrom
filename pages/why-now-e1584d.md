This 493-year cycle governs "shifts in the deeper trends that drive human society," particularly "the intersection between power structures, Pluto, and our fundamental beliefs about the world, Neptune." ### Historical Parallels: The 1890s

Three threads emerged simultaneously:

- **Theosophical Society**: Helena Blavatsky's "hidden masters" guiding civilisation—precursors to modern "ascended masters" and extraterrestrial helper narratives

- **Science fiction**: H.G. Wells's *War of the Worlds* (1898) "seeded" the alien invasion narrative

"In that single decade of the 1890s, the alien invasion narrative, the ascended masters narrative, and something resembling an actual UFO phenomenon all entered the culture simultaneously at the seed point of the master cycle that we're still living through now."

### Alice Bailey and the Lucis Trust

Alice Bailey developed a Theosophy-derived system in the early 20th century, writing 24 books allegedly dictated by a "hidden master" named Djwal Khul. She predicted "the externalization of the hierarchy"—the emergence of these masters into visible guidance of humanity, purportedly around this time. Bailey founded the Lucifer Publishing Company, subsequently renamed the **Lucis Trust**. "Today the Lucis Trust is embedded within the United Nations itself. It advises the UN on spiritual matters and it even maintains premises in the UN building in New York." The Pluto-Neptune cycle concerns "the intersection of power and belief"—and here is "an organization promoting these very ideas that's embedded within the center of global governance itself."
