# Why you need a website - and it's easier than you think

💡 Ali explains why you need your own website and tells you how easy it is to do

### [Why you should have a website](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=acBJsjCqgtM&t=51s&ref=rogerprice.me) - 6 benefits and a bonus one!

- develop yourself and your ideas
- helps professional life (interviews and contacts/references)
- lots of connections - networking
- expose yourself to interesting opportunities
- you can have more impact - equiv to good networking - global reach
- you can monetise - eventually if you want!
- 🏆 Bonus - you can avoid the censorship and shadow-banning of social media and establish your own platform and distrubution channels

### [How to create a website](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=acBJsjCqgtM&t=363s&ref=rogerprice.me)

- [Squarespace](https://www.squarespace.com/?ref=rogerprice.me) (if you have no computer skills) or [Ghost](https://ghost.org/?ref=rogerprice.me) (Ali and I use it)
- Do not over-think it - just DO IT and blog weekly
- Get your own domain

### [What to do with it](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=acBJsjCqgtM&t=867s&ref=rogerprice.me)

- You must have at least: About, Contact and Blog
- Blog regularly - weekly is a good schedule...
- Write whatever interests you - something useful for at least one person!
- Document document what you do & learn - it's easier than creating content
- Share on Social Media!
- Focus on writing useful content and encourage people to share!
