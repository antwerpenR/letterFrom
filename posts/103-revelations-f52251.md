#### Crotty Judgement

First up is a nice surprise from Ireland. You may or may not recall the ***Crotty judgement.*** This refers to a 1987 ruling by the *Supreme Court of Ireland* that established the precedent that any amendments to the Irish constitution relating to national sovereignty must be put to a referendum.  Some key details:

- Raymond Crotty brought a legal challenge against the ratification of the Single European Act by the Irish government in 1986 without holding a referendum.
- In April 1987, the Supreme Court unanimously ruled. It found that the Single European Act amended substantial parts of the Irish constitution relating to sovereignty, and as such required approval of the Irish people through a referendum.
- The Crotty judgement established that any future treaties or amendments that substantially affect areas covered by the Irish constitution, such as national sovereignty, must be approved by citizens in a referendum.
- You likely do recall that it resulted in Ireland *having to hold referendums for every major EU treaty since*, including the Maastricht Treaty, Treaty of Nice, Treaty of Lisbon and others. *Nobody has authority to commit for Ireland on such topics without a referendum.*
