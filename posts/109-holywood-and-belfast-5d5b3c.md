In the last few months in Thailand I met so many interesting people and quite a few have also joined up as subscribers. *Welcome to you all*. I hope you find the newsletters interesting and beneficial. Take your time to read them and do refer back to previous ones - the website search works quite well.

Knowing what weather was in store for us in Belfast (it's 25 degrees colder) we made the most of our last weekend in Bangkok to enjoy the warm weather and all the sites of the city. I also met up with a long term subscriber who was visiting in Thailand for a month or so. Nice! We had a good day out together checking out the sights and locations in town.

This week, with all the travelling, I had plenty of time to listen to many good podcasts and interviews - so you have a veritable treat of interesting episodes below. There are many who are awake to what has been going on and they are actively taking steps not only to share their insights but also take practical steps themselves and with others.

> *No one can be told what The Matrix is. You have to see it for yourself.*

Our travel was pretty smooth - we flew with Qatar to Doha and then on to Dublin. This was nice timing, leaving Bangkok around 8pm and arriving Dublin around 6am with just R&R in between. I am pretty much fully recovered from my operation and certainly glad that I took advantage of the excellent medical facilities available in Bangkok.

Everything with our travel was smooth - smoother still since V had persuaded me that business class travel was worth it following my recent operation. From Dublin we also took a taxi up to Belfast - also a smoother experience than the bus! One of the counterintuitive things that you will discover is that once you live on the *bitcoin standard*, *things get cheaper and more affordable every year*.

That reminds me - on **Tuesday 2 April at 7pm** in the evening we have the next [Bitcoin Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/ni-bitcoin-meetup-belfast/events/300045640/?ref=rogerprice.me) here in Belfast at the [Panama Cafe](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Panama+Cafe/@54.5942628,-5.9351596,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x4861099b51500a09:0xc65ccf8e7dcfd872!8m2!3d54.5942628!4d-5.9325793!16s%2Fg%2F11fj6vxg6t?entry=ttu&ref=rogerprice.me) on McClintock Street.
