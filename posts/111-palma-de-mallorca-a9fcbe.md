Full Circle - back in Palma de Mallorca

🇪🇸 Over the weekend V and I flew back to Palma de Mallorca. We have gone *Full Circle* - back here again after one year. Travel was fairly smooth, albeit with a rather early start on Saturday morning and breakfast at Aldergrove. We arrived at our apartment in Palma around lunchtime - nice!

On Sunday the weather was great and to get back in the swing of things here I walked up to [*Castell de Bellver*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bellver_Castle?ref=rogerprice.me). Wow - there was a huge festival that day, [*Diumenge de l'Angel*](https://mallorcaglobalmag.es/en/diumenge-de-langel-2024/?ref=rogerprice.me) or Angel Sunday. It was a big event with several thousand people there around the castle. Among other things there was a chess challenge where a dozen or so people were playing against a master who walked around playing all the tables along with all the usual stands that you would expect at a mediterranean celebration event. I got there in time to see the *Angel’s Walk* (*Passejada de l’Àngel*) - a procession all around the castle.

We had a crazy busy day on Monday. We are in a different apartment this year and it is really very nice - better than the one we had last year and still in the same area next to the Tennis Club. Even so, V wanted a few things to make it perfect for the coming months so we hired a van for the day and went around all the places to get what she wanted - Ikea did good business yet again. Tuesday morning was for assembly and construction - if we had been in Asia we would have taken the delivery and assembly service but this is Europe! I think we are all set now….at least I hope so!

Among other things we also sorted out our *Tennis Club* membership and finished off our day with a nice swim at their pool. The water is certainly cooler than Bangkok but the pool and the setting are great and all the staff there are friendly. It's great to have this world class facility right next door. We have been swimming daily and are now branching out into the other services including gym and Tai-Chi.

The weather here is perfect. 12-25 degrees every day with clear blue skies and spectacularly clear, bright sunshine. This is certainly much nicer than chilly and damp Northern Ireland and it is also nicer than 35 degrees in Bangkok with excellent air quality here too!

This is already the third year that we have come here for spring and it's nice to meet up again with folks that I met before who either live here or have similar travel schedules - go figure! There is a *bitcoin meetup* here in Palma on the 3rd Wednesday of every month - do come along if you are nearby - [17 April 7pm at Ventura Bar](https://www.meetup.com/bitcoin-mallorca/events/300075163/?ref=rogerprice.me). There are quite a few bitcoiners here and yachting is also rather popular!
