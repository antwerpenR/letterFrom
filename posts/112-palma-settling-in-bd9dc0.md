### End of empire bureaucracy

Some of you will know that I lived for some years in Belgium. This past week the Belgian bureaucracy required me to get some paper signed and stamped by the local consulate. First problem - Belgian Consultate in Palma has closed - the nearest one is in Madrid.

I naively assumed that, with two other nationalities to choose from, one of them might help. The British were thoroughly unpleasant and unhelpful. "F\*-off" basically - "you're on your own with that!". The Irish Consultate has a physical presence here and the guy there seemed like he wanted to help but after a day of prevarication he said "HQ says NO - I am not allowed to help you".

Surely the Belgians will help since it is their form? Eventually I manage to get in contactby phone with the Spanish *Honorary Consulate of Belgium*. She seemed pleasant and intelligent - even if she did not like the Swiss and was not happy that the Irish had sent me to her. She was ready to help until she found out that I was not Belgian - she can only help Belgians!

Another day of emails and phone calls and the bureaucrats in Brussels suggest to go to the local Spanish police. I try that. Local police will not help because the form is not in Spanish (only in Dutch, French and English). Another day of delay and I manage to get the form in Spanish and go back to the police. Surprise - now they will not help because I am not a resident of Palma.

I am better than all this - so I did not let it get to me and I did find a solution in the end but if you ever wonder why the West is failing and why the Empire is collapsing, this story might help you to see and understand.

### This is how it works

Pournelle's Iron Law of Bureaucracy, formulated by science fiction author Jerry Pournelle in 1970, states that "in any bureaucratic organisation there will be two kinds of people: those who work to further the actual goals of the organisation, and those who work for the organisation itself." This means that some individuals within a bureaucracy prioritise the interests of the institution over its intended purpose, leading to inefficiencies and misalignment between the organisation's mission and its actions.
