### Simplex Chat

You may well be aware that am a fan and supporter of [SimpleX Chat](https://simplex.chat/?ref=rogerprice.me). Simplex is a recent and still-developing private messaging app with a slant toward privacy over convenience.

*Evgeny*, the lead developer, gives his critical thoughts on other private messengers, explains how privacy software can fund itself, exposes the myth of disappearing messages and gets into some of the finer details of how Simplex Chat has made different choices from its competitors. Amazingly SimpleX gets rid of accounts altogether, allowing you to create as many “profiles” as you want that are localised to your device. The result is, as Evgeny says, SimpleX knows nothing about its users.
