🇬🇧 Last weekend we finally had a few sunny days, so V and I got out to make the most of it. One of our trips was to the *Ulster Folk Museum*. This is on the road to Bangor, a couple of miles outside Holywood - with the *Transport Museum* on the other side of the road. Both are worth a visit if you have not been; last time V and I were there must be at least 25-30 years ago!

There's one to two dozen houses that you can visit, spread out over the extensive grounds (plenty of walking!) - some are replicas while others are original properties that they have relocated to the museum. Many are populated by volunteers who will give you the historical narrative of their building. This was a relaxing afternoon out and we even got *Senior Citizen* tickets! There were plenty of families there having picnics with their children and the weather was great - just the way you would want it to be.
