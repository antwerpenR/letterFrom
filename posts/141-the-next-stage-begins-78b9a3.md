Another evening at the races - Happy Valley

🇭🇰 We had another busy week here in HK. We have been out and about every day. This is the HK lifestyle - with your apartment being so small you have to get out every day and there is plenty of sunny weather to enjoy. This is certainly one of the benefits of living in HK!

Over the weekend I was out hiking with the group - this is a fantastic way to meet people and to see some of the more interesting parts of HK. This week the hike was *Wong Nai Chung Gap* to Central - so pretty handy! Sunday I was out hiking again with a friend and we decided to go from Central up to the Peak and back down for lunch in FCC - this is something of a tradition.

During the week I got in a few more hikes, including *SKW* to *YSW* midweek with a great brunch at *Jaybird* on the Pier (recommended). Following that V decided that she wanted to go back for an evening at the races again this week - so that was another night out.

Other destinations during the week included *Sham Shui Po*, *Mong Kok* and *Causeway Bay* in addition to *Hung Hom* and *North Point*. You have got to love how well-connected everything is in HK.

We had Friday lunch at the Windsurfing club as usual and Friday evening V has booked us to go to the *HK Cultural Center* for some [modern dance](https://asiaplus.gov.hk/2024/en/+1+1+1+?ref=rogerprice.me); let's see - this is not something you can do in Bangkok, Palma or Belfast! This coming weekend I will be out hiking again and there are quite a few friends visiting from abroad. Another advantage of living in the world city that is HK!

Meanwhile, in *the matrix* things do seem to have moved on to the next phase. The much anticipated US (s)election came and went pretty much as expected and I tend to agree with Jason's perspective on what follows - he did predict this years ago and now seems to be the time. My thinking on all of this and how you can best navigate what ensues pretty much aligns with what David Icke shares in [*The Reveal*](https://rogerprice.me/140-halloween-in-hong-kong/#book-of-the-week), so if you have not read it yet you might want to make a start.
