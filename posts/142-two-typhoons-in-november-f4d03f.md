At the Star Ferry, Kowloon-side - in between the typhoons!

🇭🇰 Over last weekend we had typhoon Yinxing pass by Hong Kong! In case that was not enough, during the week we got typhoon Toraji as well. This is the latest in the year that we have had typhoons - normally in November the weather should just be warm and sunny every day. So this was not the best time for all the visitors who came here but we had a good time anyway.

Saturday I had a great hike from *Aberdeen* to *Central*. This was via quite a few lesser-known tracks and really good fun. I also had some great discussions with quite a few of the hikers - we talked about books, philosophy of life and bitcoin!

Sunday I was out again hiking - this time we did a *Lamma* *Island* loop with a friend who lives there as well as a former colleague visiting HK for a bit. We had good fun together with quite some reminiscences and it was good to catch up on what we have been doing since we were colleagues and what's next!

Later in the afternoon I caught up with yet another former colleague visiting HK as one stop on a much larger regional trip. Yet another great meeting with excellent and interesting discussion and I think we both learned lots!

In all the discussions with people we discussed quite a few book recommendations. I love to get book recommendations and I am happy to share mine. To make it easier, I have extensively revamped the [*References*](https://rogerprice.me/references/) section of the website to give one single list of books in alphabetical order of title. This should make things easier to find with just one main source. Do check it out and let me know.

Monday I had to get some HK admin done. Thankfully, being Hong Kong, you can outsource dealing with the machine to others to do it on your behalf. Obviously this is not necessarily optimal and *Austrian Economics* would rightly say that there is likely too much bureaucracy and administration but it is good to have options and this is free-market capitalism in action.

Tuesday was warm and sunny so V and I set out first to *Ocean Park* and from there we walked along *Mills and Chung Path* via *Deep Water Bay* and *Middle Island* to *Repulse Bay*. There I crossed the *Bridge of Longevity* again and collected my extra days and then we had lunch together by the beach.

Wednesday morning the rain from typhoon Toraji arrived but we had a dry patch around 10am so I seized the opportunity and did a nice hike on *Lamma* from *SKW* to *YSW* finishing up with brunch at *Banyan Cafe* since *Jaybird* was closed for some reason. Later in the day V decided that she wanted yet another evening at the races - so *Happy Valley* it was - only to have that terminated early when the Observatory announced Typhoon 8 was about to be hoisted! That's life in HK.

Thursday was pretty miserable with occasional rain and dark clouds. With Typhoon 8 in force, everything was closed to start the day although (as usual) there was no sign of any wind! Some do say that since the [Big Buddha](https://www.nextstophongkong.com/big-buddha/?ref=rogerprice.me) arrived at Ngong Ping the typhoons don't come to HK any more….and there does seem to be some truth to this - we mostly just get the rain bands from typhoons that go east or west of HK.

On Friday the weather was still rubbish with plenty of rain so my regular hike to the Peak and our lunch at the windsurfing club were postponed. Instead we took the opportunity to do some necessary tasks and errands around HK. After that the weekend can start - I plan to be hiking on [Saturday](https://www.meetup.com/eye-opener-hk-hiking-%E9%96%8B%E7%9C%BC%E7%95%8C%E8%A1%8C%E5%B1%B1%E9%9A%8A/events/304226714?ref=rogerprice.me) and Sunday.
