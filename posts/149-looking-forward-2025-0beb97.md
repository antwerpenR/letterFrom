### Book of the week and recommendations

*The Alchemist* has been in my TBR queue for a while. It is wildly popular worldwide and an international best-seller (more than 65m copies), translated into more than 80 languages. It was Laura's video below that prompted my to get out my copy and read it. It is not a long read (4-5 hours) and I wholeheartedly recommend it to everyone. The book is packed with wisdom and life lessons.

The book tells the mystical story of *Santiago*, an Andalusian shepherd boy who yearns to travel in search of a worldly treasure. His quest will lead him to riches far different—and far more satisfying—than he ever imagined. Santiago's journey teaches us about the essential wisdom of listening to our hearts, recognising opportunity and learning to read the omens strewn along life's path, and, most importantly, following our dreams.

You will see how following your dream, while important, will not be easy and you should expect to be challenged at every opportunity. Only those who persist go on to live their Personal Legend.
