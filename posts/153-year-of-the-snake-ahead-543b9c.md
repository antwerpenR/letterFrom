### Too many things just do not add up

Have we have been lied to? During 1850-1915 Great Expositions (Fairs) were built worldwide including: Chicago 1893, Paris 1900, St. Louis 1904, San Francisco 1915. Per the official story: these giant 7-1,200 acre expos were each built in an impossibly short time of less than two years. Then following the end of the event, they were demolished, destroyed, and thrown into landfills.

Howdie reads Chapter 3 above - chapter 1 [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oPjy2fIGKIs&ref=rogerprice.me) and chapter 2 [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2IA2kjREqN8&t=3s&ref=rogerprice.me) - full book [here](https://www.amazon.com/Exposing-Expositions-1851-1915-Revised-2021/dp/8269126624?crid=1VIIVY36W0D2N&sprefix=exposing+the+world+fairs%2Caps%2C170&sr=8-1&ref=rogerprice.me)

Each of these fairs were built to resemble Ancient Rome, and Howdie now feels that is no accident. But were the buildings of these World Expositions new ones being built, or old ones being restored- part of a civilisation that was coexisting with Ancient Rome and Greece? Click above for Howdie to read his [book](https://www.amazon.com/Exposing-Expositions-1851-1915-Revised-2021/dp/8269126624?crid=1VIIVY36W0D2N&sprefix=exposing+the+world+fairs%2Caps%2C170&sr=8-1&ref=rogerprice.me) to you.
