#### The Biology of Belief

This week "The Biology of Belief" by Bruce Lipton challenges traditional views on genetics and biology. The central premise is that our mind, beliefs and thoughts have a much more profound impact on our biology and overall health than you were ever allowed to think.

> [!image]
> [![Recommended reading - it will open your mind](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/2025/02/image.jpeg)](https://annas-archive.org/md5/06619f1a4ca91905f1aa208dba92891c?ref=rogerprice.me)

#### Key Ideas in the book

1. **Cell Intelligence**: Lipton argues that cells are incredibly smart and adaptable, with their behaviour controlled by the ***cell membrane*** rather than the nucleus. Specifically cells with their nucleus removed continue to function for months - they are just unable to reproduce since they lack the DNA in the nucleus.
2. **Environmental Influence**: He goes on to explain that environmental signals, including our thoughts and perceptions, play a crucial role in shaping our biology. He presents much evidence that this is not DNA or gene related showing multiple examples where identical genes result in very different results. All of this challenges the concept of *genetic determinism.*
3. **Evolution and Cooperation**: Cooperation, rather than competition, should be the central tenet in our understanding of evolution. There then follows evidence that our conscious and subconscious minds significantly influence our physical health through chemical signals in our body. Obviously this strikes right at the heart of Darwin's ***Theory*** *of Evolution* and the empire fervently rejects any challenge on this topic - however it seems that even Darwin himself had some doubts in this direction (of course they have been suppressed).
4. **Placebo Effect and Power of the Mind**: The mind's power over the body is demonstrated through the placebo effect, highlighting the role of belief in healing. Lipton explains the difference between the *Conscious* and *Unconscious* mind and how this influences our behaviour and biology. Recall that we have discussed both of these topics back in Newsletter #136 in Massimo Citro's [Basic Code of the Universe](https://rogerprice.me/136-holywood-and-belfast/#book-of-the-week) - another book that the Empire does not want you to read.

Other authors that the Empire does not want you to read include: *Rupert Sheldrake* on *Morphic fields*, *Ervin Laszlo* on the *Akashic field*, *David Bohm* and *Karl Pribram* on *Holographic Paradigm*, and *Masaru Emoto* on the *memory of water* and *homeopathy*.
