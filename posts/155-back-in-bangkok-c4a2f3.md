🇹🇭 V and I are now back in Bangkok. We made the right decision to travel back to Holywood for a bit and to give priority to family and friends there. We are now happy to be back home in SE Asia and we have the rest of [our 2025 plans](https://rogerprice.me/149-looking-forward-2025/#so-whats-the-plan) ahead of us.

We plan to be back in *Holywood* at the end of March for several family birthdays, before heading on to *Palma de Mallorca* to enjoy some European spring weather and see our friends there. How are your plans for 2025 going?

I have really had an action packed 10 days or so. It has been great to have quite a few meetings with Newsletter Subscribers and quite a few bitcoiners, both in Belfast and here in Bangkok. We have been discussing my projects and they also have project ideas of their own - how interesting...

Our travel back was quite smooth, if somewhat arduous, taking more than 24 hours door to door - two lost days due to the timezone differences. We were up early in the morning and drove to Dublin; it is certainly easier than taking the bus. After a lazy afternoon in the airport the flight to Qatar was OK, arriving after midnight local time. We got to "enjoy" the Doha airport facilities overnight for our connecting flight departing 0900 that arrived in Bangkok 1900 local time. Our luggage was first off the flight (luck happens sometimes!) and we had a car waiting to take us back to our apartment.

The weather here in Bangkok is fairly warm - [up to 37 degrees](https://rogerprice.me/february-in-bangkok/) most days but at least it is not humid like Hong Kong. Warm as it is we certainly prefer this to being cold in Northern Europe in the winter! For those who wonder - there are 3 seasons in Bangkok: *Hot season* (now, November thru March, *Very Hot* (April,May) and *Hot & Wet* (June thru October).
