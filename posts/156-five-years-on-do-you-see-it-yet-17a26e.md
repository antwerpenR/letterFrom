There is always something here!

🇹🇭 CNY and Valentines day have now passed and Bangkok is a bit quieter again. The next public holiday here is *Equinox* on 20 March, so this is the time to relax and enjoy the sunny weather.

V and I have been out and about around town and I have been meeting up with Newsletter subscribers here in Bangkok. Lots of interesting discussion. The weather in Bangkok is [slightly cooler](https://rogerprice.me/bangkok-late-february/) at 34 degrees and we have occasional showers to change things up a bit. Best times to be out are early morning and late afternoon.

During the week I also noticed that the screen on my trusty CalyxOS phone was coming loose. You might recall that Elvis replaced my cracked screen for me about 18 months ago in Hong Kong. Deeper inspection showed that the battery was swelling and this pushed the screen loose. Happily, here in SE Asia things like this are quickly solved. A short trip to MBK Mall and USD$ 25 got me a nice new battery while I waited and it is really like having a new phone again.

We had the usual Bitcoin meetup on Monday, back at the location where we had the 10-year party last year. I had some great discussions with rather interesting bitcoiners and it was nice to meet people that I had not seen since last year's party! It seems like we are back into roaming locations again after a good spell in Soi 23. We also had a meeting at BoB Space with HRF and Bitcoiners from the Greater Mekong region.

This week also marks some interesting anniversaries. For one, it is a year since my operation here in Bangkok and I am fully recovered and have restarted yoga. I can recommend the 30 minute [Bikram sequence](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xc3FOKlCXZM&ref=rogerprice.me) in the morning and [yin yoga](https://youtu.be/7yiV2mYBAy4?si=rcgnpMZKfzs1KldE&t=37&ref=rogerprice.me) in the evenings. Yoga in the mornings gives you energy for the day and yoga in the evenings gives you the deepest and most restful sleep ever.

Another anniversary is that it is now 5 years since we needed "*2 weeks to flatten the curve*". Check my [diary entry](https://mega.nz/file/FE4xBIzJ?ref=rogerprice.me#p8ys8HjPCHY-2o5FQjJZuVNU1nuSabW7b45uXCX-C34) from 3 years ago: a full 2 years after that initial lie. Hopefully by now you are awake to the lies and crimes that have been committed.

You will likely appreciate that the Covid times have passed (that was *first seal*) and we are now in the time of the *second seal* when yet more state crime and "unveiling" takes place - just look at the news these days and you will see it. Do read this week's [*Closing Thoughts*](#%F0%9F%A4%94-closing-thoughts) for deeper insight on what is happening and what comes next. Be aware and observe but do not participate or give this your energy.

For those of you who are just waking up to this I recommend "[Anatomy of the State](https://rogerprice.me/89-hiking-in-hong-kong/#book-of-the-week)" as a starter book to explain things. It is freely available in PDF and Audiobook and at only 60 pages, you can read it in one hour.

### Evil Amazon - you have been warned

Starting on February 26th, 2025, Amazon is removing a feature from its website allowing you to download purchased books to a computer and then copy them manually to a Kindle over USB.  This also prevents you making your own backup of the books that you purchased.

This is a reminder that you don’t actually own much of the digital content you consume, and without the ability to back up copies of ebooks, you could lose them entirely if anything happens to your amazon account and amazon. You are watching them prepare for re-writing history and forcing you to accept new terms and conditions due to your "*sunk cost*". George Orwell warned you and now it is happening in front of your eyes.
