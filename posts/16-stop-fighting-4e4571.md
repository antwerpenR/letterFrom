Just a short one this week - I have been really busy with work and am now starting a few weeks off.  Still on location at the moment.  Hope to be getting back into my stride on reading, watching, listening and thinking - do feel free to reach out if you want to discuss anything that you read here!

As the madness around the world continues to esclate I feel compelled to revisit a few things that I have posted in previous newsletters.  Maybe collecting them here in one place does make them a bit easier to get your head around and see clearly.

Back in [issue 7](https://rogerprice.me/time-to-wake-up/) I recalled growing up in Belfast in the 1970s and living through the proxy war fought in my hometown.  More recently I lived through a remarkably similar situation in HK during 2019 and observed it happening too in Bangkok with some of the same participants (instigators) spotted in media clips who had travelled from HK, presumably to orchestrate it there too.   For years the motto of Belfast was "Belfast Says No" and it was prominent in a huge banner on the City Hall.  Bitterness and resentment reigned for years until "Peace Broke Out", led by **the women of Belfast** insisting that people "**Stop Fighting**".   Remember the Peace People?

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/2022/02/image-2.png)

Bottom line - war is never good.  **Benefits to society only ever emerge at times when people are working together, trading and collaborating for the better good.**

### Perpetual War

The perpetual war footing that we find ourselves in was clearly warned about in [George Orwell's 1984](https://youtu.be/37N0aFmO19o?ref=rogerprice.me) - spot the drumbeat of hostile distant foreign empires and their aggressions and victories.

No surprise that this was the conclusion of the [Report from Iron Mountain](https://archive.org/details/pdfy-A5uQx1ByqfwWuHma) from a 1967 US  government panel which **concluded that war, or a credible substitute for war, is necessary if governments are to maintain power**.

With your eyes now open you might also want to read [Albert Pike's letter](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1KnhRAx1pU64Y2Gnv5YABmm2Tw7ocsRyK/view?usp=sharing&ref=rogerprice.me).

### You might be surprised...

Lex's long-form [interview with Saifedean](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gp4U5aH_T6A&ref=rogerprice.me) - a well-worthwhile listen (do so at 1.75x speed).  You will understand how WW1 was funded and how this destroyed the British Pound, leading to WW2 and the introduction of US Dollar as Reserve Currency and its inevitable decline into Fiat currency in 1971.   Particularly insightful and clear are his [first hand experiences from growing up in Palestine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gp4U5aH_T6A&t=14401s&ref=rogerprice.me) and his clear explanation as to how all the issues came about.   If you want to read the original Bank Of England documents (that only became public in 2017) see [The Bank of England 1914-21 (Unpublished War History)](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/archive/bank-of-england-1914-21-ww1?ref=rogerprice.me).  I think it is not linked on their website but still accessible if you know where to look (maybe download and safestore it before it disappears). This corroborates and elaborates on what you can read in [The Creature from Jeckyll Island](https://www.audible.com/pd/The-Creature-from-Jekyll-Island-Audiobook/B00DZUGWX6?qid=1641109461&sr=1-1&ref=a_search_c3_lProduct_1_1&pf_rd_p=83218cca-c308-412f-bfcf-90198b687a2f&pf_rd_r=BVAQ5VZHY39AYPT6ZJX8).

### Likely you missed these...

Be rather concerned about the latest EU Rules - Steve explains [here](https://overcast.fm/+LUuRtu7lU/1:34:18?ref=rogerprice.me).  Perhaps you prefer to [read the EU law yourself](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=COM%3A2022%3A209%3AFIN&qid=1652451192472&ref=rogerprice.me).  135 pages long, they do not make it easy to understand what they are up to but the intention is clearly to outlaw all private communication between individuals and to legalise automated scanning or all communication for anything that the government does not agree with.  This will eventually lead to banning all person to person transactions unless the government can intermediate itself into them - this has long been known - [Revelation 13: 16-18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Revelation%2013:16-18&version=KJV&ref=rogerprice.me).  It's funny how many people miss that one - it's hardly like it was secret or hidden!

This is in addition to the proposal that was voted on 22 May to get all of the emergency C-19 restrictions codified into normal law so that they can be deployed on you any time or all the time in future.   Likely  you missed that one too - [watch here](https://www.bitchute.com/video/i1s4NPyfBeI3/?ref=rogerprice.me) or click below for the explainer.
