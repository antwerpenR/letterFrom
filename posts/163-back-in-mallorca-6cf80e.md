Back in Palma this week

🇪🇸 V and I travelled back to Mallorca over last weekend. We did get to enjoy superbly sunny weather in Belfast before we left and I have included a few more photos below. We are somewhat repeating our tried and tested travel schedule, but there are also quite a few extensions and variations ahead.

It was nice to check back into the same apartment that we had last year. One advantage of planning ahead and having good relations with people around the world is that you can come back to familiar locations. Everything really was pretty much as we left it last June. It is really nice to be back here again and we have quickly settled into our daily routine here including daily yoga and swimming in the sunshine.

We are staying in Santa Catalina part of Palma. We really like this area - everything is very convenient. For example the Market is just a few minutes walk and V loves to do her shopping there. It is also a great place to drop in for local food and snacks and it is a real center for people to meet and chat - very nice!

Among other things we are back at the *Tennis Club* and enjoying the facilities there very much. We also enjoy our weekly Tai Chi sessions. I am also back in contact with many friends and bitcoiners from here and we have quite a few meetups planned in the weeks ahead.

### GrapheneOS Installation

This week I got round to installing *GrapheneOS* in my *Pixel 7a*. I am so happy with the result and I am sharing with you the steps and some observations on the process in case you are motivated to do this yourself.

The entire process takes about 20 minutes - that's all. You just need to follow the instructions on their website and I do recommend that you use the Web Installer.

> [!bookmark]
> [GrapheneOS web installer](https://grapheneos.org/install/web?ref=rogerprice.me)
> Web-based installer for GrapheneOS, a security and privacy focused mobile OS with Android app compatibility.
> — GrapheneOS | ![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/thumbnail/opengraph.png)

Click for prerequisites and the step-by-step web installer

A few remarks you may find helpful for the install process:  
- It is easiest to do on Mac, Linux requires a few extra steps - if you are on Windoze I recommend that you make getting off that your priority!  
- You do need to create a first Android user (you will erase it shortly). No need to upgrade Android (indeed after the Pixel 4a debacle you likely want to avoid this) but you do need a WiFi connection to be able to *Enable OEM unlocking*.  
- Other than that, just go step by step and follow the instructions exactly  
- the first thing to install is F-Droid (do this with Vanadium) - from there you can install Aurora Store and you are off to the races  
- I do recommend that you set up multiple user profiles and share F-Droid and Aurora store with each of the profiles  
- Set other profiles NOT to run in background  
- Talk to me if you have questions or want to know more
