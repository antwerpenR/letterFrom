🇪🇸 We have had a busy week here in Palma. We have fully settled back into our routine here and we have a busy weekend ahead (more on that next week).

The weather changed to be cooler this week and we had quite some rain on Sunday and Monday. The rest of the week was nice and sunny but notably fresher than when we arrived. That did not stop people getting outside to enjoy themselves.

Over the weekend I had a nice lunch with quite a bunch of bitcoiners here. We also had the regular Bitcoin meetup on Wednesday and I have been having ad-hoc meetings with bitcoiner friends around town. We have planned a few more meetups in the coming weeks so let me know if you want to meetup or join.

Midweek, V and I did a nice walk along the beaches and waterfront all the way to Playa de Palma. This is a walk that we do every year and it is the walk that triggered me to say "*enough is enough*" and to "*opt out*" when we were here three years ago. With hindsight, that was certainly the right decision and I'm so glad to have taken it. [Deleting my LinkedIn account](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YRvBQdJlBeo&ref=rogerprice.me) formalised my "*exit from the Matrix*" - immensely liberating and highly recommended.

The local council seems to be investing in the waterfront with quite a lot of new breakwaters having been placed to protect the beaches. The whole walk is lively and full of people enjoying themselves. The lifeguard huts seem to have been renewed and there seemed to be quite a few more parasols out and being placed.

I know that I have said it before and done nothing yet but V and I might even learn a bit of Spanish while we are here. Considering that we are return visitors here it would be good to get off *Zero*, at least. Let's see...
