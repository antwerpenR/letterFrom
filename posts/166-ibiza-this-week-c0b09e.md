Sunday was *Diumenge de l'Angel*. This is a festival that takes place on the Sunday after Easter every year. It commemorates the feast day of the *Ángel Custodio* (*Guardian Angel*) who was the primitive patron saint of Palma. The festival is held up at *Bellver Castle* with the *giants* and *parades* all around. Also many concurrent chess matches.

> [!bookmark]
> [Diumenge de l’Àngel 2025: programme of activities - Mallorca Global Mag](https://mallorcaglobalmag.es/en/diumenge-de-langel-2025/?ref=rogerprice.me)
> Next Sunday, 27 April, the Castillo de Bellver will host Diumenge de l’Àngel 2025 from 10:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
> — Mallorca Global Mag | ![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/thumbnail/Diumenge-de-lAngel-2-e1745330117801.webp)

The celebration has a long history, dating back over 600 years. The modern iteration of the festival was revived in 1981 and has since become a significant festival in the annual calendar.

During the week we made a hop over to Ibiza to meet up with some family who were visiting there. We did a tour of a few of the main sights on the island: *Eivissa* old town and castle, *Festival Club* and *Santa Gertrudis*.

*Festival Club* opened in 1972 as the ultimate party destination on the island featuring impressive architectural elements like large arches at the entrance. The main area was arranged in a circle around a large stage, surrounded by stone steps, benches, and tables. It was intended as a live concert venue and even included its own bullring. A fall in tourists in the mid 70's (and oil crisis) forced it to close and now it is reclaimed by nature and a popular spot with local graffiti artists.
