### I learned a new thing!

This is nice. All of the main browsers are now supporting *Text Fragment Linking*. This makes linking back to previously published material easier since it is no longer necessary to link to a published Anchor Tag, rather you can link to text on the page itself! Read the explanation and details here:

> [!bookmark]
> [Text Fragments Enable Deep Linking on Web Pages - TidBITS](https://tidbits.com/2025/04/23/text-fragments-enable-deep-linking-on-web-pages/?ref=rogerprice.me)
> After decades of only being able to link to the top of Web pages or specially prepared named anchors, modern browsers now support text fragment links that can scroll to and highlight any text on any page. This long-awaited feature brings us closer to Ted Nelson’s original vision for hypertext and makes sharing specific information dramatically more efficient.

### You are likely missing out

We have discussed quite a few times about so-called *Artificial Intelligence* (AI). You will be aware of my opinion that there is currently not much *intelligence* rather it is incredibly *advanced pattern matching*. That said, you are missing out on enormous productivity and efficiency gains if you do not use these tools. They even make possible things that were previously impossible to do.

For the past few weeks I have been experimenting with [*NanoGPT*](https://nano-gpt.com/?ref=rogerprice.me). This is a most interesting tool. It gives you access to *all of the current AI models* (their full and unfettered versions), allowing you to select which model(s) you wish to use or even letting it decide which model is best for your specific query.

Over the last weeks I have used this to answer dozens of questions needing deep search and also to troubleshoot numerous installation and configuration issues on Linux systems. I can honestly say that without using tools like this I could not have done those things at all - certainly not with the thoroughness and accuracy that these tools can archive. Total cost paid - less than $1.50.

Even more interesting is that, whilst this is a paid service, you can use it fully anonymously - without creating an account and paying by Monero. The fees are very reasonable and you avoid all of the subscription nonsense. FYI I use Monero for all of my recurring-type and/or subscription services wherever possible. Indeed having this (or Bitcoin over Lightning) as a payment option is one of the prime selection factors for me.
