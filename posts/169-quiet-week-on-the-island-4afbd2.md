🇪🇸 This week V and I have been enjoying some quiet time here back in Palma de Mallorca. We have been really busy for the last few weeks so it is nice to just be able to do our own things here and not have too many obligations. In the coming weeks we have family and friends visiting us here on the island so there will be plenty of things going on.

Midweek we had the regular Bitcoin meetup round at Ventura Bar. It was nice to meet up again with Chris and all the others. On Friday we had an influx of the Belgian side of the family - coming to visit us here for a while. Needless to say, V has lots of things planned and this ends our quiet week!

On Sunday we have yet another Bitcoiner Brunch. A few of the crowd are heading off around the world (as one does). It's good to have these face to face meetups to establish and build personal relationships and contacts with like-minded and intelligent folk with whom you can have some good discussion on many significant topics.

### Book and Thread of the week

For the last couple of weeks I have been re-reading a book that I have mentioned before. This time I complemented my reading with the [audiobook](https://www.audible.co.uk/pd/The-Holographic-Universe-Audiobook/1662083998?source_code=ASSGB149080119000H&share_location=pdp&ref=rogerprice.me) - this has significantly improved the experience and I have learned so much more from the book this time round. It is mind-blowing and you should really give this one a go.

Recall R2D2 projecting Princess Leia, begging for Obi-wan Kenobi's help - that was a hologram. Holograms are even much more interesting; when you make a hologram (using laser light) you can break the photographic plate but each part of the plate can regenerate the entire hologram! When you look at things around you they appear in the distance; in reality the image is on the back of your eyes but your brain "projects" it out to where you "see" it. Close your eyes and visualise your favourite place - imagine walking around it, picking up objects and looking behind things; what is different about this image than seeing it with your eyes...or maybe in a dream? Perhaps now you see where all this goes?
