### Nostr and Reads

If you have not yet looked at Nostr, it is time that you did. Nostr is a decentralised social network protocol built on simplicity and resilience. Unlike traditional platforms, Nostr isn't a single entity but a set of rules allowing diverse clients and relays to interact. Its key feature is censorship resistance: no single authority can block or remove content, as users can choose different relays to broadcast and receive messages. It is also a platform for all sorts of new applications.

> [!image]
> [![Click to create your own Nostr account - just follow the Getting Started instructions](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/2025/05/image-38.jpeg)](https://primal.net/Travellr?ref=rogerprice.me#reads)
> Click to create your own Nostr account - just follow the Getting Started instructions

Primal is one of the best Nostr clients but you are able to use any one of many clients and you can move between them with your Private/Public key. Just enjoy the freedom from the madness on the X, Facebook and other social media and follow whoever you like. The link above gives you access to my Newsletters that I am also copying over to Nostr.

**Key Benefits:**

- **Censorship Resistance:** Impervious to single-point censorship efforts.
- **Decentralisation:** No central control means greater network stability and resistance to manipulation.
- **User Ownership:** You control your data and identity using cryptographic keys.
- **Open and Extensible:** The simple protocol allows for innovation and diverse client development.
- **Data Portability:** Your identity and data are not locked into a single platform. You can move between clients and relays.
- **Global Availability:** Operates worldwide without regional restrictions inherent in centralised platforms.

Creating an account on Nostr is free and you have have as many as you want - just remember to securely save your *Private key* for each account you make. I do recommend that you use a Password Manager for this - [KeepassXC](https://keepassxc.org/?ref=rogerprice.me) is a good option - it is also free and open source.

### The 13th Word

Also known as a "passphrase", the so-called 13th word gives very important privacy and security extension to any capable Bitcoin Wallet. This is a powerful feature giving you as many additional wallets as you want that are derived from your 12-word seed but known only to you. It comes with the responsibility of remembering the passphrase perfectly. If you're new to Bitcoin, it's best to thoroughly understand the basics of the 12-word recovery phrase before experimenting with passphrases. Always test your recovery process before committing significant funds.
