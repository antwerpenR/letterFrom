### When you kick the hornets' nest...

I have told you many times how much I (and countless others) hate Amazon for the restrictions they impose on the Kindle and books that you "buy" from them. This culminated in February with the removal of the ability to download and keep your own backup copy of books that you had paid for.

Well there is now a tremendous backlash and I welcome that. You too may want to join in. You can "jailbreak" your kindle (it's simple to do, and reversible) and this enables you to read any format of book on it and it also recovers the ability to make backup copies of your books.
