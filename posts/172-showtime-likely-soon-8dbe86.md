### How could you have known?

This week I have been re-reading [*The Sovereign Individual*](https://www.whatyouwilllearn.com/book/the-sovereign-individual/?ref=rogerprice.me). It was written back in 1997-1998. The writing style is a delight to read and the insights that they share will blow your mind.

I only discovered the book back in 2012 when I moved to *Hong Kong* and since then I keep getting drawn back to re-read and check sections. Each time it seems ever more prescient and obvious. How did we not see all these things before? Well, you can certainly see them now if you look around.

This time I have been reading the book while listening to the audiobook - this is an excellent way to absorb the wisdom within. If you get only one medium, get the audiobook.

### Ad-free viewing

You may well have noticed that Google is getting more aggressive on pushing Ads into youtube. The latest push seems to be targeted at people who are using alternative browsers (than Chrome) that still support *Manifest V2*. You should recall that we have discussed before how [Manifest V3 is a devious attempt by Google](https://rogerprice.me/139-letter-from-hong-kong/#:~:text=and%20alternative%20systems.-,Google%20does%20evil) to neuter the capabilities of Ad-blockers and that this does fundamentally reduce your security since scammers will exploit this. Everything that Google claims about this "*being for your safety*" is a blatant lie.

Assuming that you want to avoid weakening your security controls, the best option does seem to remain *Brave Browser* with *uBlock Origin* and *Privacy Badger* installed and active. Also *Orion Browser* from *Kagi* (currently Mac/iOS only) is most interesting since it built on *Webkit* (not *Chromium*) but it does support Chrome extensions like *uBlock Origin* with *Manifest V2* support. Grok did a comprehensive [comparison here](https://rogerprice.me/orion-brave-and-chrome/). *Mullvad browser* (being based on a fork of Firefox) is yet another one to keep in mind. Bottom line - you have options - recall that we also [discussed them here](https://rogerprice.me/160-st-patricks-day-again/#:~:text=For%20those%20of%20you%20who%20are%20fed%20up%20with%20Firefox%20and%20looking%20for%20alternative).

For additional backup you probably want to have a copy of [*FreeTube*](https://github.com/FreeTubeApp/FreeTube?ref=rogerprice.me) - it is straightforward to install and very easy to use. You can also donate to support them. You do need to take time to add in your subscriptions but you only need to do this once and you might even use this as an opportunity to omit and drop those that you no longer want to see!

Another great app to have and know about is [*Grayjay*](https://grayjay.app/?ref=rogerprice.me). Grayjay enables you to watch video content on your own terms, fully retaining ownership and having control over what you watch.

> [!bookmark]
> [Grayjay App - Follow Creators Not Platforms](https://grayjay.app/?ref=rogerprice.me)
> — Grayjay | ![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/thumbnail/Vectors-Wrapper\_7.svg)

Fabulously useful app - buy it to support Louis, the creator - license is valid on all platforms

There are so many interesting features in this app that works on desktop as well as Android/GrapheneOS phones. Not least is the ability to follow creators across multiple platforms - eg YouTube, Odysee, Rumble, Bitchute etc. You simply install the plugins for whichever platforms you like.

Lastly - you likely want to have a copy of [*yt-dlp*](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp?ref=rogerprice.me). This is a free, open-source, command-line tool designed for downloading videos and audio from YouTube and over 1,700 other websites, including Vimeo, Dailymotion, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and Twitch. It is a fork of the older *youtube-dl.* Released under the Unlicense, *yt-dlp* is available for Windoze, macOS, Linux, and Android (via Termux), making it highly versatile.
