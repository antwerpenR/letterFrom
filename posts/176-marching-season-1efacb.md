🇬🇧 Somehow I managed to capture the photo above during a momentary sunny spell! The weather has been the usual Northern Ireland Summer - mostly cloudy with frequent showers and occasional heavier rain and intermittent sunny spells. We have totally missed out on all the apparent heatwaves elsewhere.

Tuesday morning was the opening of the Weaver Cross Halt. This is the old Europa Bus station. It has been nicely converted into what could be a lively public space. The waiting room has been transformed with tables and there is a very lively bar. In what used to be the bus lanes there are now small cafes and shops in converted containers - unusual but it might well work. Let's see!

During the week we fitted in several lunches with friends and we are getting ready for a couple of family birthdays this month, including mine! A recommended stop is [McKee's farm](https://www.mckeesproduce.com/?ref=rogerprice.me). Fresh and freshly cooked local food and they have a great shop where you can buy and support local produce. It should be clear to you by now that it will be increasingly important going forward to support places and initiatives like this.

As we head into the weekend we have the [Eleventh Night Bonfires](https://rogerprice.me/the-famous-twelfth/#:~:text=Eleventh%20Night%20Bonfires) followed by the [*Glorious Twelfth*](https://rogerprice.me/the-famous-twelfth/) on Saturday. This kicks off the Marching Season and the *Twelfth Fortnight*. Currently the weather (forecast at least) is looking [rather good](https://rogerprice.me/july-in-belfast/) and this usually does not bode well for peaceful evenings - we shall see.
