### Key Themes and Arguments

- **AI's Disruption of Labour and Value**: Livingston posits that AI doesn't merely replace jobs but erodes trust, consensus and meaning by commodifying human knowledge and learned skills. What begins as augmentation inevitably concludes as substitution. Not because AI produces objectively superior outputs but because it generates acceptable outputs at fractional costs.

> You unwittingly trained your replacement. Not deliberately, not consciously but inevitably. Every public artefact of your professional existence, every email, every publication, every case study, every presentation, every open source contribution, every blog post became training material for the machine learning subsystems that now simulate your professional function.

- **Philosophical and Spiritual Dimensions**: The book weaves economics, metaphysics, and technology into a narrative that frames the rise of AI as a "metaphysical migration" and a battle between simulation and truth.

> And so, once again, the task of civilisational memory falls to a ***remnant***. Those who refuse the easy path of digital dissolution. Those who build systems anchored in the immutable rather than infinitely manipulatable. The new monks are emerging, not with tonsures and habits but with *private keys* and *proof of work*. Their monasteries are being built, not with stone but with the protocols that cannot be corrupted by the whims of digital emperors.

## 🛠️ Project Updates

This week my focus is on the big picture of what is happening in the Bitcoin space. The network and the asset have proven themselves over the last 15 years. Now it is really going global and this has implications and opportunities.

### The big picture

Lyn Alden’s new piece lays out the full arc of Bitcoin's monetisation path. This includes leverage, ETFs, treasury strategies and how Bitcoin is infiltrating the legacy financial system not despite its cypherpunk roots—but because of them. You will also learn the important difference between *situational money* vs. *ubiquitous money.*

> "In order for something to be used as ubiquitous money, the spender has to already have it, and the recipient has to want to hold it. Notably, people usually want to receive more of what they already have. After all, if a potential recipient wanted to hold it, they likely would have already bought some. So, if a new type of ubiquitous money were to arise, the majority of people would likely first encounter it as an investment."  
> ~ Lyn Alden

You can read the full article here. For those who prefer - Guy will [read it to you](https://podcasts.apple.com/na/podcast/read-891-the-rise-of-bitcoin-stocks-and-bonds/id1359544516?i=1000716815169&ref=rogerprice.me) and and you can follow along.

Some of the key points:

- **Corporate Bitcoin Adoption**: The Bitcoin ecosystem has seen a surge in corporate adoption of Bitcoin as a treasury asset, driven by a 2023 FASB update and market cycles, with companies like Strategy leading the trend.
- **Investor Appeal**: Bitcoin stocks and bonds attract investors due to mandates restricting direct Bitcoin investment, offering indirect exposure through corporate securities.
- **Leverage Advantages**: Corporations leverage Bitcoin more effectively than individuals or hedge funds, using long-term debt like bonds to avoid margin calls and volatility issues faced by leveraged ETFs.
- **Bitcoin as Money**: Bitcoin's journey from a niche asset to a portable store of value is highlighted, emphasising its role as a situational money that is increasingly seen as a sound investment.
- **Network Effects**: Bitcoin's success is tied to its network effects, making it more attractive as a store of value and portable capital, surpassing many fiat currencies in global usability.
- **Corporate Impact**: Corporations do not impair Bitcoin's decentralisation; they contribute to its growth and liquidity, making it more robust and widely accepted.
- **Risks and Opportunities**: While concentration of Bitcoin holdings by corporations poses risks, the network's design and economic incentives mitigate these concerns, offering opportunities for broader adoption.
- **Retail Access**: The proliferation of Bitcoin ETFs and corporate stocks improves retail access, reducing reliance on cryptocurrency exchanges and their associated risks.
- **Bitcoin's Future**: Bitcoin's path includes potential adoption by sovereign entities and as a ubiquitous medium of exchange, driven by its increasing liquidity and decreasing volatility.
- **Bitcoin's Role**: Bitcoin is positioned as a long-term investment and a tool for financial freedom, with ongoing efforts to lower barriers to entry and promote its use globally.

### For balance - a caution to keep in mind

I do agree with Heidi that the resistance/refusal of mainstream finance to use the P*roof-of-reserves* tools that they forced onto the crypto exchanges is a rather big red flag. Lyn also acknowledges that those who do proper *proof-of-reserves* will have *competitive advantage* over time and be *less prone to fraud*.

These tools allow you as a holder to cryptographically verify that your holding is part of the reserve that the custodian claims to have and to validate that the claimed holdings are properly held.

In case it is not obvious to you. Not using these tools enables the (corrupt) entities to say that they hold more bitcoin than they really do. This is also known as "*paper bitcoin*". It it analogous to "*paper gold*" - it is nothing more than traditional banking fraud, an *IOU* and you can be sure that when *SHTF* you will not be among the senior creditors - that is the whole purpose of [*The Great Taking*](https://rogerprice.me/105-the-great-taking/#:~:text=Interview%20of%20the%20week).

In this sense, bitcoin is uniquely valuable since it is [*outside money*](https://rogerprice.me/135-moving-on-again/#:~:text=really%20is%20%22-,outside%20money,-%22%20that%20they) that you can choose to hold it yourself in "*self custody*" and not rely on any third party. ## 🤔 Closing Thoughts

When you take your seat for *Cirque du Soleil*'s longest-running Las Vegas production, *Mystère*, you might think you’re in for a night of spectacular acrobatics and whimsical characters. You are, but you’re also witnessing something far deeper: an initiatory ritual with a story hidden in plain sight. The first clue is in the title itself. "Mystère" is derived from the Greek *mystérion*, meaning a "*secret rite or secret doctrine*".

### Decoding the "Mystère" - thanks to Jason

The show's powerful music isn't just atmospheric gibberish; it's a fabricated language with a hidden meaning known only to the creators and maybe the performers. Every element, from the symbolic Phoenix palms dotting the stage to enigmatic slogans, is intentional. The show is not just a performance; it's a ritual and narrative waiting to be decoded.

The story begins with the *creation myth*. The opening act, with its bungee-cord-bound performers soaring through the air, represents the organisation of chaos into order. This primordial moment introduces the core duality of the human experience through two characters: a *Sun baby* and a *Moon girl*. The Sun baby symbolises the part of humanity that seeks meaning through external authority, dogma and religion. In contrast, the Moon girl represents those who find meaning within—in the raw, beautiful, and chaotic experience of life itself.

This newly formed world is soon visited. The arrival of masked dancers signifies the descent of the *Anuna*, or the "*Watchers*" of ancient lore, into a pre-flood world inhabited by skilled acrobats who represent earthborn humans. But this intervention leads to catastrophe. A planetary cataclysm is depicted through a sudden pole shift, triggered by the arrival of an intruder planet—symbolised by a menacing horned figure perched on its own sphere.

In the aftermath, giants walk the stage, a direct reference to the biblical Nephilim born from the *union of the Watchers and humans*. A seesaw act becomes a powerful symbol for the Earth's violent, wobbling shift on its axis. The show's climax is a grand cosmic resolution: a red world, perhaps the intruder planet Nemesis X, is seen departing the system. This allows for the return of the Sun, represented by a glorious solar chariot. As the old order fades, a gigantic snail slithers across the stage—a perfect symbol for the slow, antediluvian world that was lost to the flood.
