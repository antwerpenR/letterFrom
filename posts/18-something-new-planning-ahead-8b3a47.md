#### 1.  Bitcoin is not "Crypto" they are different things don't mix them up!

**💰 Bitcoin is "******hard digital money******".**  Lyn Alden tells the history of money, and examines this rather unusual period in time where we seem to be going through a gradual global transformation of what we define as money, comparable to the turning points of 1971-present (Petrodollar System), 1944-1971 (Bretton Woods System), the 1700s-1944 (Gold Standard System), and various commodity-money transition periods (pre-1700s).  Read Lyn's full article: [Lyn alden - What is Money](https://www.lynalden.com/what-is-money/?ref=rogerprice.me) - or [see her discussing this with Peter MacCormack](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zzj3qeKoghg&t=153s&ref=rogerprice.me)

🤔 **Intrigued?**  [A Fundamental Analysis Of Bitcoin](https://seekingalpha.com/instablog/52233964-tom-r-h/5603746-fundamental-analysis-of-bitcoin?ref=rogerprice.me) - a good article to get you started. The target audience is people who struggle to see the value behind bitcoin, or those want to learn more about it as an investment.

- Starting with the basics, and a very important question: what is money, really?
- Compare bitcoin's ability to act as money with that of gold and U.S. dollars.
- After examining bitcoin's fundamentals, here are two methodologies that could be useful in determining long-term price targets for bitcoin.

⚠️ **Consider this** from the founding principles of the US.  [The Separation of State and Money](https://t.co/cSqVrI8j8x?ref=rogerprice.me) is the most important thing you can listen to this week.   Thanks to Allen Farrington - his [paper is here](https://medium.com/the-bitcoin-times/the-separation-of-money-and-state-eecedcfe8d69?ref=rogerprice.me) for those who prefer to read.    Wake up, folks!

#### 2.  Only the strong survive - the Celsius, BlockFi debacle was entirely foreseeable

🥷 The *Crypto* environment and "*Defi*" (Decentralised finance) largely replicate the players and (often corrupt) techniques of the traditional banking industry but without constraints.  What could possibly go wrong?

Back last September,  Alan Farrington explained - only the strong survive: [Part 1](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/read-562-only-the-strong-survive-part-1-allen/id1359544516?i=1000536341348&ref=rogerprice.me), [Part 2](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/read-563-only-the-strong-survive-part-2-allen/id1359544516?i=1000536556374&ref=rogerprice.me) and [Part 3](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/read-564-only-the-strong-survive-part-3-allen/id1359544516?i=1000536913249&ref=rogerprice.me) - or [here](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/only-the-strong-survive-full-audio/id1359544516?i=1000537577749&ref=rogerprice.me) if you prefer one uninterrupted read.  [Original paper here](https://www.uncerto.com/only-the-strong-survive?ref=rogerprice.me)

#### 3.  The problems and opportunities are bigger still

🁯  Listen to Guy's latest take on the [Dominoes that are falling](https://overcast.fm/+MYnwsOSgE/4:16?ref=rogerprice.me) and how things will evolve.  No mystery that he should start his story in 1971 when the US broke from the Gold Standard to fund the Vietnam War and all the perpetual wars ever since - recall the [Report from Iron Mountain](https://archive.org/details/pdfy-A5uQx1ByqfwWuHma/page/n5/mode/2up).

In theory Governments should respect [Consent of the Governed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consent_of_the_governed?ref=rogerprice.me) and the 1948 [Universal Declaration of Human Rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Declaration_of_Human_Rights?ref=rogerprice.me) states that "The will of the people shall be the basis of the authority of government".   **For you to decide if and to what extent governments today are acting in line with these principles.**  Can you put your hand on your heart and say that you approve fully of what your government is doing in your name?
