### Monero - under attack

I have told you before why Monero is an important cryptocurrency. It is unique due to its private-by-default transactions that enable privacy of sender, recipient and amount - it is amazing when you think about it. Privacy is a human right and privacy of transactions is essential and another way to opt out and exit the matrix.

Clearly this is not something that the Empire is keen on. The privacy on Monero has never been broken. The latest attack feels like an orchestrated event (just like all the [false flags](https://rogerprice.me/116-around-the-island/#:~:text=Watch%20out%20for%20the%20False%20Flags)) to discredit *Monero* and *proof-of-work*. To be honest, it is rather clever, but attacks like this should fail if enough people mine Monero and this is certainly encouraging people to get going and do it.

Monero mining is intended to be simple for anyone to do - it does not require any specialised hardware - you can run it on any old computer or laptop. [*Gupax.io*](https://gupax.io/?ref=rogerprice.me) is the simplest way to get going. Break out your old laptops and computers - you can be up and running in less than one hour - an easy weekend project.

> [!image]
> [![Absolutely the simplest way to start Monero Mining](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/2025/08/image-29.png)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HiyItDKbEAo&ref=rogerprice.me)
