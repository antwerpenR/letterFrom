Back at Ponta do Sol...

🇵🇹 This week V and I got out and about around the North and East of the Island by jeep and by motorbike trike. You have likely seen photos of motorbike trikes before and you may even have been on one yourself - it's good fun - give it a go if you get the opportunity!

We visited many places that we had not been to yet - including: *Santa Cruz* and *Machico* with its *old for*t at the original landing spot of the Portuguese on the island. From there we went up to the viewpoint at *Pico de Facho* and out to the *Miradouro da Ponta do Rosto* on the very far east of the island and on round along the north of the island via the *steam-powered* sugar-cane *Rum distillery* before heading back via *Portela*.

I also got to go back to *Ponta do Sol* (above). This is the digital nomad centre of the island. Other attractions include being the sunniest beach on the island (admittedly only stones, no sand) and being home of Madeira's famous *Banana museum* - yes, this is the centre of banana production on the island.

### Fedimint revisited

*Fedi* is the second of the two flavours of *Chaumian eCash* - *Cashu* being the other. Fedi aims to be a more industrial strength version that is suitable for worldwide deployment with easy out-of-the-box deployment as opposed to the enthusiast approach of Cashu. Fedi has been dogged by some challenges on the way, not least with the *xyz domain*, however it is good that it now seems to be progressing well.

*Justin* and *Brad* are contributors to the *Fedimint* open source project. This is a great interview with Matt - you will learn a lot. They do not shy away from technical discussion and I do encourage you to participate in the testing if you have the ability and time.
