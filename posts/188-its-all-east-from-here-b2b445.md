## 🛠️ Project Updates

This week I am focusing in on some tools that you ought to know about and will likely find rather useful. What are you doing?

### Vertical tabs in Brave

For a long time I have been aware of people who raved about *Vertical Tabs*, rather than the usual default of horizontal in your browser. I did try it a few times and quickly gave up each time since it was so different and it never really "*clicked*". The last week or so I switched to vertical tabs in *Brave browser* with determination and I am not going back - it really is so much better. Try it and let me know your thoughts.

Some tips: you likely do want to use *Tab Groups* to group tabs that are related and you will probably want to have *separate Windows (*and name them*)* to separate projects and major activities (eg research, projects, watching, one for [Tor](https://www.torproject.org/?ref=rogerprice.me) etc). Right click on any tab to add it to a *Tab Group* or move it to another *Window*. On Mac *CTRL-down* or CMD-` (the back-tick, under the ESC key) lets you quickly switch between windows - on Linux use *ALT-tab*.

### Downie, Grayjay and yt-dlp

You likely have noticed how YouTube is becoming ever more aggressive in enforcing the Empire agenda on you and feeding you propaganda via enforced "ads". You can eliminate all the noise by using [Brave Browser](https://brave.com/?ref=rogerprice.me) with [uBlock Origin](https://ublockorigin.com/?ref=rogerprice.me) extension installed - just do it.

After that you can avoid the algorithm by following your trusted researchers via *RSS feeds* or using *GrayJay*. You may well also want to safestore copies of useful and interesting material for future reference given how things tend to disappear and get modified online.

[**yt-dlp**](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp?ref=rogerprice.me): this is a feature-rich command-line audio/video downloader with support for [thousands of sites](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp/blob/master/supportedsites.md?ref=rogerprice.me). The project is a fork of the venerable [youtube-dl](https://github.com/ytdl-org/youtube-dl?ref=rogerprice.me). It is frequently updated to deal with the latest tricks of the Evil Empire.

[**Grayjay**](https://grayjay.app/?ref=rogerprice.me): Grayjay enables you to create and watch video content in your own terms, fully retaining ownership and having control over what you watch. Your content on your terms. Another fabulous open source offering from Futo - do donate to support them.

[**Downie**](https://software.charliemonroe.net/downie/?ref=rogerprice.me): Ever wished you could save a video from the Internet? Search no more, Downie is what you're looking for. Easily download videos from thousands of different sites. This is a paid app but it has a 14 day free trial to make sure it is right for you and it does have support for any issues you may encounter.

You likely do want to have all of these in your armoury. They all come in useful at different times in different circumstances. When one fails for you, one of the others will likely get the job done.
