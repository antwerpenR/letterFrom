### Picture in Picture

This is yet another great feature that you can use with *Brave Browser*. When watching a YouTube video you may well want to minimise the image so that you can keep it playing but allowing you to work in other windows while still being able to see it. This is straightforward and once you know how you will wonder how you lived without it!

- Using YouTube's context menu (works when the PiP button isn’t visible):
  - Right‑click the video twice (first right‑click opens YouTube’s custom menu, second opens the browser menu).
  - Choose `Picture in picture` from the browser menu.
