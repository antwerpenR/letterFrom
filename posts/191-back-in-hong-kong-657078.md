🇭🇰 Over the weekend V and I travelled back home to Hong Kong. Travel was fairly smooth and we arrived just in time to enjoy the cooler weather from *severe tropical storm* Fengshan. It passed well south of HK and everybody here was loving the [cooler weather](https://rogerprice.me/tropical-storm-fengshan/) it brought but things will be back to normal by the weekend.

We are staying in Wan Chai and we wasted no time getting back into our Hong Kong routine with hikes to the peak and meeting up with friends here. Thanks to one of my subscribers for a great evening at the Foreign Correspondents Club (FCC). It's been a good nine months since we last met up so we had plenty to catch up on - we are both certainly *older and wiser* now!

We had a good laugh to discover that we both had had to install the local *IamSmart* app that day to deal with local government services - recall that there are no coincidences. Be in no doubt that this is the *Mark of the Beast*. The empire is rolling this technology out worldwide in every country - each with slightly different flavours but the goal is the same - totalitarian control.

Of course V and I went to the horse races at *Happy Valley*. The weather was not the usual balmy evening you expect in HK in October and V's skill at picking the winners needs a bit of practice. I expect we'll be there next week for the Halloween celebrations - maybe see you there?

During the week I did a hike from *Central* to *the Peak* and back down again - nice in the cooler weather. I also did a morning hike from *Sok Kwu Wan* to  *-* via *Lo So Shing* beach - this is one of my favourite short hikes and it is great to finish up with breakfast by the pier.

I'll be out hiking over the weekend. If you are in HK or will be here in the weeks ahead do reach out and say "*hello"* - happy to catch up.
