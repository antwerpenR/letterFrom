At the waterfront

🇭🇰 This week, V and I have been *breaking pattern*. We are up in Sai Kung in the North East of Hong Kong - a popular area for holiday, hiking and all sorts of water-sports. The weather was [often cloudy and not too hot](https://rogerprice.me/early-november-in-sai-kung/) - almost perfect in fact!

We used the week to relax and enjoy life - disconnecting from all the nonsense going on in the world - [*breaking pattern*](https://rogerprice.me/141-the-next-stage-begins/#:~:text=other%20current%20events.-,Breaking%20Pattern,-Somehow%20this%20video) indeed. There are plenty of walking trails around Sai Kung and it is really nice to just enjoy them - eg down the peninsula to Trio Beach. On that one we were accompanied by a school outing with dozens of kids running around heading to the beach in packs of 5-20; this is very typical of Hong Kong.

We also made a fabulous boat tour around the islands off Sai Kung. This took us up to the [HK Geopark](https://www.geopark.gov.hk/en/discover/attractions/high-island-reservoir-east-dam?ref=rogerprice.me#:~:text=Route%20in%20PDF-,Photos%20Gallery,-Nearby%20Geopark%20Attractions). I grew up beside the Giant's Causeway and for years I believed it was the best global example of hexagonal rock columns. Well the Geopark is at least an order of magnitude bigger in every dimension - well worth a visit if you are ever in Hong Kong. The world is a big place and you have to go to places to see what it is really like.
