**Five Key Points from the Book:**

1. **Reject the Default Path** – College/University is no longer a guaranteed ticket to success. *The Preparation* dismantles the myths around higher education and shows you how to create a life of opportunity without drowning in debt.
2. **Become Competent, Confident, and Dangerous** – This is more than self-defence; it’s about becoming a person who can think independently, act decisively, and adapt to any challenge.
3. **The 16 Cycle System** – Sixteen three-month programs—ranging from EMT training to sailing near Cape Horn—each designed to deliver immersive, hands-on skills while building a portfolio of achievements that compound over time. 16 “anchor” courses combined with academic courses taught by some of the best professors in the world will leave anyone miles ahead of their peers.
4. **Important Lessons and Real-World Economics** – Learn what actually matters: sound money, entrepreneurship, history and the ability to recognise and seize opportunities—taught by people who have lived it.
5. **Adventure and a Global Network** – Instead of four years in a lecture hall, you’ll spend them in the mountains, on ranches, in the cockpit of a plane, on the deck of a ship—meeting exceptional people and stacking valuable experiences.

### Privacy is taking centre stage

People are beginning to realise how important privacy really is when it comes to your personal payments and mobile device usage. All of this is under attack from *Government overreach* and *Captured Corporate fascism*.

Juraj is a self-described “shaman” of the crypto world who bridges many blockchain communities. In this discussion he dives into the contrasts and common ground between *Monero* and *Zcash*, exploring both the technical and cultural dynamics of the leading privacy coins. This is one of the most comprehensive and well-balanced discussions on these topics that you will find.

Juraj also discusses Zcash’s growing DEX integrations like Zashi Wallet and Near Intents. They compare this with Monero’s ongoing DEX efforts like Sarai and potential Thorchain integration, highlighting the shared goal of improving usability and interoperability.

The two also discuss each project’s funding models, mining approaches, and community cultures, debating decentralisation, security, and the future of privacy in crypto. Juraj emphasises that real adoption often depends more on social dynamics than technical merit, and that long-term holders give privacy coins their true value.

### What a brilliant idea - fighting back against the Empire
