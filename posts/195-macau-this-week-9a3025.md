Senado Square - Macau

🇭🇰 🇲🇴 We are really making the most of our stay in Hong Kong. In addition to doing all the usual things this week, V and I broke pattern again - we did a trip over to Macau. Since we had such a good time in Madeira, why not visit its twin over here in the Far East!

Macau is about an hour from Hong Kong on the high speed ferry. You have many options to get there including bus, car and helicopter. We opted for the ferry - nice and relaxed and they will include transport in Macau if you want! We had a fabulous time - and we got upgraded to the VIP suite!

On arrival we skipped the free shuttle and walked into town visiting quite a few interesting sights on the way, including [Sun Yat Sen house](https://kagi.com/images?q=sun+yat+sen+house+macau&ref=rogerprice.me) and the new [Guia pedestrian tunnel](https://kagi.com/images?q=guia+pedestrian+tunnel+macau&ref=rogerprice.me) as well as [Lou Lim Ioc](https://kagi.com/images?q=lou+lim+iog+garden&ref=rogerprice.me) garden. Once in town we wandered around all the normal sights including [Ruins of St Paul](https://kagi.com/images?q=ruins+of+st+paul&ref=rogerprice.me) and around [Senado](https://kagi.com/images?q=Senado+Square&ref=rogerprice.me) but also quite a few other places we had not been before. From there we took the new MRT down to [Cotai](https://kagi.com/images?q=cotai+casino&ref=rogerprice.me) and did a tour of all of the major hotels and casinos before our show - the [*Dancing Waters*](https://www.cityofdreamsmacau.com/en/house-of-dancing-water?ref=rogerprice.me) at City of dreams.

Of course we also got to Happy Valley Horse races. We had another great evening there. We've had a cold snap during the week with temperatures dipping to [12 degrees C](https://rogerprice.me/cold-snap-in-november/) - that's about as cold as it gets here in HK Central. Luckily it is back up again and it's looking good for the weekend.
