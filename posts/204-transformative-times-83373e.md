### Messengers

You really should want to **avoid** *Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp, Telegram*, and similar mainstream apps for **privacy and security reasons**. These run as centralised services with data collection and data mining and your discussions are not confidential - they will be shared with third parties outside your control.

[**Signal Messenger**](https://signal.org/?ref=rogerprice.me). Start here. This is the easiest one to get started with and likely some of your friends will already use it (and wish you would too!). It is an open-source, end-to-end encrypted messaging service offering *instant messaging*, *voice calls*, and *video calls* across *multiple platforms* including *Windoze*, *macOS*, *Linux*, *iOS*, and *Android*. It provides features like *group chats*, *file sharing*, *disappearing messages*, *screen sharing*, and *story sharing*, all while using your phone number as an identifier.

### Social Media and Information sharing

Traditional Social Media (*Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and Twitter (X)* etc) has become a real cesspit of moral corruption in recent years. In large part this is due to the algorithmic feeds being optimised for reactive engagement.

There are valid reasons for wanting data and information sharing like these platforms but without all the toxicity. That is where Nostr steps in. Nostr is an alternative platform that has been built up over the last three years or so. It supports community building and unstoppable information sharing.

Core Advantages of Nostr:

- **No email or phone number needed to use it**  
  No one (not even a government or mega-corp) has any way to take your account away from you. You can easily migrate to any client or app instantly by importing your key.
- **Your content is backed up and distributed worldwide**  
  Content lives on many independent relays (servers) that anyone can run. If one relay blocks you or your posts, you switch to (or add) others. No single company or moderator team can de-platform you globally.
- **Simple chronological feed from people important to you**  
  No doomscroll-optimised feed designed to keep you addicted and angry. You can add algorithmic feeds if you want them, but they're not the default and not controlled by a single entity trying to maximise ad revenue.
- **Integrated payments (monetisation using Bitcoin)**  
  Most clients support Lightning Network "zaps". These are small Bitcoin payments you can send/receive directly in replies or on posts. This creates real economic signals and creator monetisation without middlemen taking 30% cuts or freezing payouts.
- **Use whatever client you like best and easy switching**  
  Pick from Damus, Primal, Amethyst, Snort, Coracle and many more. Your followers see your content regardless of what client they use. If you hate one app's UI or moderation policy, switch without losing your audience or history.

I recommend [Primal](https://primal.net/Travellr?ref=rogerprice.me) as the easiest client to get started with. Just go to the web-page or download it on your phone/tablet and give it a go. You can [follow me here](https://rogerprice.me/contact/#:~:text=You%20can%20also%20find%20me%20on%20Nostr:).
