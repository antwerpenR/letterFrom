🇹🇭 Here in Thailand there are three seasons in the year. Officially called *Cool Season* (November to February), *Hot Season* (March to May) and *Rainy Season* (June to October). Locals refer to them as *Hot, Very Hot* and *Hot and Wet*. Since 22 February we are [now in the Summer](https://www.bangkokpost.com/thailand/general/3201065/summer-season-starts-on-feb-22?ref=rogerprice.me) (Very Hot season) and it is expected to be hot indeed this year. You can already feel it warming up. Our daily morning swim is rather necessary.

There were two Bitcoin meetups this week - the usual *Satoshi Square* on Monday and *Bob Space Market* on Friday. Both were great events with many interesting folks from around the world. Bob Space Market has local people selling all sorts of goods and food for bitcoin. Bob Space also delivers Bitcoin education in Thai language. Needless to say, everyone is excited that Bitcoin is *currently on-sale* and now is certainly an interesting time to *Dollar-Cost Average* and *acquire more*.

I have been quite busy this week with hands-on project work. This includes server updates and reconfiguration as well as some new pieces of *Sovereign Computing* infrastructure. I'll be sharing details in *Project Updates* going forward. I also have a couple of books on the go at the moment and you'll be getting reviews of those too shortly.

Apart from that I had a great outing with the group on Saturday exploring *Saen Saep canal* and the area around *Khao San Road*. V and I have been out and about exploring some new spots in Bangkok (photos below). Of course we also went to the *flower market* and this Saturday we will have our *Valentine's day* dinner, now that the crowds have dissipated.

If you don't have a Nostr account yet, create one at [*Primal.net.*](https://primal.net/home?ref=rogerprice.me) Here are the main Nostr-based alternatives focused on music streaming, sharing and live audio as of early 2026 - Give them a go.

- Wavlake ([*wavlake.com*](https://wavlake.com/?ref=rogerprice.me))  
  A prominent music platform built on Nostr. Artists upload tracks, fans stream them, and support via zaps. It integrates deeply with Nostr for sharing songs, playlists, and value-for-value (V4V) payments. Musicians earn Bitcoin directly from streams/listens. It's often highlighted as a decentralized alternative for indie artists.
- Zap.stream ([*zap.stream*](https://zap.stream/?ref=rogerprice.me))  
  Primarily known for live video streaming on Nostr, but heavily used for music performances, DJ sets, live concerts, and audio-focused broadcasts. Streamers (including musicians) go live, chat with viewers, and receive instant zaps. Many music creators experiment here for its low barriers and direct monetization.
- Tunestr [*(tunestr.io*](https://tunestr.io/?ref=rogerprice.me))  
  A specialized platform for live music performances and streaming on Nostr. It emphasizes paid/live musical experiences with Lightning payments integrated (e.g., via Alby Hub for seamless tipping).
- [*Fountain.fm*](https://fountain.fm/?ref=rogerprice.me)  
  A podcast and music app powered by RSS, Lightning, and Nostr. It supports emerging artists, value-for-value boosts/zaps, and podcast-style music sharing. Artists retain ownership and fan relationships without platform lock-in.
- ZapTrax ([*zaptrax.app*](https://zaptrax.app/?ref=rogerprice.me))  
  A Nostr music player with Wavlake integration. It lets you discover, stream tracks, create playlists, like songs, and zap artists directly.
