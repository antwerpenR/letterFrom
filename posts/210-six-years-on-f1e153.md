### Beyond Cashu - Numo and more

Marty does one of his regular check-in's with Calle. They cover how Artificial Intelligence (AI), is changing things really fast, especially for software developers. Calle, who works with Bitcoin, explains that AI has totally changed how he programs. He sees AI developing in three stages:

- Level 1 was using AI tools like ChatGPT in a web browser, where you had to copy and paste code.
- Level 2 is AI coding assistants like GitHub Copilot that can directly help write code on your computer, making people much faster at their jobs.
- Level 3, which is happening now, involves personal AI agents that are always on, remember things, and can do tasks for you. Calle thinks these Level 3 agents will become common very quickly.
