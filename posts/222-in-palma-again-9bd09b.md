### Open source, Linux Self Hosting

For years I have been recommending and practising steps to get out of Big Tech. Steps that I have taken include:

- getting photos out of Google into self-hosted *Immich*
- getting email out of gMail into alternatives like *ProtonMail* and *Fastmail*
- using Linux laptop as daily driver -*MacOS* is not great, *Windoze* must be avoided at all costs

Well, Foci (a Scandinavian student and researcher) has taken things to the next level. He really has embraced the change and you will be amazed at what he has managed to do in the last 6 months.

> [!image]
> [![Click to view](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/2026/05/image-28.png)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DXH7yLFN0yI&ref=rogerprice.me)

He certainly gives inspiration for what you can do and I will be trying some of these possibilities going forward - how about you?

I am really pleased with my Immich migration. I now have easy access to all my photos - over 500,000 of them and they are no longer accessible to Google. I have a couple of full instances running locally in different locations and one that has only recent years (and select albums) running in the cloud.

With email it is impractical to host an email server yourself - you really have to use a hosting service of some sort. There are secure options that do not scan your mail and you will benefit from getting out of Google. Take your time, go step by step and ensure that you have a complete and stable system for several months before deleting gMail.

For both Photos and gMail you should always keep your Google Takeout file(s). In doing so you always have a backup of what Google had before you deleted it.
