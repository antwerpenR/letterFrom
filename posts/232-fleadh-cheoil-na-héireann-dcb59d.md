### Landline Phone

Do you still have a landline phone? You might have forgotten about it and still be paying for it. First thing is to check. You likely can just eliminate it and then be sure you are not paying for it via some other bundled subscription.

Should you wish to keep it (in the UK), just ring BT support and demand the "Line Only" price. £13.09 per month is the best you can get. All incoming calls are free. If you need to do outgoing calls, use your mobile phone (see below).

### Mobile Phone

Likely you are locked into some long-duration contract here too. If not, they will already be charging you a premium for being "out-of-contract", but (joy and happiness) you are then free to look around and switch. Under UK law you do not even need to call your old provider - your new provider will do everything.

1. **Text `INFO` to `85075`** to check your contract status and any exit fees
2. **Check coverage** at your home/work postcode on [Ofcom's coverage checker](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/phones-and-broadband/coverage?ref=rogerprice.me) or your preferred provider's site
3. **Pick a deal** from the table for your location
4. **Text `PAC` to `65075`** to get your switching code (to keep your phone number)
5. **Sign up** with your new provider and give them the PAC code
6. **Done** — the switch completes within one working day and your old account closes automatically

Last, but not least - do not be tempted to buy the latest new computer. For whatever you are doing you likely can do this very well on a second-hand or refurbished computer running Linux.
