Gabriel Custodiet, host of the *Escape the Technocracy* podcast and author of the *Watchman's Torch* premium newsletter, *argues* *against the broad use of cryptocurrency hardware wallets*.

**First**, hardware wallets complicate fund access — transactions require both the device and a software wallet, so if your signer is stored remotely, you cannot move funds quickly. This discourages actually using cryptocurrency.

**Second**, the devices create a physical security target: their appearance signals crypto wealth, and shipping data breaches (like Trezor's in August 2026, affecting customers across seven countries) expose buyers to home-invasion risks.

**Third**, they are over-marketed to under-educated users who never learn fundamental crypto skills, breeding incompetence.
