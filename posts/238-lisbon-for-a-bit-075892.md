Lisbon this week - we had a real holiday week!

🇵🇹 V and I are taking a few days *R&R* down here in Portugal. We are staying in Lisbon and exploring the city and surroundings. The weather has been fantastic all week and we have been out and about every day, making the most of our time here.

We travelled down on 9/11, so we got to enjoy quieter airports. That said, we had the ***worst check-in experience ever***. *TAP Air Portugal - absolutely dreadful* service and they should be avoided in future - read [more below](#check-in-chaos-when-paid-extras-dont-follow-you-to-your-new-flight):

Apart from that Lisbon has been excellent for us. This is the first week that we have really had to ourselves for months - since we left Bangkok, in fact. We had an excellent apartment with superb east-facing terrace and handy bus connection to the center. We are also close to the Metro and Trains and there is a supermarket opposite!

V got us Lisboa Cards for a couple of days - these give you free access to many of the major locations, discounts for many more and free transport for 24 hours. For the other days we used the Navegante cards - prepaid for trams, trains and buses. Do make use of the excellent Lisboa Card [documentation book](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1tVnk8u0tN68_agKLV1XV3FH4PtIBRphQ/view?ref=rogerprice.me) - it is a comprehensive explainer of everything that is included free and discounted - you can plan your trip optimally (one of V's superpowers).

A few places that we recommend visiting include: The *S Jorge Castle* on the hill, the *Decorative Arts* museum, *Sao Vicente monastery*, the *National Pantheon* and the *Flea Market* where we had a superb Mushroom meal at *Santa Clara dos Columeros*! Subsequent days took us to *Belem Tower* and the J*eronimo monastery*, not to mention many superb parks with their original trees, shady seats and usually a lake with wild birds.

Beyond that we got to do a trip along the coast here to *Estoril* and *Cascais*. We went along to check these out as possible locations for a future stay and also to meet up with a former colleague who lives here - we last met up in *Bangk0k* and before that in *Hong Kong* - nice!

##### **Check-in Chaos: When Paid Extras Don't Follow You to Your New Flight**

As you may recall, we pushed our flight back from 1 to 11 September so we could spend more time with V's mum. V had booked a few extras with TAP Air Portugal (we do treat ourselves to some little luxuries now and again) — extra luggage, plus premium services: priority check-in and fast-track-security as well as lounge access.

The trouble surfaced the night before, when V checked in using the *TAP app* and found that our paid extras had not made the move to the new flight. She had to pay all over again — and even then, the app refused to issue boarding cards, insisting we check in at the airport.

We arrived early and were first in the premium queue, handled on TAP's behalf by Transavia. They refused to check us in, saying there was no trace of our luggage in their system — and even when we showed them the TAP website proving we'd paid twice for our suitcases they wouldn't discuss it further!

After a thoroughly frustrating 45 minutes on the phone, V persuaded TAP support to email us the "*EMD numbers*" for the suitcases. Armed with those, we convinced the Transavia agent to enter them manually, and at last we had our boarding passes. With all the delay we did not get to enjoy the lounge that we had paid for.

We made the flight — but only thanks to V's persistence and after paying twice for luggage we'd already covered. ***The lesson learned***: if you change a flight, don't assume your paid extras travel with it - so add extra items only when doing the final check-in.

> We have issued a formal complaint and will pursue this all the way to get our money back as a minimum. Avoid TAP Air Portugal if possible.

My own introduction to this genre came last year through [***Manacled***](https://annas-archive.pk/md5/673f7036d46e5b9f9447cf80293b4d11?ref=rogerprice.me). I have to say that all the warnings that Ali gives about the content are correct and even understate things quite a bit. Reading this really is an eye-opener on many fronts and will make you think deeply about many things.

The story is set in an ***alternate universe where Voldemort won the war**.* ***Hermione Granger*** is captured by the *Death Eaters* and forced into slavery under a *re-population mandate.* She is assigned to ***Draco Malfoy***, now the *High Reeve,* who uses her as *a breeding tool*. The narrative explores themes of power, control, survival, and *psychological trauma* as Hermione harbours an Order secret buried deep in her mind that the *Death Eaters* are determined to extract.

The book became so popular that it prompted the author *SenLinYu* to decide to take it down and make a new book that she could commercialise. As Ali explains the *Fan Fiction* must be distributed *Free of Charge* and *cannot be commercialised*.

So [*Manacled*](https://annas-archive.pk/md5/673f7036d46e5b9f9447cf80293b4d11?ref=rogerprice.me) was removed from AO3 (Archive Of Our Own) and other sites ahead of the novel's release in 2024. Fans were given advance notice to download and preserve copies for personal use - if you know where to look, you can still find the original - hint ([Anna's Archive](https://annas-archive.pk/md5/673f7036d46e5b9f9447cf80293b4d11?ref=rogerprice.me) is a good place to start for things like this).

In *Alchemised*, *Hermione Granger* and *Draco Malfoy* became ***Helena Marino* and *Kaine Ferron***, with entirely new world-building built around *necromancy* and *alchemy* rather than the *Potter-verse.*

### Sparrow and Frigate for Silent Payments

First up I am highlighting this interview of Craig Raw by Gabriel. Craig is the creator behind [Sparrow Wallet](https://sparrowwallet.com/?ref=rogerprice.me) which has got to be one of the top open source bitcoin wallets. Note it is only available on [Mac and Linux](https://sparrowwallet.com/?ref=rogerprice.me) (and even *Windoze*) - **NB: *there is no phone-based version*** - so do not be fooled.

In this interview they discuss how Craig has tackled the problem of making truly anonymous bitcoin transactions using what looks like a fixed Bitcoin Address. This is a fascinating story, how he built on the existing capability of Silent Payment addresses to bring about a *10x performance improvement*, followed by an *additional 10x improvement*. After all of that he worked with another talented developer to get *10x performance beyond that*. With *1000x the performance* of the initial implementation, this has reached the level of scaling needed for general public use.
