## How is your "To Do" list progressing?

I have recently been helping some folks get on top of their "To Do" lists. In doing so I usually share a couple of things that I have found to be most helpful over the years.

First up, of course, is [*The Seven Habits of Highly Successful People*](https://rogerprice.me/choices-you-de/#the-7-habits-of-highly-effective-people). For me, this is the foundation that you must be aware of and build on. *By default, everyone does the opposite of each of the 7-Habits!* This is remarkable but true.

Next is to stay on top of your daily and weekly tasks and activities. For me, a good paper list works best and I'm happy to share an interesting technique that you may well want to try - [The Resistance Zero System](http://markforster.squarespace.com/blog/2022/6/14/resistance-how-to-make-the-most-of-it-the-resistance-zero-sy.html?ref=rogerprice.me). Ben explains it in his video below with some real life examples:

> [!image]
> [![Click for Ben's very practical guide to this useful system](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/2025/07/image-6.png)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tsgj1\_OwhPs&ref=rogerprice.me)
> Click for Ben's very practical guide to this useful system

This really does work extremely well. Essential tips are:  
- review and progress your list daily, *starting from the bottom of the list*  
- items that have been static for too long likely need closing by re-phrasing and splitting  
- when you near 3/4 page with closed items it's good to copy open items to a new page and continue there. This may also prompt you to rephrase and split some items.

Have you tried this? What are your tips and tricks?

### The Great Hackening

2026 has seen a wave of cyberattacks targeting Bitcoin infrastructure. Like Guy, I see this as a transformative stress test for the network's long-term security. Two incidents in particular stand out - somewhat reminiscent of *Celsius* and *FTX* in scope and impact.

- Coldcard devices experienced a critical entropy vulnerability in the firmware protection mode, where a faulty check caused seed generation to fall back to a weak pseudo-random number generator; you must move to an alternative setup - Coldcard has lost trust.
- The Liquid Network by Blockstream suffered a 4,200 BTC drain due to a key cache bug in confidential transactions, with the attacker returning most funds in a grey hat display of rational opportunism.

### Self Custody options and Multisig

As we have discussed before, you do need to plan for the value of your assets (in fiat terms) to increase significantly and you will likely want to re-evaluate your setup and custody options - considering all risks.

Mike Belshe (CEO of Bitgo) emphasizes that balancing **self-custody** and **custodial** services is essential for security as holdings grow. The BitGo model utilizes a **multisig** (2-of-3) framework to mitigate risks of theft and loss without relying on a single point of failure.

This is a most interesting discussion. I am not endorsing his company and he is not pushing his company's services, rather he describes rather clearly options that you likely want to consider.
