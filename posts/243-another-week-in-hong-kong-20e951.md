🇭🇰 We have had a really busy week here. After our long trip we have lots to do and get sorted - we are working through our list. In addition to that we have been out and about, enjoying the best season of the year in HK.

Many days I have hiked Central to The Peak and back - you are welcome to join me any day, just say. We have also been *island hopping* with trips to *Lamma* and *Cheung Chau*. The *Surf Club* was unusually closed but we had a great lunch at *J&J*'s, the pink place around the beach and they have an even better view of the beach.

I have also been getting out hiking with the groups. These are fun because the route is planned and you just need to turn up and enjoy yourself. You will always meet interesting people and I had fascinating conversations with people from UK, Germany and Philippines in addition to many from HK. Among other things we were discussing and sharing our recommended book reads. Obviously these included Animal Farm, Brave New World and 1984, all of which you should have read. Most were unaware of my strong recommendation for The Mandibles, by Lionel Shriver.

"[*The Mandibles*](https://rogerprice.me/getting-feet-on-the-pedals/#:~:text=Mandibles%20%2D%20The%20plot,by%20runaway%20inflation.)" claims to be 100% fiction but every word feels like right now - or imminent - and even more so with all the nonsense that Trump and USA/Israel have been pushing recently. The Mandibles family once expected a huge inheritance from their 97-year-old patriarch Douglas Mandible; when the dollar is abruptly devalued after all the other countries become so fed up with USA behaviour that they club together and replace it with an international digital currency (the "Bancor"), that fortune evaporates overnight.

### Remember 2019?

2019 was defined by **mass protests worldwide, major disasters, and the first documented cases of what would become the global pandemic.** Key events include:

- **First known human case of COVID-19** was documented in Wuhan, China, preceding the pandemic declaration.
- **Hong Kong protests** erupted over a proposed extradition bill, escalating into month-long clashes with police and anti-establishment mobilization.
- **Notre-Dame Cathedral fire** in Paris consumed the 850-year-old landmark.
- **Christchurch mosque shootings** in New Zealand killed 51 people, triggering a global debate on extremism.
- **US – Donald Trump** became the third US president to be impeached.
- **Brexit deadlock** continued to dominate UK and European politics.
- **Climate activism** surged, with global strikes and growing public pressure on governments.

The year was broadly characterized by widespread street protests (Hong Kong, Chile, Iraq, Sudan, France) and a sense of geopolitical upheaval heading into the 2020s.
