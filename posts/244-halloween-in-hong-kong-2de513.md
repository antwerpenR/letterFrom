Kowloon-side - Sham Shui Po and Mong Kok

🇭🇰 We have really settled back into our routine in HK. I have been out hiking and island hopping most days and we usually end up at Happy Valley on Wednesday evening - V has a preferred spot and we get there early to grab it!

I did go over to Kowloon for Camera and Computer shopping. This was AI-assisted, meaning that (for the camera) I told AI the type of photos I take and gave it a few constraints and it picked the best models for me to check out. It also told me suggested shopping areas (Apliu Street and Sim City) as well as prices and negotiation techniques since I am buying second hand. For the computer I had specific configurations in mind but the challenge comes when you see other models alongside and you need to compare pros and cons; AI is fantastic for this.

For the camera the recommended model was the *Lumix LX100*. I have had Panasonic cameras before and this seems attractive. Unfortunately the 2nd hand market in HK has been pushed up by significant buying pressure from the northern neighbours, so the choice was less than I had hoped for. There were two in Apliu Street but both turned out to have some faults that I was not happy with and the one in Sim City is actually the *Leica version*, so it comes with a correspondingly higher price tag. Further AI discussion suggested that a good alternative would be *Olympus* OM-D E-M10which also hits most of my requirements - so that's what I got. More on this in coming weeks.

As for the computer I wanted another *X1 Carbon 16GB* and *Gen 9* preferred. These were harder to find than I had expected - I did find several in the street behind the *Golden Computer Mall*. I also found one on Carousell that looks a better deal - to be continued.

Our Wednesday trip to the races fell through. Instead of Happy Valley, the races this week were in Sha Tin, way up in the New Territories! V forgot to check and our planned meeting with friends got shifted to the Royal Hong Kong Yacht Club instead. Had a fabulous evening and lots of interesting discussion on many topics. I also got to visit some rather nice yachts.

### Bitchat - grab it before you need it

Traditional messaging apps (*Signal*, *WhatsApp* etc) depend on centralized infrastructure that can be monitored, censored or disabled. *Bitchat* creates ad-hoc communication networks using only the devices present in physical proximity. Each device acts as both client and server, automatically discovering peers and relaying messages across multiple hops to extend the network's reach.

This approach provides censorship resistance, surveillance resistance, and infrastructure independence. The network remains functional during internet outages, natural disasters and in locations with limited power and connectivity.
