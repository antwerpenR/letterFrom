🇭🇰 🇲🇴 Another busy week here in Hong Kong. We are still working through all the things that we need to get done after an extended trip away. Every year we like to make at least one trip to Macau and V had that planned in for last weekend.

We had a most enjoyable time as tourists in Macau town and around the island. Macau has a very different feel to Hong Kong. It also has a lot of Portuguese links and we noticed even more of these after our recent stay in Lisbon.

V booked us to see two shows while there. Macau is famous for the enormous hotels and casinos with extravagant shows to watch. First up was Le Sseraphim in Galaxy Arena. This was a Korean Girl K-Pop group, Macau being the first part of the Asia leg of their world tour. Capacity at the venue was 16,000 people - mostly over-excited girls, replete with all the requisite merchandise, photographs and light sticks. Hardly very much culture but it was an experience!

After that we went back-stage after the City of Dreams, Dancing Waters show. This was much more interesting - some photos below. CoD is a purpose-built arena seating 2,000 people per show with 2 shows per day, 5 days per week. The show is spectacular (see [here](https://youtu.be/Ql7mmzwAWmU?si=v9IGPA8uMapnqmtu&ref=rogerprice.me)). Backstage is most interesting. We got to see how the stunts are done and how all the props and stage equipment work. If you go to the show you should go backstage too!

Apart from all that we had the first mornings with temperature below 20 degrees here. The locals were suddenly out in force in anoraks and coats. We found the weather to be really rather pleasant - it brought clear blue skies and nice sunshine that was not too hot or humid - rather like a perfect day in European summer!
