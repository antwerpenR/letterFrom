The last **Symphony of Lights - 幻彩詠香江 -** ending after 22 years

🇭🇰 We have spent this week in and around Hong Kong, doing our normal routine. I was delighted to meet up with visitors from abroad as well as a former colleague with whom I worked here for many years. Tomorrow, Saturday, I will be on the [hike to Tai To Yan 大刀屻](https://www.meetup.com/eye-opener-hk-hiking-%e9%96%8b%e7%9c%bc%e7%95%8c%e8%a1%8c%e5%b1%b1%e9%9a%8a/events/317337122/?slug=eye-opener-hk-hiking-%E9%96%8B%E7%9C%BC%E7%95%8C%E8%A1%8C%E5%B1%B1%E9%9A%8A&eventId=317337122&ref=rogerprice.me) - looking forward to meeting many friends there and you are welcome to join.

We did all the usual things this week, including lunch at the Windsurfing Club and J&J at the beach. Christmas decorations are appearing now around town and in most of the shopping malls - we have yet to hear *Jingle Bells* but that will surely come! There is something big in *Chater Garden* that they have been working on for weeks - seems it opens this week along with the one at TST and another in Causeway Bay. I'll likely share photos of those next week.

On Wednesday we skipped our usual trip to *Happy Valley* and instead walked down to the *Golden Bauhinia Square promenade* in Wan Chai to watch the last *"Symphony of Lights" - 幻彩詠香江* at 8pm*.* It ends after 22 years, to be replaced (apparently) with new light programmes at different districts and times of the year, starting progressively in 2027. The final performance included some *rooftop pyrotechnics* as a farewell.

I had lots of time this week to listen and read. These are certainly *Interesting Times* so I encourage you to listen to this week's [interview](#Interview-of-the-week) that explains a lot. On the [project side](#🛠️-project-updates) I did a deep dive into *Omarchy* - you will understand why I think "*it's time*". For the [*Useful Links*](#🔗-links-for-your-edification), I have picked three that explain the big things in the news, why, what next and how best to navigate what's coming. Lastly, in [*Closing Thoughts*,](#🤔-closing-thoughts) you have Jason's best-yet delivery of how and when this current cycle commenced and what will happen from now till the next one begins.

### Remember Yalta?

You may or may not recall the **February 1945 conference at Yalta** in Crimea, where *Roosevelt, Churchill and Stalin met* near the end of the Second World War *and laid out the shape of post-war Europe* - occupation zones, Poland's borders, the framework of the United Nations, and a Soviet sphere across Eastern Europe.

Yalta later became "shorthand for great-power deals imposed on smaller countries". So when commentators warn about "Yalta 2.0" or "Shenzhen 26," the claim is not that a treaty will be signed - it's that three men will be photographed deciding the fate of Ukraine, Taiwan, Europe and everyone else without those parties in the room.

Two ironies sharpen the image.

- Yalta is now **inside territory Russia annexed in 2014**, so the venue of the original "settlement" is itself a disputed conquest.
- And China, which hosts the sequel, **was not invited in 1945** - the secret Far Eastern protocol cut China out of decisions about Outer Mongolia, Port Arthur and Manchurian railways, which is a live grievance in Chinese nationalist memory.
