🇲🇹 This week we have changed into a different gear and have been relaxing and exploring Malta - going around to see the sights and experience the place. It does have special vibes, quite unlike other places we have been to.

🌡 For one thing, it is really nice to be back in a warm climate. We have missed this so much since we left HK back in February. The weather here is 20-30 degrees every day, sunny and not humid - that'll do nicely. [Walkabout here](https://youtu.be/x44tE8mHY2Y?ref=rogerprice.me)

3️⃣ We visited [Three Cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Cities?ref=rogerprice.me) and took an electric "buggy" for the day. This was fun and enjoyable if a little frustrating in that that the top speed was really limited to 20km/h! The buggy has an onboard computer with GPS and guides (tracks) you along a route to see at your own pace. You have got to wonder if this is predictive of what is coming - not least when the first stop on the tour was Malta Smart City - this [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ok0GiOaQkxo&ref=rogerprice.me) from a couple of years ago still looks remarkably like it does also today. See also their [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SmartCity_Malta?ref=rogerprice.me) and their [website](http://smartcitymalta.com.mt/?ref=rogerprice.me). To me this smells like [Cyberport in HK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyberport?ref=rogerprice.me) (at best) and more likely WEF... given the link with other smart Cities, including in Dubai. Needless to say, its construction is years behind schedule and Millions (of who's money?) are still being spent on it.

🏝 As an alternative approach (and provocation?), consider Peter's discussion with [Peter Young on Free Cities](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QXFAOBjSOzE&ref=rogerprice.me) - recall also Titus' [Keynote address](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dfL7Yc1IV2A&ref=rogerprice.me) that we talked about last week. This will sound familiar if you have read [The Sovereign Individual](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/82256.The_Sovereign_Individual?from_search=true&from_srp=true&qid=rkHNI6LW7b&rank=1&ref=rogerprice.me) - chapters 10 and 11 - think also of Citadels.

Small as Malta is there are two smaller islands associated with it. The smallest is Commino (on which only two families live) but it has a regular daily tourist influx to visit its [famous Blue Lagoon](https://bluelagoon.com.mt/?ref=rogerprice.me). The other one is Gozo and this was our island of choice for a day out on Quads - we are not too old for this yet! Many [things to do here](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Attractions-g190313-Activities-a_allAttractions.true-Island_of_Gozo.html?ref=rogerprice.me). We ended up down [at the little cafe](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Attraction_Review-g230156-d2495218-Reviews-Dwejra_Bay-San_Lawrenz_Island_of_Gozo.html?ref=rogerprice.me#/media-atf/2495218/422463340:p/?albumid=-160&type=0&category=-160) in [Dwerja Bay](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/Attraction_Review-g230156-d2495218-Reviews-Dwejra_Bay-San_Lawrenz_Island_of_Gozo.html?ref=rogerprice.me) - apparently one of the scenes for shooting in Game of Thrones (recall our [visit to the Studios in Banbridge](https://www.gameofthronesstudiotour.com/?ref=rogerprice.me), Northern Ireland). On the way back we took the [Fast Ferry](https://gozofastferry.com/?ref=rogerprice.me) - this is certainly the recommended way to get to and from the island - now you know!

We also did a trip down to Mdina and Rabat on the public bus. Early start is the way to go. We got there around 9am before the tourists arrived. Brilliant - check out [this walkabout](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tPdsOFCvCqY&ref=rogerprice.me) (not mine) to get the atmosphere. From there we walked the 300m or so to Rabat and of course we visited St Pauls Grotto and Catacombs - another [walkabout here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hTDJqWdcvZk&ref=rogerprice.me).

🧘‍♂️ We are taking advantage of the new location to switch into a new routine. V is swimming daily in the morning and I have restarted yoga; we both feel better for it and aim to continue.

🤪 I have also re-started playing "scrabble" on my iPad - using "[*Words with Friends*](https://www.zynga.com/games/words-with-friends-2/?ref=rogerprice.me)" - so if you are interested to have a daily game going with me - just let me know!

Also on the iPad I do recommend [Sudoku.com](https://sudoku.com/?ref=rogerprice.me) - this is just the best implementation and my favourite is "Killer Sudoku" by Easybrain. How about you? Also a great source of inspiration on things like this is "[Cracking the Cryptic](https://crackingthecryptic.com/?ref=rogerprice.me)" - check their [youtube channel](https://www.youtube.com/c/CrackingTheCryptic?ref=rogerprice.me) and see also their [Web-App](https://app.crackingthecryptic.com/?ref=rogerprice.me) These guys are amazing and former UK champions. Watch their videos and learn (and be amazed) and I recommend also their [CrackingTheCryptic App](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cracking-the-cryptic/id1629992934?ref=rogerprice.me).

📱 Other adjustments include leaving the main smartphone at home (as I mentioned last week). I can still recall being paid a premium to be "on-call" and reachable outside work hours - now that seems to be expected for everything all the time and there is certainly more tracking going on than you would want; this will be exploited if you do not stop, wake up and push back! Feature Phone is too basic for me (at the moment) so I have switched to using my Pixel phone running CalyxOS (not Android!) with just minimal applications that I really need to have. I am still reachable but only to those with my disposable phone number or via [Session](https://getsession.org/?ref=rogerprice.me) (an open source fork of Signal with some significant advantages). Great thing is the removal of all Social Media temptations (not to mention tracking) and you can still have the advantage of downloaded podcasts, books and music. Check out [this dedicated page](https://rogerprice.me/calyxos-starter-configuration/) for a step by step guide.

## **🛠** Update on my Projects

### Website and mobile device safety and security

You may recall the discussion back in [Issue 3](https://rogerprice.me/welcome-to-the-matrix/) on good practice to keep your data safe and secure. This week I have been working through some more tips - focussing on mobile phones - guiding principle: do not let perfect be the enemy of good!

Goals that I have in mind with the configuration:

- have a minimal configuration - just what is really needed. There is a mindset shift here too - among other things you do not need to be online 24/7.
- avoid (as much as possible) using anything that tracks you - you will thank me later!
- should allow efficient, secure and reliable sharing of information peer-to-peer (avoid cloud services like iCloud, Dropbox, Google Drive or similar) - eg photos and documents

Details on the configuration and how to do it are here and I will be updating it from time to time based on experience and feedback. [Check it out](https://rogerprice.me/calyxos-starter-configuration/)
