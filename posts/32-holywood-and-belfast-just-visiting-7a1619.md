🇮🇪 🇬🇧 This week we got back on the road again and we are in transit thru Ireland and UK.   Over the weekend (evening of 1 October) there were big celebrations in Valetta with fireworks everywhere and bands in the squares and diners out in every street.  The rest of the weekend was sunny and warm so we charged our batteries for a week in transit.

Travel on Monday and Tuesday was smoother than we had feared.  Valetta airport was friendly and no issues at all.  Arrival in Dublin also went smoothly - we could see the queueing barriers in the carparks where they are able to make you snake around for miles before getting into the terminal but they were not in use.   Travel up to Belfast also went smoothly - Aircoach is easy as long as you book in advance.

It has been a bit of a whirlwind this week.  We had numerous family visits during the week as we do not plan to be back in Europe this winter.  Tried to meet everyone and inevitably got involved in solving many computer problems!

Saturday morning we are heading in for one last trip into Belfast - stopping off at St Georges Market for [Belfast Bap](https://www.instagram.com/belfastbapco/?ref=rogerprice.me) and to pick up a few things for our travel.  If you get the chance you should go there and support the good folks from the market - pay in cash.
