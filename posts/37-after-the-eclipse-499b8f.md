🇹🇭 This week we had [Loy Kratong Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loy_Krathong?ref=rogerprice.me).  This is celebrated every year here in Thailand on the evening of the Full Moon.  Many of the other Southeast Asian countries celebrate it too. The name could be translated as "to float ritual vessel or lamp," and comes from the tradition of making *krathong* or buoyant, decorated baskets, which are then floated on a river. Many Thais use the krathong to thank the Goddess of Water.  There has certainly been an overabundance of water  everywhere here recently!

This year the festival coincided with the second total lunar eclipse of the year - more on that below.

It has been really good for us to be here in southern Thailand recently.  We have really enjoyed the environment and new contacts here.  Phuket is an Island and you will know that we are keen on island life - recent locations being Madeira, Mallorca and Malta, not to mention Ireland.  Up until this year our 10 years on HK (also an island) was about the best that I can remember; regrettably the madness there [seems to be persisting](https://news.rthk.hk/rthk/en/component/k2/1674670-20221108.htm?fbclid=IwAR1JMF6etBSGrnHNl8XFj67eOkL32L6jMkwxr-2bh_5-XrVd8lhEukIGdp0&ref=rogerprice.me).

The economy here in Phuket is pretty much tourist-based; this has pros and cons.  
On the positive side:

- Thailand is rather welcoming to tourists and even for those looking to stay here longer
- The people who come here tend to be awake to the nonsense elsewhere and actively seeking something different - as such there is an interesting community of open-minded people who bring plenty of experience, ideas, energy and insights from elsewhere
- You have all possible and desirable facilities readily available - including warm weather, nice beaches, good accomodation, shopping, gyms, yoga, good transportation, money exchange as well as good, affordable medical and dental services
- It is an island with a track record of having more reasonable policies than the rest of the country and many other places - this is as foreseen in The Sovereign Individual (Chapter 10 is currently in play) and what we noticed this also on other islands - eg Madeira.
- There is plenty of local produce and local markets and shops abound - it is largely a cash economy that does not really depend on imports.  You ought to be able to understand why this is important.

As to the cons:

- the economy is largely dependent on the tourist market and so subject to any changes up or down
- the nonsense elsewhere is making this quite a desirable location and economics-101 dictates that prices go up with higher demand and fixed supply!
- inevitably not everyone who comes here will have pure motives and to be seen to what extent enclaves get established

We have achieved most of the goals that we set for being here and have enjoyed our time here.  We have had plenty of relaxation, time for us and time to work on our projects and priorities as well as meeting interesting and open/like-minded people.
