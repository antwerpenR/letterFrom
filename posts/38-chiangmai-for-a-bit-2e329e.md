🇹🇭 This week we took an internal flight up to the north of Thailand and we are in Chiangmai for a bit.  Thailand is a big place and this is 1500 miles north of Phuket.   We are here continuing to scout out interesting locations for the future - recall our previous expeditions to [Madeira](https://rogerprice.me/on-location-in-madeira/), [Mallorca](https://rogerprice.me/on-location-palma-mallorca/) and [Malta](https://rogerprice.me/and-so-on-to-malta/) as well as [Bangkok](https://rogerprice.me/bangkok-for-a-bit/) and [Phuket](https://rogerprice.me/phuket-changing-gear/) down south.  If you have relevant experience or suggestions - do comment below.

The weather here is fantastic - warm and sunny mostly with seasons [like in HK but without the humidity](https://www.timeanddate.com/weather/hong-kong/hong-kong/climate?ref=rogerprice.me) and maybe even a bit nicer; the only downside seems to be the bad air quality in Feb thru Apr due to crop burning.

> [!image]
> ![Click for source](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/2022/11/image-32.png)
> Click for source

We had a super week here and were very busy.  [Chiangmai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiang_Mai?ref=rogerprice.me) is the largest city in Northern Thailand, 700 miles north of Bangkok with a population of 130,000 people.  As such it is a fairly big place with lots to see and lots going on.

The city is completely flat and easy to walk around but you can expect to do a lot of walking - distances are large - you can explore [here on Google Maps](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Old+City+Mueang+Chiang+Mai+District,+Chiang+Mai/@18.7884976,98.967833,15z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m6!3m5!1s0x30da3a9967743d43:0x9ca5249215f989a5!8m2!3d18.7897361!4d98.9857795!16s%2Fg%2F11hbr7jg_1?ref=rogerprice.me).

The larger shops are limited to the shopping centers dotted around the city.  You have the original markets like Warorot, then places like Lotus (formerly Tesco Lotus) and the the up-market malls like Central Festival (similar to the one in Phuket).

> [!image]
> ![Central Festival Mall and Warorot Market](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/2022/11/IMG\_1327.jpeg)
> Central Festival Mall and Warorot Market

Public transport is limited to the Red Minibuses and Tuk-Tuks and of course you have Grab cars (like Uber or Bolt).   The fares are all pretty reasonable and advantage of Grab is that you get a pricepoint that you can use in negotiations with others!  Traffic is not great and there seem be be a few hours of bottleneck each day.

On Sunday we had a great day out doing the Self Guided [Chiang Mai Introduction Walking Tour](https://www.gpsmycity.com/gps-tour-guides/chiang-mai-4977.html?ref=rogerprice.me).    This is a most interesting website and app and there is likely a tour for places near you.

[![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/2022/11/image-26.png)](https://www.gpsmycity.com/gps-tour-guides/chiang-mai-4977.html?ref=rogerprice.me)

Noteworthy spots on this tour included:  Cultural Center and 3 Kings monument, Wat Phra Sing (photo above), Wat Chedi Luang, Tha Pae Gate and market as well as the Warorot Market.

![](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/2022/11/IMG\_1283.jpeg)

While visiting Tesco Lotus we found a few interesting streets nearby that are full of street art:

> [!image]
> ![Graffiti walls around Tesco Lotus Khamthiang](https://storage.ghost.io/c/9c/2a/9c2aa86d-156e-419f-9a0f-38bf91aebf15/content/images/2022/11/IMG\_1325.jpeg)
> Graffiti walls around Tesco Lotus Khamthiang

We also checked out all sorts of accommodation options, also  [visa arrangements](https://www.thaiembassy.com/category/thailand-visa?ref=rogerprice.me) and the local health services as well as gyms and yoga.

Other activities included trips [outside of Chiangmai too](https://www.voyagetips.com/en/things-to-do-in-chiang-mai/?ref=rogerprice.me) - including to Wat Pha Lat, Doi Suthep, Bhubing Palace and Mon Tha Than Waterfall.   There are quite a few interesting meetup groups here too including [Bitcoin Weekly Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/bitcoinsinchiangmai/events/khrwwsydcpbwb/?ref=rogerprice.me) (a most interesting group of people) and [Hiking Chiangmai](https://chiangmaihiking.wordpress.com/2022/11/10/2022-11-13_doi-nang-mo-nw-route/?ref=rogerprice.me) (suitable for walk and talk).  We also checked out quite a few of the [co-working spaces](https://www.madmonkeyhostels.com/h2o_blog/best-coworking-spaces-in-chiang-mai/?ref=rogerprice.me); they are pleasant, affordable and packed with people - some are so full they have stopped taking new members!  There is quite a dynamic community of foreigners here and good facilities for collaborating and doing business.
