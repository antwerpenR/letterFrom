Did you miss the wakeup call?

🇭🇰 We are still in HK and will be here for a few more weeks. I did manage to get back into daily routine and hiked to the Peak most days during the week despite the weather. It all feels rather strange back in HK - more on this below.

We were busy with plenty of things to do - especially catching up on all the admin things and ensuring that all bank and credit cards are renewed to the max - in addition to passports, international driving licenses etc. We are actively planning things for 2023 and lots to do on that. How about your plans? Tips [here](https://rogerprice.me/choices-you-de/).

I had hoped to go to the Bitcoin Meetup on IFC roof but it seems to have moved to [next Thursday](https://www.meetup.com/bitcoin-hk/events/290047193/?ref=rogerprice.me) - perhaps because of the cold weather.

During the week there was briefly some buzz about restrictions around Covid dropping but this is too little and much too late and achieves nothing useful; in fact it is rather the opposite. The "LeaveHomeSafe" app has apparently been "scrapped" - so indeed scanning is no longer needed (not that people were doing it anyway) but they are *keeping vaccination requirement for many things* and you can be sure that *this will come back more later in some worse form* of smartphone-based Digital ID that is linked with CBDC and your money with built-in tracking - more on that below from Sorelle and Guy. Also "the mask requirement continues". Recall [how useless masks are](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=il-5HVmmMXE&ref=rogerprice.me) and *recognise the social and psychological damage that they cause* - zombifying and dividing people.

Normally the weather in HK is dry during the winter and it can be surprisingly cool (usually around 26 January is coldest day of the year) but this week in HK has been fairly miserable with temperatures dipping down as low at 7 degrees! Quite a change from Thailand and Malaysia. Nobody has heating in HK - the best you can do is electric blanket and wooly socks.

It may surprise you that we have seasons in HK - we are certainly in winter now!

## Regarding CBDCs (Central Bank Digital Currencies)

While these may sound boring you absolutely do not want these despite what your government will say - as Sorelle explains. Be in no doubt - governments will try to get these introduced and accepted by all possible nefarious means. In HK they were slipped in via the Octopus card and "Consumption Vouchers" that everyone was happy to receive - these seem to be free money but you can only spend them on things the government approves and there are additional constraints that can be applied including expiry dates and holding limits - *they are not equivalent to cash - they are inferior and dangerous*. You have been warned. Ask for and use cash wherever possible.
