Apple in Bangkok at CentralwOrld

🇹🇭 Wow - I can hardly believe it but we have been here in Bangkok now for a month and what a fabulous month it has been.  I really appreciate living in the center of a world city with so much going on, so many interesting people to meet and it's warm and sunny!

This week I had a bit more time to read and think about things so I have quite a smorgasbord of topics to cover this week - each with a bit of depth.   Enjoy and do  follow the links to interviews you can watch and listen to as well as the related articles and books to read.

I am enjoying the Bitcoin meetups here in Bangkok - I have been pleased to help some more people start using Lightning to receive and spend with bitcoin and we are getting some peer-to-peer trading experience.  If you are interested do check out [Issue 14](https://rogerprice.me/getting-started-phoenix-is-easy/) and [Wallet of Satoshi](https://www.walletofsatoshi.com/?ref=rogerprice.me) is a simple and recommended lightning wallet - very easy to get started - an interesting alternative to [Phoenix](https://phoenix.acinq.co/?ref=rogerprice.me).

We are enjoying our daily and weekly routine  here in Bangkok - it's quite different from Hong Kong and also from what you can do in Europe.  One thing that we do look forward to is our weekly visit to the food and vegetables market ([Khlong Toei](https://search.brave.com/images?q=khlong%20toei%20market&source=web)) - it is on a scale that you likely cannot imagine.  You will find all sorts of interesting things, the people are friendly, the prices are good and the food is fresh daily.

## Project Update

This week I have been looking deeper into Nostr and getting more familiar with it.  Recall that Nostr stands for "**Notes and Other Stuff Transmitted by Relays**".  A couple of good introductory one page explainers [here](https://nostr-resources.com/?ref=rogerprice.me) and [here](https://uselessshit.co/resources/nostr/?ref=rogerprice.me).

I am so impressed by how quickly this fully open source project ([github](https://github.com/nostr-protocol/nostr?ref=rogerprice.me)) has taken off; it really is like going back to the early days of the internet and now that we have Bitcoin and Lightning it feels like this may well go big.  There is a really dynamic community of developers and projects pop up everywhere - it is sooooo early and exciting.

One example of an app that has sprung up is [Nostrgram](https://nostrgram.co/?ref=rogerprice.me#popular:allPopular) - check it out!   I think that [this discussion](https://nostrgram.co/?ref=rogerprice.me#thread:22d7599d4ac5c3d525bf97ec93366dfb61677bd7d5e97c037bae486a8c8766ea:22d7599d4ac5c3d525bf97ec93366dfb61677bd7d5e97c037bae486a8c8766ea) sums up [@jb55](https://twitter.com/jb55?ref=rogerprice.me)'s recent struggle with the Evil Empire; at its heart is the basic issue of self-sovereignty and decentralisation may ***The Force*** be with him.

You can follow me [here](https://nostrgram.co/?ref=rogerprice.me#profile:allEvents:662f9bff98cdc84cc395589aee538a826933b804be76ae6b682d59e18960f6b2) on Nostrgram.  You will notice the lightning symbol and this is already revolutionising how people can monetise and pay for services - more on this certainly in subsequent weeks.

## 🔗 Links and things

"Today no money in the world fully performs all three services. National currencies are being used as means-of-payment and standard-of-value money, but none in this inflationary age is an assured store-of-value money. In fact, a foremost concern to voters and politicians everywhere is that so many currencies are so rapidly losing their value in terms of commodities and services. Commodities like gold and silver, which are being used as store-of-value money, are not being used as either means-of-payment or standard-of-value money. Thus the world we have so long known, in which most currencies were redeemable at a fixed price in a store-of-value money like gold, is in disarray." - John Exter, at AIER (in 1972!)
