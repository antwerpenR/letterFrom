🇪🇸 Mayday came and went this week relatively uneventfully here in Palma.  It was nice weather and many places were closed so the few that were open were pretty busy!  The boat show has been ongoing here but I did not seem to get any invitations and we didn't bring our yacht so we have not ventured into that.

Continuing our exploration of Palma, V and I checked out the great sports and leisure facilities here in town.  Noteworthy among them include the [Tennis Club](https://www.palmatennis.com/?ref=rogerprice.me) (next door), [Queens Center](https://www.queenscentre.es/en/los-valores-del-centro/?ref=rogerprice.me) and [Deva Yoga Shala](http://devayogashala.com/en/?ref=rogerprice.me).  An advantage of being a global traveller is that you get to experience, compare and contrast all of these and meet all the interesting people therein!

The lunar eclipse and coronation are ahead this coming weekend - decide yourself what that implies - I see plenty of ritualistic opportunity that will likely become clear(er) with a little hindsight.  For example, you might recall that on the lunar eclipse in May last year Terra Luna collapsed and on the lunar eclipse last November FTX collapsed.  There does seem to be rather a lot of collapsing going on these days so nothing would surprise me!  Recall our [previous discussion on this](https://rogerprice.me/after-the-eclipse/#%F0%9F%A4%AA-how-curious-is-thisrituals-or-what) topic.  Jordan's expectation is [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PYrLWvMyc1I&ref=rogerprice.me).

### Artificial Intelligence (AI) - you really ought to be aware

My focus this week is to help you understand the developments that are happening in the AI space.  You may well have heard of ChatGPT but thought it irrelevant or something that you would never encounter.  Think again - it is but one of a whole slew of AI related products that are already transforming many so-called "white collar" areas and the speed of change is increasing.

- *Programming* - ChatGPT is happy to give you steps to take to learn programming and it will suggest an outline learning journey with pointers to readily available source material.  But it can do much more - just ask it to write any program for you in any language and it will do it.  When you copy the code to your computer and try to run it you will likely encounter errors; just tell ChatGPT those errors and it will tell you how to solve them and get it working.  Be amazed.  See [one example here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_g4BiBcYdZQ&ref=rogerprice.me) and listen to [TechLead give his opinion](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ab6xJ4E23VQ&ref=rogerprice.me).   Mind-blowing!
- *Art and Photography* used to be space reserved for people with special talents and time.  You will be amazed by the current state of *StableDiffusion* and *MidJourney* - just two examples of AI in this area.  Watch Matt explain how to use ChatGPT to write prompts for [MidJourney here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B66GJKxQxfc&ref=rogerprice.me).   For those who want to run their own instance of *StableDiffusion* Laura shows you how to [install this on Google CoLab](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uFNz4y8Ebz4&ref=rogerprice.me) for free as well!
- *Music* is also an industry that is being and will be further challenged by AI developments.  [Listen here to IAMLXGEND](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uWmT69-qjBQ&ref=rogerprice.me) explain how he uses multiple AI tools to make his music.   It is refreshing to hear how some will see this as an opportunity - he does not want to be a dinosaur!  A fascinating video!
- *Language learning* - being here in Spain I was wondering if this might be possible and how to go about it.  Well, listen to Jerry explain to you how to [install voice input and output to ChatGT](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_J6e0JHl3C4&ref=rogerprice.me) with a couple of clicks and then learn your chosen language.   Like him, I think the second option is the better one - the additional controls help.  He has a follow-up video explaining how to do this too [on your phone](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bm5SKmOdEgw&ref=rogerprice.me).
- *Learn anything* - Bri explains to you how she uses AI to help her [learn anything](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MnDudvCyWpc&ref=rogerprice.me)!
- For *creators* in general listen to [this selection of 10 tools](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pNbjkmcnW7k&ref=rogerprice.me) that might well give you a productivity and idea boost - be amazed.
- Lastly, consider NLW's recent [discussion with Annie](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B66GJKxQxfc&ref=rogerprice.me); impressive as this is it obviously has some "trainer wheels" and constraints in place.  At the current pace of development expect those to be removed in short order.

Do you begin to see how much this challenges everything you may have believed to be true?  Not only does this technology give you the ability to learn faster and better than all of the traditional education methods but it also is getting to the point where it obviates the need for you to learn a skill; it will just do it for you!

There are many traditional "knowledge work" professions that this technology will transform faster than you can ever imagine.  Listen to [this extract from recent All-In-Podcast](https://dts.podtrac.com/redirect.mp3/traffic.libsyn.com/secure/allinchamathjason/ALLIN-E126.mp3?dest-id=1928300&ref=rogerprice.me#t=1391) where the hosts (all Silicon Valley VCs) talk about some of their ideas and what they envisage as the implications.

You might want to pause and reflect on the above - what are you going to do with this knowledge and these tools?  Will you just ignore it all or will you leverage some to your advantage?
