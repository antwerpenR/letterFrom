#### The Great Filter

This is an interesting one. If there are billions of stars in the Milky Way galaxy, many of which are similar to our Sun, some of them would be likely to have Earth-like planets in their habitable zones. With the vast number of potentially habitable worlds, it seems reasonable to assume that at least some of them could have developed intelligent life capable of technological advancement.

However, despite extensive efforts to search for extraterrestrial intelligence we have not yet detected any conclusive evidence of intelligent life beyond Earth. This raises the question: If intelligent alien civilisations are so common, why haven't we seen any signs of their existence?
