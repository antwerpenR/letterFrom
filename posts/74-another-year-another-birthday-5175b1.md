#### Laptop update

This week I have been using the T470 as my main computer, just to check that I have all the programs that I use every day and that the synchronisation and backup etc between my devices works OK.  So far, so good - this is the advantage of using a minimal collection of Open Source software and a few browser-accessed cloud services.  This article was written using both Lenovo and Mac M1!

Nice as the Lenovo is I do have to say that Apple does make the nicer computers.  Yes, the M1 Macbook Air is a delight to use and you also have many of the linux features available on it but I just do not trust Apple for the medium to longer term to keep their App Store open, not to mention surveillance and other blocking technology that they may weaponise.  You have also got to consider the tools that they have for suppression - for example their proprietary [xProtect](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/security/sec469d47bd8/web?ref=rogerprice.me) feature and when push comes to shove you might just find your preferred programs black-listed and unable to run.

And the Lenovo costs about 1/8 the cost of a medium spec M1 Mac - cheap insurance!

One thing that I have enjoyed this week is to be able to run VirtualBox again; this is no longer possible on the Apple M1 machines due to the change in processor architecture.  VirtualBox gives you easy access to all the other operating systems and variants - BRILLIANT!  It also enables you to run things that you want to be sand-boxed from the rest of your system; something to keep in mind.

I have also been getting my development environment up and running on the laptop and in sync with my Mac.  The wonders of *Syncthing* allow open-source, peer-to-peer synchronisation between all your devices.  Works a treat, like magic.
